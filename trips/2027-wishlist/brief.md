## Rules applied to every plan
- Own room every night — private rooms in hostels, guesthouses, B&Bs, agriturismi, shared houses, a Japanese temple lodging. Airbnb only where nothing else exists. No insects, no bad plumbing, hot water, wifi.
- Maximize culture, history, cuisine and nature; day hikes 4–7 h, moderate–strenuous fine; no multi-day treks.
- **Greece and Central-Southern Italy are built to maximize ancient sites, monuments and battlefields** — that is the stated purpose of those two, not a side benefit.
- Safest transport; trains preferred; automatic rental car only where the route needs one (Greece, both Italys, Iceland, 4–5 days in Provence and Northern France); IDP.
- No budget ceiling: lean and best columns with the levers between them.

## The two ancient-history trips

**Greece, 1–16 Oct, 15 nights, car for the Peloponnese.** Athens (Acropolis and museum with a licensed guide, Agora, Kerameikos, Pnyx, National Archaeological Museum) → Attica day trips: **Marathon** tumulus and museum, the **Salamis** strait, Sounion, Eleusis → Delphi, with **Chaeronea**'s lion and **Thebes/Leuctra** en route → **Thermopylae** (Leonidas monument, Kolonos hill) → Acrocorinth and Isthmia → Mycenae, Tiryns, Argos, Epidaurus from Nafplio → Sparta, the Menelaion, Mystras → **Messene**, Pylos and the Sphacteria bay → Bassae → Olympia, Nemea → Crete: Knossos with a guide, Phaistos, the Heraklion museum, the Samaria gorge. Plataea and Mantinea/Tegea are on the driving line. Early October keeps summer opening hours (they switch ~1 Nov) and Samaria open to 31 Oct; the plan avoids the free-entry weekend of 25–26 Sep, which is the worst Acropolis crush of autumn.

**Central-Southern Italy, 16 Sep – 9 Oct, 21 nights.** Rome 5 nights (Forum/Palatine/Colosseum full-experience with a guide, Capitoline, Pantheon, Baths of Caracalla, the Appian Way on a car-free Sunday, Ostia Antica, Hadrian's Villa at Tivoli, Palazzo Massimo) → Naples 3 (the MANN, **Pompeii** and **Herculaneum** with guides, Vesuvius crater, the **Phlegraean Fields** — Cumae's Sibyl, Baiae, Pozzuoli — **Capua**'s amphitheatre and Spartacus, **Paestum**'s Greek temples) → Amalfi 2 (Path of the Gods) → **Cannae** battlefield on a Thursday with a private guide in the best column → Matera 2 → Lecce 2 and the Via Appia's end at Brindisi, Taranto's Magna Graecia museum → Sicily: **Syracuse** 2 (Neapolis, Ortigia, the Athenian expedition ground at Epipolae/Euryalus and the Great Harbour), **Agrigento**, **Selinunte** and **Segesta**, Villa del Casale, Palermo 2, Etna. Timed so Pompeii still closes at 19:00 (it drops to 17:00 in mid-October) and Etna's upper slopes are snow-free.

Two honest gaps: **Lake Trasimene** (Hannibal, 217 BC) sits in Umbria and belongs to the Tuscany/Umbria loop, not this route; and **Actium, Pharsalus and Philippi** are in Greece, **Zama** in Tunisia — none is reachable from either itinerary. Say the word and I can graft Trasimene onto the Tuscany loop and the three Greek battle sites onto a longer Greece plan.

## Findings worth knowing before you choose
1. **Cost floor.** Nothing lands under C$4,600 lean, and lodging is 25–40% of every total. The cheap trips are cheap because of ground costs, not flights: Japan at C$344/night and Vietnam/Thailand at C$354 both carry C$1,500–2,100 of airfare.
2. **Two plans found a schedule problem and fixed it.** Sweden is timed to the moon rather than the month (full moon 15 Sep would wash out the aurora, so Abisko sits on 6–8 Sep), and its Aurora Sky Station evening programme does not run in September at all — aurora watching is free from the lakeshore. Germany's route is reversed so Ravenna Gorge's Thursday–Sunday market and weekday Köln both work.
3. **Safety routing costs money in two places**: Oaxaca is flown from Mexico City because ADO buses on that highway were attacked in late August and early September 2026, and Ha Giang is done by car with a driver rather than the motorbike loop.
4. **Lodging screen rejected**, with review evidence: Casa de Sierra Azul and Casa Angel (Oaxaca), Black Forest Hostel, Hotel Feldmann, Alex 30 (Germany), Pensione Bencistà, Fattoria di Maiano, Castello di Volpaia, Hotel Langhe (Italy), Villa Saint Exupéry, Le Limas, Cassitel (Provence), Auberge de Meo Vac, Nguyen Shack, Stamps (Vietnam/Thailand), Hlemmur Square (Iceland), Generator Stockholm (fees, group noise), Milhouse (Buenos Aires party hostel). Hotel Hornsgatan (Stockholm) has no usable review record and is flagged, not recommended.
5. **PTO.** 2027 gives 15 days; Iberia uses 5, leaving 10. Every trip except Italy-ancient-south (15 days) fits the remaining 10. Two trips in one year means unpaid leave or shortened versions — and the 21-night Italy needs its own year, or to be cut to ~14 nights by dropping Sicily or Puglia.
6. Each research run hit its web-search limit near the end, so a handful of prices per plan carry "(est.)/verify" — listed inside each file, to be confirmed on a live pass once you narrow the list.
