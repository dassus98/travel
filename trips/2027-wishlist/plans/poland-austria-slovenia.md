## 1. When + why

- **Mid-September beats late May.** (a) **Julian Alps huts**: Koča pri Triglavskih jezerih (Seven Lakes) runs **6 Jun – 30 Sep** in 2026 ([sedmera.si](https://sedmera.si/en/), acc. 2026-09-12); in late May the valley still holds snow and the hut is shut, so the best Triglav day hike is off the table. (b) **Tatra**: Kasprowy Wierch cable car runs May–Oct 08:00–17:00, closes for maintenance in November ([zakopanehalal.com](https://www.zakopanehalal.com/en/blog/kasprowy-wierch-cable-car-complete-guide.php)); Morskie Oko's quietest window is "mid-September to mid-October, fewer visitors, golden larch" vs. "late May: waterfalls full" ([morskieokolake.com](https://www.morskieokolake.com/en)). (c) **Holidays**: late May 2027 collides with Pentecost Monday (17 May, Austria) and **Corpus Christi Thu 27 May** (public holiday in Poland and Austria → 4-day weekend, Zakopane and Kraków packed). Mid-Sept has no holiday in any of the three countries; schools are back from 1 Sep (PL/SI) and ~6 Sep (Vienna). (d) **Food calendar**: Wachau grape harvest and *Sturm* (new wine) at the Heurigen start mid-Sept; Ljubljana's Odprta kuhna runs to 9 Oct ([slovenia.info](https://www.slovenia.info/en/things-to-do/events/25144-odprta-kuhna-2026)). (e) **Weather**: Kraków Sept 19.5 °C/9 °C, 65 mm/9 rainy days vs May 19.8/8.5, 75 mm/10 days ([climatestotravel](https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/poland/krakow)) — a wash on temperature, drier in Sept. (f) **Fares**: Kayak shows May as the "most popular" YYZ–WAW month; Sept LOT fares from C$663 ([kayak.ca](https://www.ca.kayak.com/flight-routes/Toronto-Pearson-Intl-YYZ/Warsaw-WA1)).
- **Length: 13 nights.** Kraków needs 4 (three day trips + the city), Vienna 3 (museums, Schönbrunn/Heuriger, Wachau), Ljubljana 2 + 1, Bled/Bohinj 3 (two hikes + Vintgar/castle). Twelve loses a hike; 15 adds Piran/Soča for C$400+ and a 4-h transfer each way — not worth it on this itinerary.
- **PTO: exactly 10 days** (Mon–Fri 13–17 and 20–24 Sep; depart Fri evening, land Fri evening). That is all the PTO left after Iberia's 5 — this trip and Iberia together use the full 15.
- **±1 week**: **+1 (17 Sep–1 Oct)**: Seven Lakes hut closes 30 Sep, Vintgar hours shrink to 09–15, first snow possible at 2,000 m; Open Kitchen still on. **−1 (3–17 Sep)**: warmer lake swims, but Kraków and Bled still in late-summer crowd mode and fares higher. Chosen window is the sweet spot.

## 5. Day-by-day

- **Sat 11 — Arrive Kraków (~17:00).** Old Town loop: Rynek, Cloth Hall, St Mary's hejnał on the hour. Dinner: **Pod Baranem** (ul. Św. Gertrudy; żurek in bread, roast duck; ~PLN 90 ≈ C$33; sour rye soup has sour cream — ask *bez śmietany*). Fallback: rain → Rynek Underground museum.
- **Sun 12 — Auschwitz-Birkenau (anchor).** 07:00 Lajkonik bus; **3.5-h guided tour with a museum educator, ~PLN 130 ≈ C$48**, booked only at visit.auschwitz.org, reservations open ~90 days ahead and English weekend slots go first — book 2–3 weeks minimum, target the day it opens (~13 Jun 2027); free unguided entry is only afternoon slots and still needs a timed pass; bags ≤ 30×20×10 cm, arrive 30 min early ([auschwitz.org](https://www.auschwitz.org/en/visiting/guided-tours-for-individual-visitors/), [visitauschwitz.info](https://www.visitauschwitz.info/en/faq/), acc. 2026-09-12). Back by 15:00; quiet evening in **Kazimierz**: **Hamsa** (Israeli mezze, largely dairy-free, ~PLN 70) or **Starka** (pierogi, vodka flights, ~PLN 90; ruskie pierogi = cottage cheese, choose meat/cabbage-mushroom). Skip organised "Auschwitz + Wieliczka in one day" combos — nine hours of bus.
- **Mon 13 — Tatra day (hike 1).** ~07:00 IC to Zakopane (2–2.5 h), minibus to Palenica Białczańska. **Morskie Oko + Czarny Staw pod Rysami**: 8–9 km each way on the asphalt road (464 m gain, 931→1,395 m), plus 1.5–2 h up to Czarny Staw (1,583 m) — **~20 km, ~650 m, 5–6 h**, easy-moderate, no guide needed; TPN entry ~PLN 9 online ([morskieokolake.com](https://www.morskieokolake.com/en)). Alternative (harder, more alpine): Kuźnice cable car up **Kasprowy Wierch** (PLN 140 return / PLN 90 one-way; be there by 08:00 or queue 2 h) then the ridge west via Kondracka Przełęcz to **Giewont** (1,895 m, chained summit) and down to Kuźnice — ~3 h to Giewont from the top, ~11 km, 5–6 h total, strenuous, exposed in wet; a licensed Tatra guide (TatraAdventure) ~€150–200 (est.) is the "best" option. Museums are closed Monday anyway. Dinner in Zakopane before the ~18:00 train: **Karczma Zapiecek** (kwaśnica, grilled oscypek — sheep cheese, skip if dairy is bad that day). Fallback for rain: Zakopane's Tatra Museum + Krupówki.
- **Tue 14 — Wawel + Kazimierz + Schindler.** 09:00 Wawel: State Rooms + Cathedral (~PLN 40 + 25, est.; timed tickets, book online); 12:00 Kazimierz free walking tour (Walkative, tip PLN 30–50); 15:00 **Schindler's Factory** (Oskar Schindler's Enamel Factory, ~PLN 40, timed, sells out — book 2 weeks ahead, est.) + Podgórze ghetto square. Best column: 3-h licensed private guide for Jewish Kraków, ~€150 (est.). **Cooking class**: pierogi class in Kazimierz ~PLN 250–300 ≈ C$95–110, 3 h (est., e.g. Cooking Classes Kraków) — Tue evening. Lean food: **Hala Forum**/Plac Nowy zapiekanki (PLN 20, order without cheese).
- **Wed 15 — Wieliczka then EC to Vienna.** 08:00 train to Wieliczka Rynek-Kopalnia; **Tourist Route in English from PLN 131 ≈ C$49**, 2–3 h, 800 steps, 17 °C; book the first English slot online ([wieliczka-saltmine.com](https://www.wieliczka-saltmine.com/individual-tourist/tourist-route)). Back 12:30, lunch **Zazie Bistro** (French-Polish, ~PLN 60), **EC Porta Moravica 14:00 → Wien Hbf 19:49**. Fallback if the mine's morning slots are gone: do it Sun afternoon after Auschwitz.
- **Thu 16 — Imperial Vienna.** **Kunsthistorisches Museum** 10:00 (Bruegel, Vermeer; ~€24, est.; open Thu to 21:00), Hofburg Sisi Museum + Imperial Apartments (~€19.50, est.), afternoon **Café Central** or **Café Sperl** (Melange, Apfelstrudel — cream on the side). Evening **Heuriger in Grinzing**: tram 38 to **Weingut Reinprecht** or, quieter, **Zawodsky** (Sturm + buffet; ~€25–35, est.). Best: 2-h licensed private guide of the Hofburg/Ringstrasse ~€200 (est.).
- **Fri 17 — Wachau.** Train Wien Hbf → Melk (~1 h). **Melk Abbey** €16 (+€4 English guided tour 10:55 or 14:55), 09:00–17:30 ([stiftmelk.at](https://www.stiftmelk.at/en/visit-experience/opening-hours-prices-guided-tours/)). DDSG/Brandner boat Melk → Dürnstein (~€30, est., harvest scenery); **walk Dürnstein → Krems (hike 2: ~9 km, flat, 2 h on the Welterbesteig through the vineyards; castle-ruin climb +200 m)**; train Krems → Wien FJB (~1 h). Dinner: **Gasthaus Pöschl** (Tafelspitz, Wiener Schnitzel; ~€35) or splurge **Plachutta Wollzeile** (Tafelspitz ritual, ~€50; sauces are apple-horseradish and chive-cream — skip the chive). Fallback (rain): Schönbrunn instead — **Palace Ticket €42, fixed time slot, buy online only** ([schoenbrunn.at](https://www.schoenbrunn.at/en/tickets-and-prices), acc. 2026-09-12) — and Wachau by rail/boat is still fine in drizzle.
- **Sat 18 — EC Emona 07:53 → Ljubljana 13:53.** Breakfast in the SŽ restaurant car (cash). Afternoon: **Central Market** tail end (Sat is the big market; Sun closed), Triple Bridge, Ljubljana Free Tour 15:00 (tip €10). Dinner **Gostilna Šestica** (Slovenian classics, štruklji are cheese-filled — order the sausage/kranjska klobasa, ~€25). Fallback: none needed.
- **Sun 19 — Ljubljana.** Castle via funicular (~€18 incl. exhibitions, est.), **Plečnik House** (closed Mon — this is why it's Sunday, ~€8), Metelkova by daylight, Tivoli park. Lunch **Druga Violina** (social enterprise, ~€12). Modern splurge for the best column: **JB** or **Monstera Bistro** (~€60–90 tasting, book 2 weeks). Fallback: National Gallery.
- **Mon 20 — To Bled.** Bus ~09:00, check in, **Bled Castle** (~€20, est.), lake loop 6 km, kremšnita at Park café is cream-heavy — one bite counts. Dinner **Oštarija Peglez'n** (trout, ~€25). Best: rowed *pletna* to the island (€15 est.).
- **Tue 21 — Triglav Lakes Valley (hike 3, the anchor hike).** Bus to Bohinj (Stara Fužina), then the Planina Blato shuttle (runs in high season; if it has stopped by Sept, taxi ~€25 est. — private cars pay a €20 toll). **Planina Blato → Koča pri Triglavskih jezerih (Double Lake): 15.2 km out-and-back, 1,051 m gain, ~6 h, easy-moderate marked trail**, hut serves goulash/barley stew (cash) ([moonhoneytravel](https://www.moonhoneytravel.com/triglav-lakes-valley-hike-slovenia/), acc. 2026-09-12). **Optional single hut night (flagged optional)**: sleep at the hut (135 beds; rooms and dorms), full card prepayment, free cancel to 2 days before, **no sleeping bags — cotton liner only**, PZS card for the member rate ([sedmera.si](https://sedmera.si/en/)); next morning push on to the upper lakes and back — the only hut-to-hut worth doing on this trip. Guide not needed in September fair weather; a licensed IFMGA guide for the Seven Lakes ~€200–250 (est.) is the best-column option. Fallback (rain/thunder): Vintgar + Bled (swap with Wed).
- **Wed 22 — Vintgar + Bohinj/Savica + Vogel.** **Vintgar Gorge** first slot: €15 adult, timed tickets with a 245-person cap, buy online, one-way boardwalk 1.6 km out via Šum waterfall and forest path back (~1.5 h), free e-shuttle from Bled bus station; Sept hours ~09–15/16 ([bledadventures](https://bledadventures.com/en/vintgar-gorge-tickets-parking-opening-hours-tips-for-2026/), [earthtrekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/ultimate-guide-visit-vintgar-gorge-lake-bled-slovenia/)). Bus to Bohinj: **Savica waterfall** (500 steps, ~€4 est.) then **Vogel cable car** (~€30 return est.) for the Bohinj panorama; hike 4 (short): Vogel → Šija ridge ~2 h. Dinner Bled: **Gostilna Pri Planincu** (goulash, ~€20). Fallback: **Velika Planina** instead (bus/ taxi to Kamnik is awkward from Bled — do it from Ljubljana Thu if weather flips).
- **Thu 23 — Bled → Ljubljana.** Morning bus; afternoon Velika Planina alternative (Kamniška Bistrica cable car + chairlift ~€25 est., shepherds' huts, 2–3 h walking) or a Ljubljana food walk (~€70–90, est.). Farewell dinner **Gostilna As** or the cheaper **Klobasarna** (kranjska klobasa €8).
- **Fri 24 — Odprta kuhna 10:00** (Pogačarjev trg, Fridays 10–22 to 9 Oct; weather-cancelled if raining — [slovenia.info](https://www.slovenia.info/en/things-to-do/events/25144-odprta-kuhna-2026)): early street-food lunch, 11:00 bus to LJU, **LO616 13:30 → LO45 17:00 → YYZ ~20:10**.

Events in window: Sturm/harvest season Wachau and Grinzing (mid-Sept on, no ticket); Odprta kuhna every Friday; Kraków's Pierogi Festival (mid-Aug) and Vienna's Lange Nacht der Museen (first Sat Oct) are just outside — no ticketed festival drives dates.

## 9. Risks

1. **Entry: EES + ETIAS.** EES biometric registration is live at Schengen borders (first entry means fingerprints/photo at WAW — allow +30 min for the KRK connection; the 14:50 leg is the safe one). ETIAS was slated to start in late 2026 and, with a transitional grace period, to become mandatory in 2027 — the official site would not load in this pass; **check travel-europe.europa.eu/etias in Jan 2027** and apply the moment it's live (€20, est., 3-year validity). Passport valid ≥3 months past 24 Sep 2027.
2. **Julian Alps weather/early snow** (late Sept). Mitigation: hike days Tue/Wed are swappable with Vintgar/Vogel; check meteo.si and the hut's status; never start the Seven Lakes in a thunderstorm forecast.
3. **Auschwitz and Wieliczka slots gone.** Mitigation: book at the 90-day mark; if the 12 Sep educator tour is full, Tue 14 or Wed 15 morning works with the mine moved to Sun afternoon; last resort is a licensed Kraków agency tour (bus + educator, ~PLN 200, est.).
4. **Train sell-out / strikes.** Emona is one train a day with a restaurant car, Porta Moravica has few direct alternatives. Mitigation: book at 6 months; fallbacks are the railjet via Graz (Vienna–Ljubljana) and Regiojet/Leo Express via Katowice–Ostrava (Kraków–Vienna). No night buses needed anywhere.
5. **Crowds/cleanliness pinch points**: Morskie Oko road (arrive before 08:00 or go on the Kasprowy option), Bled lakeshore at weekends (you are there Mon–Wed by design), Metelkova at night (Celica cell away from the courtyard, or Cubo).
