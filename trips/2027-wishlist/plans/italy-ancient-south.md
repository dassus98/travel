# Italy, ancient-Rome maximal — Rome → Campania → Basilicata/Puglia → Sicily (21 nights)

Researched 2026-09-12 (web only; all URLs accessed that day). FX: **€1 = C$1.58 (est.)**, US$1 = C$1.37 (est.). "(est.)" = my estimate, not a quoted price. Chitra: solo, automatic + IDP, mildly lactose-intolerant (LI notes inline). Italian state sites (cultura.gov.it) timed out behind the proxy, so several hours/prices come from secondary guides — verify on the live pass.

## 1. WHEN + WHY

**Depart YYZ Thu 16 Sep 2027 (AC890 20:00) → land FCO Fri 17 Sep → fly home Sat 9 Oct 2027 (PMO→FCO→YYZ). 21 nights.**

- **Late Sept–early Oct beats May** for this route: Etna's upper slopes are snow-free and "late September–October" is the guide-recommended window ([apartmentincatania.com](https://www.apartmentincatania.com/en/hiking-mount-etna/), [go-etna.com](https://www.go-etna.com/how-to-visit-etna/)); sea still ~23 °C for Amalfi/Vendicari; Sicily interior 24–28 °C instead of May's green-but-wet. May's only edge is wildflowers at Segesta/Selinunte and almond-free Agrigento — not worth losing Etna reliability.
- **Ends before the 15 Oct hour cut**: Pompeii closes 19:00 to mid-Oct, then 17:00 ([thebettervacation.com](https://thebettervacation.com/rome/pompeii-tickets/), [pompeii-italy.org](https://pompeii-italy.org/reservations/)); Segesta drops to 17:00 on 27 Oct ([coopculture.it](https://www.coopculture.it/en/products/segesta-archaeological-park-ticket-with-internal-shuttle-service/)). Agrigento's late-night openings end 15 Sep ([parcovalledeitempli.it](https://www.parcovalledeitempli.it/en/)) — dusk is still 18:45 on 5 Oct, which is what you want.
- Weekday logic built in: Cannae falls on a **Thursday** (the two sources disagree — "Thu–Sun" per [turistinpuglia.it](https://www.turistinpuglia.it/en/booking/guided-tour-of-the-reeds-of-the-battle/) vs "closed Sun–Tue" per [italyastheromansdid.it](https://www.italyastheromansdid.it/2025/12/13/roman-canne-in-apulia-cannae/); Thursday satisfies both). Appian Way on a car-free Sunday; Vatican on Monday; Ostia/Palazzo Massimo/Paolo Orsi avoid Mondays; MANN avoids Tuesday; the first-Sunday-free crush (3 Oct) lands on the Etna day, not a museum.
- **Why 21 nights:** 5 Rome + 3 Naples + 2 Amalfi + 2 Matera + 2 Lecce + 1 Catania + 2 Syracuse + 1 Agrigento + 1 Erice + 2 Palermo. Every night carries a distinct ancient anchor; the only "cuts" versus a 24-night ideal are Beneventum (optional add, day 8), Pantalica and a second Syracuse day. Shorter than 21 means dropping Puglia or western Sicily.
- **±1 week:** −1 wk (9 Sep start): hotter (30 °C+ in Sicily), Cannae still Thursday, Agrigento evening opening still on (ends 15 Sep) — marginally more crowded. +1 wk (23 Sep start): Pompeii and Selinunte hours shorten after 14/15 Oct on your last Sicily days, Etna summit weather deteriorates, fares ~C$50–100 lower (est.). The chosen week is the sweet spot.

## 5. DAY-BY-DAY

Guides: Rome/Naples/Sicily licensed private guides run €60–80/h (est.); group tours €45–90. LI: aged pecorino/parmigiano fine; fresh mozzarella, ricotta and cannoli/cassata are the traps — carry lactase (sold as Lacdigest in farmacie).

**Fri 17 Sep — Rome.** Land 11:10, Leonardo Express, check in. Pantheon (€5) and Ara Pacis (€13 (est.), open daily) at 16:00. Dinner **Armando al Pantheon** (cacio e pepe is dairy — take the amatriciana, €35; book 2–3 wks). Fallback: sleep.
**Sat 18 — Colosseum, Forum, Palatine.** **Full Experience ticket €24 (underground + arena) releases 30 days ahead at 08:45 Rome time and sells out in minutes** — set an alarm for **19 Aug 2027, 02:45 Toronto**; tickets are nominative, one name change by day −7 ([artvisitguide.com](https://www.artvisitguide.com/colosseum-tickets-guide/), official: ticketing.colosseo.it). Best: 3-h private licensed guide (~€240 (est.)) starting Forum 08:30; lean: Walks of Italy / Through Eternity small-group underground tour ~€90 (est.). Lunch **Trattoria Monti** (€35). 15:00 Capitoline Museums (€16 (est.)): Dying Gaul, Capitoline Wolf, Tabularium over the Forum. Fallback (rain): reverse — Capitoline first.
**Sun 19 — Appian Way (car-free Sunday).** Bike from Appia Antica Caffè or Ecobike (~€15–25/day (est.)); Catacombs of San Callisto (€10, closed Wed) then Domitilla (closed Tue; San Sebastiano is closed Sunday), Cecilia Metella, Villa dei Quintili (Appia card €10 (est.)). Picnic from Testaccio market. 16:30 **Palazzo Massimo** (€12 (est.); Boxer, Livia's garden frescoes; closed Mon). Fallback: Baths of Caracalla.
**Mon 20 — Vatican + imperial fora.** Vatican Museums 08:00 slot (~€25 incl. booking (est.); closed Sun; book 60 days out); Pinacoteca → Raphael Rooms → Sistine → St Peter's. Lunch Prati **Bonci Pizzarium** (€12). 15:00 Trajan's Markets/Imperial Fora museum (€13 (est.)), Trajan's Column. Free walking tour of centro storico at 18:00 (tip-based).
**Tue 21 — Tivoli.** Metro B Ponte Mammolo + Cotral bus (~€3) to Villa Adriana (€12 (est.), 3 h), CAT bus to Villa d'Este (€15 (est.), closed Mon). Dinner back in Monti **La Taverna dei Fori Imperiali** (€40). Fallback: Ostia today, Tivoli tomorrow.
**Wed 22 — Ostia Antica → Naples.** Train from Porta San Paolo (Metro ticket) 08:30; Ostia (€18 (est.), closed Mon) until 12:30 — Thermopolium, Baths of Neptune, theatre. 14:xx Frecciarossa to Naples (bags at RomeHello). Evening pizza **Sorbillo** or **Da Michele** (marinara is dairy-free; margherita = fior di latte, ~€10).
**Thu 23 — Pompeii, full day.** Buy the **Pompeii+ ticket (€27.50 (est. 2027), Vivaticket, 20,000/day cap, 15,000 morning slots)** at least 2 weeks ahead ([pompeii-italy.org](https://pompeii-italy.org/reservations/), [thebettervacation.com](https://thebettervacation.com/rome/pompeii-tickets/)). Circumvesuviana 07:40 (35 min). Best: private licensed guide 3 h ~€200 (est.) covering Villa dei Misteri + amphitheatre; lean: official 2-h guided tour ~€46 ([thebettervacation.com](https://thebettervacation.com/rome/pompeii-tickets/)) then self-guide. Open to 19:00 in Sept. Lunch inside (Autogrill-run, mediocre) — bring food.
**Fri 24 — MANN + Capua.** 09:00 **MANN** (€22 (est.); closed Tue) with a private guide 2 h (~€150 (est.)) — Farnese Bull, Alexander mosaic, Secret Cabinet. 13:00 regional train to Santa Maria Capua Vetere (~50 min, €5): **Capua amphitheatre** (second-largest in Italy; Spartacus' school), Mithraeum, Gladiator museum (combined ~€10 (est.)). *Optional add (Beneventum):* Caserta→Benevento ~1 h for Trajan's Arch and the Roman theatre — only if you drop Capua's Mithraeum. Dinner **Tandem** (ragù, €25).
**Sat 25 — Phlegraean Fields.** Cumana/EAV train to Pozzuoli: Flavian amphitheatre (underground intact), Rione Terra, Temple of Serapis; bus to **Baia** (imperial thermal complex; castle museum with the Punta Epitaffio nymphaeum; museum closed Mon (est.)); EAV to **Cuma** — Sibyl's cave, acropolis, Virgil's Aeneid VI on your phone. Campi Flegrei circuit ticket ~€8–12 (est.; pafleg.cultura.gov.it unreachable). Optional: Baia underwater park glass-bottom boat (~€15). Dinner Pozzuoli waterfront fish (€30).
**Sun 26 — Herculaneum → Amalfi.** Circumvesuviana to Ercolano Scavi 08:30 (site opens 08:30, €16 (est.), bag storage); 3 h; continue to Sorrento; SITA bus or ferry to Amalfi (afternoon). Sunset walk to Atrani. Dinner **Da Gemma** (€45).
**Mon 27 — Path of the Gods (hike 1).** SITA bus Amalfi→Bomerano (~€2.50, 40 min). Bomerano→Nocelle ~7.8 km, +~200/−~400 m, 3–4 h, moderate; then the 1,700 steps down to Positano; ferry back (~€10). Total 5 h. Season perfect; no guide. Lunch from a Bomerano bakery. Fallback (rain/wind): Valle delle Ferriere from Amalfi (paper-mill ruins, 3 h).
**Tue 28 — Paestum → Matera.** Ferry Amalfi→Salerno 08:00; car pickup 09:00; A2/SS18 to **Paestum** 45 min. Three Doric temples + museum with the Tomb of the Diver (€15 (est.), open daily; museopaestum.cultura.gov.it unreachable). Lunch at a buffalo farm — **Tenuta Vannulo** (mozzarella di bufala: lower lactose than cow's but still fresh cheese — a small taste with lactase, and their yoghurt/gelato are dairy). 14:30 drive Paestum→Matera 2 h 45 (A2 to Sicignano, SS407 Basentana — free, well surfaced). Evening Sassi lit up.
**Wed 29 — Matera + Murgia (hike 2).** Morning Sasso Caveoso: Casa Grotta, rock churches Santa Lucia alle Malve / Madonna delle Virtù (€3–8 each), MUSMA. 14:00 **Parco della Murgia loop**: Sassi → Tibetan bridge over the Gravina → Belvedere (the "Passion of the Christ" view) → rock churches → back, ~8 km, +300 m, 3–4 h, moderate, no guide. Dinner **Vitantonio Lombardo** (best splurge, €90, book 3 wks) or **Dedalo** (€40). Fallback: guided Sassi walk instead.
**Thu 30 Sep — Cannae → Canosa → Egnazia → Lecce.** 08:00 depart. **Canne della Battaglia** (Antiquarium + citadel + Livy/Polybius column; €8; 09:00/10:00 opening — the day-of-week conflict above; SP142 from Barletta). Guide: turistinpuglia's 2-h licensed tour is **€250 in English / €200 Italian for the whole group** ([turistinpuglia.it](https://www.turistinpuglia.it/en/booking/guided-tour-of-the-reeds-of-the-battle/)) — expensive solo; best-column only. 11:30 **Canosa di Puglia** (9 km): Daunian hypogea, Roman bridge over the Ofanto, San Leucio temple, museum (~€10 (est.)); lunch. 14:00 Alberobello (1 h 20; 45-min trulli walk, Rione Aia Piccola side). 15:45 **Egnazia** (30 min): Messapian-Roman city on the Via Traiana, cardo, basilicas, museum (€6 (est.), open to ~18:00 (est.)). 17:30 → Lecce (1 h 10), drop car at station. Tight — the fallback is to skip Alberobello.
**Fri 1 Oct — Lecce + Otranto.** Roman amphitheatre (Piazza Sant'Oronzo) and theatre, Museo Faggiano (€6), Castromediano museum (Messapian). Lunch pasticciotto at **Natale** (custard = dairy; try the rustico instead) and **Alle Due Corti** (cicerie e tria, €25). 14:30 FSE train to Otranto (via Maglie, ~1 h 20, €5): Norman cathedral mosaic, castle, Punta Palascia walk. Dinner Lecce **Osteria degli Spiriti** (€35).
**Sat 2 Oct — Brindisi → Bari → Catania.** 09:00 train Lecce→Brindisi (30 min): the **Roman column marking the end of the Via Appia**, Museo Ribezzo, port. 13:00 train to Bari (1 h 10): old town, Basilica di San Nicola, Bari archaeological museum in Santa Scolastica; orecchiette on Arco Basso. 20:00 airport train (20 min); **FR6191 22:05 → CTA 23:20**; taxi to Catania B&B.
**Sun 3 Oct — Etna (hike 3) → Syracuse.** 08:00 collect Car B at CTA; 50 min to **Rifugio Sapienza** (1,900 m). **Above 2,800 m a certified guide is mandatory** ([apartmentincatania.com](https://www.apartmentincatania.com/en/hiking-mount-etna/)). Best: **Gruppo Guide Alpine Etna Sud** summit-craters excursion, 5–6 h, high difficulty, cable car + 4×4 to 2,900 m then on foot to ~3,300 m ([guidetna.it](https://www.guidetna.it/en/); price not published — expect €90–120 pp + the €78 cable car/4×4 package (est.)); or **Go-Etna Summit Trek** from €130, 6 h, 4.9/5 ([go-etna.com](https://www.go-etna.com/how-to-visit-etna/)). Lean: Funivia **Tour 3000** — cable car (€50 alone) + 4×4 + 60-min guided walk to 3,000 m, €78 ([sicilyactive.com](https://www.sicilyactive.com/en/mount-etna-cable-car)) plus the free Schiena dell'Asino trail (3–4 h, +600 m). Cable car runs 08:30–16:00 Apr–Nov. Summit is temperature 0–8 °C, wind — bring the shell and gloves. Summit access is closed by INGV during paroxysms: **fallback** = Valle del Bove rim (4–5 h, +700 m, no guide) or the lava-cave walk with Etna Nord guides. 17:30 → Syracuse (1 h 15), Ortigia by 19:00. Dinner **Apollonion**? no — **Osteria da Mariano** (€30).
**Mon 4 — Syracuse.** 08:30 **Neapolis park** (€15–17 (est.) ([tourismattractions.net](https://tourismattractions.net/italy/syracuse-attraction-ticket-prices)); open daily): Greek theatre, Ear of Dionysius, Roman amphitheatre, Altar of Hieron. 11:30 drive to **Castello Eurialo** (Dionysius I's fortress, 8 km; €4–10, ~09:00–19:00 ([audiala.com](https://audiala.com/en/italy/syracuse/castello-eurialo))). Dinner at the hotel terrace (€60) or **Kokalos** (€35).

## 9. RISKS

1. **Colosseum underground sells out in minutes** — the alarm is non-negotiable; fallback is a licensed-guide tour operator that holds Full Experience allocations (~€90–120, book at the same time as a backstop, cancellable).
2. **Etna closed to the summit by activity** (happens several times a year): the guide services re-route to Valle del Bove; you still get a 4–5 h volcano hike. Check INGV/guide WhatsApp the day before; don't book non-refundable.
3. **Late Ryanair BRI–CTA + two cars + one huge Puglia driving day (30 Sep).** Mitigation: the Catania overnight, Alberobello as the first thing to drop, sunrise starts. If the Sat 22:05 flight isn't in the W27 schedule, take ITA via FCO by day or shift Lecce by one night and fly Sun 16:35.
4. **Automatic-car scarcity and one-way fees in the South** — book by 1 Mar; if Salerno one-way is refused, take the train Salerno→Naples→Bari (Frecciarossa ~3 h 45, from €29.90 ([seat61.com](https://www.seat61.com/Italy.htm))) + FAL to Matera and rent in Matera instead (Paestum then becomes a day trip from Amalfi by ferry+train).
5. **Entry rules:** ETIAS becomes mandatory **April 2027** — €20, valid 3 years, apply online ≥1 month before ([schengenvisainfo.com](https://www.schengenvisainfo.com/etias/)); EES biometrics are in force at FCO — expect a longer arrival queue. Passport must be valid 3 months beyond 9 Oct 2027.
6. (Bonus) **Overcrowding/dirt hot-spots** on this route are Pompeii mid-morning, Positano's steps, Naples' Garibaldi station area and Palermo's Via Maqueda at night — the plan schedules around the first two and doesn't sleep near the last two.
