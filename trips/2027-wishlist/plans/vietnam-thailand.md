Researched 2026-09-12. All prices 2026 data unless marked (est.). FX used: **C$1 = US$0.72 = ฿23.8** ([x-rates](https://www.x-rates.com/table/?from=CAD&amount=1), accessed 2026-09-12, table dated Jan 2026) and **C$1 ≈ ₫18,800 (est., via US$1 ≈ ₫26,100)**. Re-check FX at booking.

## 1. WHEN + WHY

**Chosen: depart YYZ Wed 1 Dec 2027 (01:xx), in-country Thu 2 Dec – Fri 17 Dec 2027, 15 nights.** Home Fri 17 Dec.

- Hanoi/Ninh Binh/Ha Giang: December is Hanoi's **driest month** ([Cheapflights climate note](https://www.cheapflights.ca/flights/Toronto-YYZ/Hanoi-HAN.BR.ksp), acc. 2026-09-12); the grey "mưa phùn" drizzle sets in Jan–Mar. Ha Giang Dec–Feb is "cold but dramatic" with fewer tourists ([Loop Trails](https://looptrails.com/guide/ha-giang-jeep-tour-complete-guide-2026/), acc. 2026-09-12) — pack a real fleece; nights 5–10 °C (est.).
- Northern Thailand: cool season, clear skies, **no burning-season haze** (haze builds late Feb–Apr). Kew Mae Pan trail is open (reopens 1 Nov, closed Jun–Oct: [Thai National Parks](https://www.thainationalparks.com/doi-inthanon-national-park), acc. 2026-09-12).
- Holidays avoided: Tết 2027 (6–12 Feb 2027), Tết 2028 (26 Jan 2028), Christmas/NY peak fares. Day-of-week alignment: **Sun 5 Dec = Dong Van + Meo Vac Sunday markets** (Ha Giang's best culture day); **Sun 12 Dec = Chiang Mai Sunday Walking Street**; FD871 HAN→CNX operates Sat 11 Dec.
- Why 15 nights: 9 in Vietnam (two 2-night bases + a 3-day car loop + one overnight cruise need buffer nights in Hanoi), 6 in Thailand; anything shorter drops Ha Giang or Chiang Rai, anything longer adds only repeat days.

**Alternative window (stated as asked): Thu 21 Jan – Sat 6 Feb 2027 (16 n), Vietnam first (finish by 31 Jan, before Tết travel chaos), Chiang Rai before Chiang Mai so the last weekend hits the Chiang Mai Flower Festival, 5–7 Feb 2027, parade Sat 6 Feb 08:00 from Nawarat Bridge to Nong Buak Haad Park** ([Thailand Highlights](https://www.thailandhighlights.com/thailand/chiang-mai-flower-festival), acc. 2026-09-12; dates "subject to confirmation"). Downsides: Hanoi/Ha Giang colder and drizzlier, Ha Long fog, early haze in Chiang Mai, and Vietnam pre-Tết price spikes. Verdict: **Dec 2027 is better for weather and crowds; Jan–Feb 2027 only if the flower parade matters more than clear mountain days.**

±1 week delta: Nov 24–Dec 9 = cheaper fares (Kayak: November is the cheapest month at C$1,553 vs December C$2,219, [Kayak](https://www.ca.kayak.com/flight-routes/Toronto-YTO/Hanoi-Noibai-HAN), acc. 2026-09-12) but Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum may still be in its autumn closure; Dec 8–23 = same weather, fares rise sharply toward Christmas.

(c) Viator small-group 3D2N car from Ha Giang from US$269 pp, 4.9★/60 reviews, hotel in Dong Van + Du Gia homestay ([Viator](https://www.viator.com/tours/Ha-Giang/3-days-Ha-Giang-Loop-by-Car/d50728-159077P5), acc. 2026-09-12). ## 6. FOOD SHORTLIST (all prices est. per person unless cited)

1. **Bún Chả Hương Liên**, Hanoi — bún chả + nem cua bể; ₫80k. No dairy. Walk-in.
2. **Phở Gia Truyền Bát Đàn**, Hanoi — classic beef phở, queue 07:00; ₫60k. No dairy.
3. **Café Giảng / Café Đinh**, Hanoi — egg coffee; ₫35k. **Dairy: condensed milk** in the foam; ask for "ít sữa".
4. **Chả Cá Thăng Long**, Hanoi — turmeric-dill fish; ₫170k. No dairy.
5. **A Chef's Tour Hanoi** — evening street-food tour US$49–60, 12+ tastings, max 8, tell them lactose-intolerant (they skip yogurt/sữa chua stops). Book 2–3 weeks ahead.
6. **Dong Van Sunday market** — thắng cố, mèn mén (corn mash), bánh cuốn; ₫50k. No dairy.
7. **Trang An goat restaurants** (Ninh Binh) — dê tái chanh + cơm cháy; ₫150k.
8. **Khao Soi Khun Yai**, Chiang Mai — northern curry noodles; ฿60. Coconut milk only.
9. **Warorot Market + Sunday Walking Street**, Chiang Mai — sai ua, khanom jeen nam ngiao, mango sticky rice (coconut cream, no dairy); ฿150 grazing. Avoid Thai iced tea (condensed milk) or ask black.
10. **Modern (2–3):** **Blackitch Artisan Kitchen**, Chiang Mai (northern tasting menu, ~฿2,500, book 2–4 weeks ahead, warn of dairy); **Chapter Dining & Grill**, Hanoi (Vietnamese fine dining, ~₫1.5M, book 1 week); **Lung Eed**, Chiang Rai (local classic, ฿60).

## 9. RISKS

1. **Ha Giang road safety / weather.** Mitigation: 4-wheel private car with vetted driver (no motorbike, no "easy rider"), daytime driving only, insurance covering mountain roads; Canada rates Vietnam "exercise a high degree of caution" citing road fatalities and says not to drive motorcycles ([travel.gc.ca](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/vietnam), acc. 2026-09-12). Fog fallback built in.
2. **Scams & transport fraud in Hanoi** (fake airport drivers, meter taxis, card-game "friendly invitations", ATM skimming). Mitigation: Grab only or hotel pickup; never follow an invitation to someone's home; ATM inside banks; keep the same passport for domestic flights.
3. **Entry rules changing.** Vietnam e-visa portal errors are non-refundable — apply once, carefully, on evisa.gov.vn (not look-alike sites). Thailand's 30-day cut (Sep 2026) and proposed entry fee — re-check Aug 2027. No ETIAS/EES/FMM relevance.
4. **Water/food.** Bottled or boiled water only; hepatitis A/typhoid boosters, dengue precautions (repellent even in December in Thailand); Canada also flags methanol-tainted alcohol — drink only sealed reputable brands. Dairy: Vietnamese and Thai food is naturally dairy-light; the traps are egg coffee/condensed-milk coffee, Thai iced tea and sữa chua — say "không sữa" / "mai sai nom".
5. **Fog/cold in Lan Ha and Ha Giang in December; single supplements.** Cruise views can vanish in fog and cabins are twin-priced; homestays are cold. Mitigation: book a cabin with a heater, pack a down layer, treat the cruise as a 20-hour experience not a photo day; ask operators for single-share promos; keep Ninh Binh flexible (free-cancellation) to swap cruise date if a clear forecast appears.
