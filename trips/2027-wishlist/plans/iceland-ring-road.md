## 1. When + why

- **Fly out Thu 26 Aug, land Fri 27 Aug 06:15, fly home Wed 8 Sep 17:05 — 12 nights.** Ten nights forces 350-km days on the east/north legs; 13+ adds cost without a new region. Twelve gives one buffer-free but sane loop with two-night bases at Vík, Mývatn and Snæfellsnes.
- Why this window: (a) **aurora**: new moon 1 Sep (also 30 Sep), full moon 15 Sep ([calendar-12](https://www.calendar-12.com/moon_calendar/2027/september), [astro-seek](https://mooncalendar.astro-seek.com/full-moons-new-moons-2027)); astronomical darkness returns Reykjavík ~2 Sep, Akureyri ~6 Sep ([aurora-iceland.com](https://www.aurora-iceland.com/blog/when-does-aurora-season-start-iceland-darkness-dates/)) — so your east/north nights (1–6 Sep) are dark *and* moonless. (b) **Highland hiking still open**: Fimmvörðuháls trail season averages 25 Jun–15 Sep ([FÍ](https://www.fi.is/en/hiking-trails/trails/fimmvorduhals)); Þórsmörk highland bus ran 19 Jun–6 Sep in 2026 ([Trex](https://trex.is/tour/thorsmork/)). (c) **Crowds/prices** drop after ~20 Aug; daylight still ~15 h (sunrise ~05:50/sunset ~21:00 on 27 Aug; ~06:30/20:20 on 8 Sep, est.). (d) **Réttir**: northern round-ups start the first weekend of September (2026: Norðurþing 2–13 Sep, incl. Katastaðarétt/Tungurétt in Öxarfjörður 6 Sep, Skógaréttir near Húsavík 5 Sep — [nordurthing.is](https://www.nordurthing.is/is/frettir/rettir-i-nordurthingi-2026)); the famous southern ones are the second weekend (2026: Skaftholtsréttir/Hrunaréttir 11 Sep, Reykjaréttir 12 Sep — [sunnlenska.is](https://www.sunnlenska.is/frettir/sunnlenski-rettalistinn/)). 2027 list publishes late Aug 2027 (bbl.is "Réttalistinn").
- **±1 week**: **+1 week (2–15 Sep)** catches the big southern réttir and darker nights, but moon waxes to full on 15 Sep (kills aurora in the second half), Þórsmörk bus is over, Fimmvörðuháls is at its closing edge, and Norð Austur-type seasonal restaurants shut. **−1 week (19 Aug–1 Sep)** = more crowds, brighter nights (no true darkness), no réttir. Chosen window is the best compromise.

## 5. Day by day (one anchor, one food plan, fallback)

**Thu 26 Aug** — FI602 20:45.

**Fri 27 — Reykjavík.** Land 06:15, car by 07:30, Reykjavík 08:30; leave bag at hostel. Anchor: **Reykjavík Food Walk 12:00** (3 h, 8 tastings, lactose-free handled, 17,900 ISK ≈ C$206, refundable 24 h — [wakeupreykjavik.com](https://wakeupreykjavik.com/reykjavik-food-walk-explained)) — covers hot dog, lamb soup, fish and skyr in one go. Then **Sky Lagoon** 17:00 (Pure Pass ~8,490 ISK ≈ C$98 vs Blue Lagoon Comfort ~12,990 — [sliceoficeland](https://sliceoficeland.is/articles/blue-lagoon-vs-sky-lagoon-2026)); Sky is 15 min from town, no volcano-closure risk, cleaner ritual. Hallgrímskirkja tower 1,400 ISK if awake. Dinner light: **Bæjarins Beztu** hot dog (~750 ISK) or bed. Fallback: Perlan / Settlement Exhibition.

**Sat 28 — Golden Circle → Skógar.** 08:00 **Þingvellir** (Alþingi site, Almannagjá; free ranger talks from the visitor centre) → **Geysir** → **Gullfoss** → **Reykjadalur hot-river hike** (3.5 km each way, ~250 m, 2.5–3 h incl. soak; hot-spring boardwalk, bring towel) → Skógar (1h15). Lunch: Friðheimar tomato greenhouse (soup buffet ~3,500 ISK, dairy in the soup — ask for the plain tomato base) or bakery in Hveragerði. Dinner: **Hótel Skógafoss bistro** (arctic char ~4,500). Fallback (rain): skip Reykjadalur, add Secret Lagoon.

**Sun 29 — Fimmvörðuháls day hike → Vík.** **Hike 1**: Skógar → Baldvinsskáli hut → **Magni & Móði 2010 craters** and back: ~26 km, ~1,000 m, 8–9 h; turnaround at the footbridge (km 8, the "26 waterfalls" section) makes it 16 km/4–5 h ([FÍ](https://www.fi.is/en/hiking-trails/trails/fimmvorduhals), [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/fimmvorduhals-hike-complete-guide/)). No guide needed in clear weather; GPS/maps.me, poles, full waterproofs, go only if [safetravel.is](https://safetravel.is) is green; register a travel plan. Drive 30 min to Vík; dinner **Suður-Vík** (lamb, ~5,500 ISK; Thai curries are dairy-free). **Þórsmörk alternative** (if the trail is closed): Trex bus from Hvolsvöllur to Básar, 2026 price 14,900 ISK o/w from Reykjavík, less from Hvolsvöllur; 2026 season ended 6 Sep — 2027 dates TBC ([trex.is](https://trex.is/tour/thorsmork/)).

**Mon 30 — Vík coast.** **Reynisfjara** at 09:00 (sneaker waves: stay behind the posted line, never turn your back; the beach has killed tourists) → **Dyrhólaey** arch → Skógar Museum (3,000 ISK, turf houses; best folk museum in the south) → **Sólheimajökull** guided glacier walk optional 3 h (~C$130). Lunch: **Smiðjan Brugghús** burgers/beer (~3,500). Dinner: Ströndin Bistro or grocery Krónan. Fallback: Vík wool factory + Icewear.

**Tue 31 — Skaftafell.** Vík 08:00 → Eldhraun moss field → **Fjaðrárgljúfur** canyon → Skaftafell 10:30. **Hike 2: Kristínartindar via Svartifoss** — 18 km, 1,040 m, 6–8 h, strenuous, loose scree on the final ridge; free; no guide ([myoutdoorbasecamp](https://myoutdoorbasecamp.com/kristinartindar/)). **Best alternative**: **Falljökull guided glacier walk** — 5-h "Glacier Explorer" ~US$166 ≈ C$230, 3-h beginner ~US$97, gear included, free cancel 24 h ([comparison](https://skaftafellglacierhike.com/)); you can do Svartifoss (5.5 km) plus the 3-h glacier walk in one day. Dinner: Hof hotel restaurant or Glacier Goodies food truck at Freysnes (fish & chips ~3,200). Fallback: Svartifoss + Sjónarnípa only.

**Wed 1 Sep — Jökulsárlón → Höfn.** 08:00 **Jökulsárlón** and **Diamond Beach** before the buses; **Fjallsárlón** (quieter) 15 min west; Stokksnes/Vestrahorn (1,000 ISK, photographers' spot). Höfn by 16:00. **Langoustine dinner: Pakkhús** (whole langoustine tails ~9,000 ISK; grilled in garlic butter — ask for oil) or **Humarhöfnin**. New moon tonight: first real aurora chance from Höfn harbour if Kp ≥ 3 ([vedur.is/aurora](https://en.vedur.is/weather/forecasts/aurora/)). Fallback: Þórbergssetur museum (Hali).

**Thu 2 — East fjords → Seyðisfjörður.** Höfn → Djúpivogur (Eggin í Gleðivík) → **Stöðvarfjörður: Petra's Stone Collection** (2,000 ISK) → Fáskrúðsfjörður French Museum → Egilsstaðir → Fjarðarheiði pass → **Seyðisfjörður** (Route 93; ~5 h driving). Culture: blue church, rainbow street, Tvísöngur sound sculpture walk (1 h). Dinner: **Norð Austur** sushi (seasonal; 2026 season length TBC) or **Kaffi Lára El Grillo** (fish, ~4,500). Fallback: Skálanes nature reserve.

**Fri 3 — Seyðisfjörður → Dettifoss → Mývatn.** **Stuðlagil** west viewpoint (paved, 1,000 ISK) → Ring Road → **Route 862 (paved) to Dettifoss** → **Ásbyrgi** horseshoe canyon (1 h loop, Botnstjörn) → back to Mývatn (~1 h). **Vogafjós** dinner (smoked char, lamb, geothermal rye bread; their homemade cheese is the one thing to skip). Fallback: skip Ásbyrgi.

**Sat 4 — Mývatn.** **Hverir** mud pots (1,200 ISK) → **Krafla/Víti** → **Hverfjall** crater rim hike (3 km, 1 h) → **Dimmuborgir** lava (1.5 h) → **Höfði**/Skútustaðagígar → **Earth Lagoon** (ex-Mývatn Nature Baths; from 7,900 ISK, Sept 12–22 h — [earthlagoon.is](https://www.earthlagoon.is/)). Lunch: Gamli Bærinn (lamb soup ~2,800). Réttir: if the 2027 Norðurþing list shows a Sat/Sun round-up near Húsavík or Öxarfjörður, go — it's the real thing, free, respectful onlookers welcome. Fallback: Sigurgeir's Bird Museum.

**Sun 5 — Goðafoss → Akureyri → Siglufjörður.** **Goðafoss** 09:00 → Akureyri (botanic garden, Brynja ice-cream is dairy; Akureyri Art Museum) → Route 82/76 via Dalvík and the Héðinsfjörður tunnels → **Siglufjörður: Herring Era Museum** (award-winning, ~2,600 ISK, open daily to ~17:00 in Sept — verify at sild.is) + Folk Music Centre. Dinner: **Hannes Boy** or Siglo Hótel's Sunna (fish of the day ~5,500). Aurora: darkness returns to the north this week. Fallback: Akureyri stay if fog on Route 76.

**Mon 6 — Big drive → Snæfellsnes.** Siglufjörður → Route 1 west (Blönduós, Borgarnes) → Route 54 → Grundarfjörður/Rif (~5 h). Lunch: Blönduós or Borgarnes (Geirabakarí). Sunset at **Kirkjufell/Kirkjufellsfoss** (1,000 ISK EasyPark). Dinner: **Bjargarsteinn** (Grundarfjörður, ~7,000, book 1 wk). Fallback: none needed — driving day.

**Tue 7 — Snæfellsjökull National Park.** **Hike 3: Arnarstapi–Hellnar cliff walk** (2.5 km each way, easy, basalt arches) → **Djúpalónssandur** black beach + lifting stones → **Saxhóll** crater stairs → **Vatnshellir lava cave** guided (45 min, ~4,500 ISK) → Ólafsvík. Lunch **Fjöruhúsið** at Hellnar (fish soup — cream-based; skip or ask) ; dinner **Hótel Búðir** (best splurge, ~12,000 ISK tasting) or **Sjávarpakkhúsið** (Stykkishólmur, blue mussels ~4,000). Fallback: Stykkishólmur town + Library of Water; Freezer Hostel theatre if running.

**Wed 8 Sep** — 09:00 depart, Borgarnes, KEF by 12:30 (3 h), return car 13:00, FI603 17:05.

**Guided history**: Þingvellir ranger walks (free, summer schedule — check thingvellir.is); private Reykjavík history/Saga walk with a licensed guide ~C$250/2 h (best column); Herring Museum's live salting show (summer Saturdays only — likely over). **Free walking tour**: City Walk Reykjavik (tip-based, 2 h, daily). **Class**: the Food Walk is the food product; Salt Eldhús cooking class (Reykjavík, ~C$200, 3 h) is the "class" if you take a 13th night.

## 6. Food shortlist (10)

1. **Bæjarins Beztu**, Reykjavík — the hot dog "eina með öllu" ~750 ISK; dairy-free. No booking.
2. **Icelandic Street Food**, Reykjavík — lamb soup with free refills ~2,400; broth is dairy-free (fish stew is not).
3. **Messinn**, Reykjavík — fish pans ~4,800; heavy butter, ask for oil. Book 3 days.
4. **Dill** (best; Michelin) — Nordic tasting ~C$300; lactose flagged at booking, 3–4 wks ahead.
5. **Suður-Vík**, Vík — lamb/Thai, ~5,500; walk-in early.
6. **Pakkhús**, Höfn — langoustine ~9,000; book 1 wk in Sept.
7. **Norð Austur**, Seyðisfjörður — sushi from the fjord, ~8,000; seasonal, confirm 2027 closing date.
8. **Vogafjós**, Mývatn — smoked char + geothermal rye bread ~5,500; skip the cheese plate.
9. **Hannes Boy**, Siglufjörður — fish of the day ~5,500.
10. **Hótel Búðir**, Snæfellsnes — the one big dinner; book 2 wks.
Markets: Bónus/Krónan for skyr (fermented dairy — mild but not lactose-free; Ísey lactose-free skyr exists), rúgbrauð, harðfiskur, smoked lamb. Lamb, fish, rye bread are the dairy-safe core; plokkfiskur, fish soups and "brauð með smjöri" are not.

## 9. Risks

1. **Reykjanes eruption**: no eruption as of 18 Aug 2026, but magma keeps accumulating under Svartsengi and another eruption is "likely" ([IMO](https://en.vedur.is/about-imo/news/)). Impact is local (Blue Lagoon/Grindavík roads); KEF has stayed open through nine eruptions. Mitigation: Sky Lagoon instead of Blue Lagoon; keep the first night in Reykjavík, not Reykjanes. Canada advisory: "normal precautions" (9 Sep 2026, [travel.gc.ca](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/iceland)).
