## 1. WHEN + WHY

**Chosen: depart YYZ Wed 10 Mar 2027, arrive EZE Thu 11 Mar; depart EZE Wed 24 Mar, home Thu 25 Mar. 13 nights on the ground.**

- **March–April harvest beats mid-Nov jacarandas.** Luján de Cuyo Malbec harvest peaks in March, Uco Valley runs into April; wineries (Nieto Senetiner, Santa Julia, Bressia) sell hands-on picking/sorting experiences only in this window; weather 20–25 °C ([Into the Vineyard](https://www.intothevineyard.com/journal/what-harvest-season-is-like-in-argentina), acc. 2026-09-12). Aconcagua's day-hike season (15 Nov–10 Apr) covers both windows, but mid-Nov trails can still hold snow at 3,400 m; March is dry and stable ([El Refugio Aconcagua](https://www.elrefugioaconcagua.com/en/blog/noticias-2/guide-to-activities-and-permits-aconcagua-provincial-park-2026-2027-5)). Jacarandas (peak 15–20 Nov, [Tripadvisor locals](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g312741-i979-k14398044-Jacaranda_trees_change_to_see_them_in_late_November-Buenos_Aires_Capital_Federal_District.html)) are pretty but not a reason to lose harvest + reliable hiking.
- **Vendimia 2027 avoided.** Official calendar: Bendición de los Frutos Sat 27 Feb; Vía Blanca Fri 5 Mar; Carrusel + Acto Central Sat 6 Mar; repeat Sun 7 Mar ([El Sol](https://www.elsol.com.ar/mendoza/confirmaron-el-calendario-de-la-fiesta-nacional-de-la-vendimia-2027-cuando-sera-el-acto-central/), acc. 2026-09-12). We reach Mendoza 18 Mar: hotel prices and crowds have dropped, harvest still on.
- **Holidays worked around:** Wed 24 Mar = Día de la Memoria (national holiday, huge march in BA; museums closed) — it is our departure day. Good Friday 26 Mar / Easter 28 Mar — we are already gone, so no Easter fare spike. Museo Andes 1972 is closed Sundays — we visit Tue/Wed.
- **Length: 13 nights** — 4 BA + 1 Colonia + 2 Montevideo + 4 Mendoza + 2 BA buffer. The final 2 BA nights put the domestic flight a day before the international one (domestic cancellations are the trip's #1 risk, see §9) and give a second bite at BA (Tigre, MALBA). Punta del Este/José Ignacio dropped: in March it is a half-empty beach town and would cost a Mendoza day.
- **±1 week delta:** one week earlier (3–17 Mar) collides with Vendimia hangover pricing in Mendoza and BA would be hotter (~28 °C); one week later (17–31 Mar) puts Easter weekend (26–28 Mar) in Mendoza — wineries closed Good Friday afternoon, lodging +30–50 % (est.), flights home pricier. Chosen week is the sweet spot.

## 6. FOOD SHORTLIST (price pp, dairy notes; "MEP-card rate")

1. **El Desnivel**, San Telmo — bife de chorizo + papas, USD 15. No dairy in the meat; skip provoleta. Walk-in.
2. **Don Julio**, Palermo — the classic modern parrilla; entraña, USD 60–80 with wine. Book online exactly 30 days ahead; provoleta is their signature — decline politely.
3. **Gran Dabbang**, Palermo — Argentine-Asian small plates, USD 30–40; many dishes yoghurt-based — ask. No reservations; go 19:30.
4. **El Cuartito**, Talcahuano 937 — fugazzeta pizza is all cheese; order fainá (chickpea, dairy-free) + a beer, USD 8.
5. **La Cocina**, Recoleta — empanadas salteñas, USD 2 each; carne picante is dairy-free.
6. **Buen Suspiro**, Colonia — picada board USD 25; ask for extra charcuterie instead of cheese.
7. **Mercado del Puerto / Cabaña Verónica**, Montevideo — asado de tira + medio y medio, USD 30. Weekday lunch only.
8. **Jacinto**, Montevideo — modern Uruguayan, lunch USD 25–30, dairy flagged on menu.
9. **Casa El Enemigo**, Maipú — winery lunch by Alejandro Vigil, USD 70–90 with pairing, book 3+ weeks; can cook dairy-free on notice.
10. **Fuente y Fonda**, Mendoza — classic share plates (milanesas, pastel de papa contains cheese), USD 20.
Dulce de leche is everywhere (alfajores, facturas, flan); provoleta and queso mantecoso are the traps; asado, chimichurri, empanadas de carne, choripán, malbec are all safe. Lactase tablets from Toronto — Argentine pharmacies stock them but pricey.

## 9. RISKS + MITIGATIONS

1. **Domestic flight cancellation (Flybondi-style meltdown).** → Fly Aerolíneas; MDZ→AEP the day *before* AC91; if cancelled, Andesmar/Cata semi-cama coach Mendoza→Retiro (13 h, daytime departures exist, ~USD 60) or a same-day JetSMART.
2. **Peso/card chaos.** Visa/MC now settle at ~MEP rate, sometimes slightly below; ATMs cost USD 10+/withdrawal with low caps ([Rio Times](https://www.riotimesonline.com/argentina-official-rate-card-rate-foreign-card-cost-2026/)). → Pay by card almost everywhere; carry USD 100 bills for a cueva/Western Union only if the gap re-opens; never change on Florida St. Uruguay: pay restaurants/car hire by foreign card for the 9-point VAT cut — extended to 30 Apr 2026 ([EY](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/uruguay-extends-application-of-reduced-vat-rate-for-tourism-sector-through-30-april-2026)); renewal for 2027 not yet published — check Jan 2027; lodging 0 % VAT is permanent.
3. **Street theft in BA (motochorro phone snatching; La Boca beyond Caminito; Retiro/Constitución).** → Phone away at kerbs, cross-body bag, Uber/Cabify not street taxis (rigged meters), La Boca daylight only, Palermo/Recoleta safe till late, San Telmo side streets not after 22:00 ([NomadicSean](https://nomadicsean.com/is-buenos-aires-safe-2026/)). Montevideo Ciudad Vieja empties at night — Uber door-to-door.
4. **Altitude/weather at Confluencia (3,400 m, wind, sudden closures).** → Sleep two nights in Mendoza first, start at 09:45 latest, turn around by 13:30, Laguna Horcones fallback; buy permit online beforehand (not sold at gate); March is inside the 15 Nov–10 Apr day-hike season.
5. **Entry/holiday friction.** Canadians: no visa, 90 days, reciprocity fee abolished 2019, Declaración Jurada online, onward ticket ([EntryBrief](https://entrybrief.com/ca-passport/ar/)). Uruguay: no visa, 90 days, stamped at the ferry terminal in BA. 24 Mar holiday closes museums and clogs central BA — planned as the travel day; Good Friday 26 Mar avoided. No ETIAS/EES relevance.
