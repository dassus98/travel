# Northern France (Paris · Normandy · Brittany · Champagne) — solo, 9 → 23 Sept 2027 (13 nights)

Researched 2026-09-12; all URLs accessed that day. FX: **€1 = C$1.61** (ECB reference 11 Sep 2026, https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/eurofxref-graph-cad.en.html). "(est.)" = extrapolated from 2026 data.

## 1. WHEN + WHY

**Depart YYZ Thu 9 Sept 2027 (overnight AC880) → arrive CDG Fri 10 Sept → fly home CDG Thu 23 Sept. 13 nights.**

Mid-September beats late May–June for this route:
- **Bayeux Tapestry is unavailable in both windows.** The museum "closed for renovation work until Autumn 2027" (https://www.bayeuxmuseum.com/en/the-bayeux-tapestry/); the project page says "opening is expected in October 2027" (https://www.bayeuxmuseum.com/en/actus/follow-the-stages-of-the-new-bayeux-tapestry-museum-project/). The tapestry itself is at the British Museum **10 Sept 2026 – 11 July 2027** (https://www.britishmuseum.org/exhibitions/bayeux-tapestry), then goes back for conservation and re-installation. So no 2027 date before October shows it, and October is a first-target date that can slip. Decision: **don't plan around it**; Bayeux stays for the D-Day beaches, cathedral and Museum of the Battle of Normandy. If seeing the tapestry is non-negotiable, push the trip to **late Oct 2027 or 2028** (lever noted in §9).
- **D-Day week inflation avoided.** Early June Normandy lodging doubles and guides sell out months ahead; mid-Sept is shoulder pricing with guides freely available.
- **Weather/sea:** Sept is Brittany's driest, warmest-sea month; hydrangeas/heather on the Granit Rose still out; sunset ~20:00 (vs 22:00 in June — the one real loss).
- **Journées Européennes du Patrimoine Sat 18–Sun 19 Sept 2027** (third weekend, https://icalendrier.fr/fetes/journees-du-patrimoine) — we're in Saint-Malo/Dinan, where private manors, the Grand Bé and normally closed ramparts towers open free. Deliberately *not* in Paris that weekend (mob scenes).
- **Cancale oysters** are best after the "R" months start; Sept is prime.
- French school holidays ended ~1 Sept; Parisians are back at work, so museum queues are far shorter than May–June.

**Length:** 13 nights = 7 bases with only two 1-night stops (Rouen, Mont-Saint-Michel — both are deliberately short "evening + dawn" experiences). 12 nights would cut Reims; 14 would just add a Paris day.

**±1 week delta:** One week earlier (2–16 Sept): Paris still emptier, fares similar, but misses Patrimoine weekend and the Cancale season opener. One week later (16–30 Sept): Paris Fashion Week (late Sept, est.) pushes Paris hotel prices +20–30 % (est.), Perros-Guirec chambres d'hôtes start closing for the season around 30 Sept.

- TER BreizhGo examples: Rennes–Saint-Malo from €9 (https://www.ter.sncf.com/bretagne) — backup if car plans change.

**Car (5 days, Bayeux → Rennes one-way, automatic):** Bayeux has Hertz, Europcar, Avis, Budget, Free2Move, UCAR; avg **C$82/day**, small cars C$73, best booked ~90 days out (https://www.ca.kayak.com/Cheap-Bayeux-Car-Rentals.41226.cars.ksp). Automatics are a minority at small-town branches — **reserve by early June 2027** (Europcar/Hertz "automatic" filter; Peugeot 208/2008 EAT8 class), or pick up at Caen station (bigger fleet, 20 min by train) if Bayeux shows none. Budget: compact automatic €65–85/day + one-way fee €50–80 + zero-excess "Premium/Super Cover" €18–25/day → **€600–750 (est.) all-in** for 5 days. Fuel ~€70 (≈550 km), A84 is toll-free; the only toll is trivial. No ZTL/low-emission zones on this route outside Paris (where you won't drive). IDP + Ontario licence + credit card in your name. Parking: Bayeux street lots free after 19:00; Mont-Saint-Michel car park **€22/24 h mid-season (Sept)**, free shuttle "Le Passeur" every ≤12 min, 12 min ride (https://montsaintmichel.gouv.fr/acceder-au-mont-saint-michel/horaires-et-tarifs); Saint-Malo intra-muros hotels with garages (below) or Paul Féval P+R free with shuttle; Perros-Guirec free at Trestraou; Carnac free at Maison des Mégalithes; Rennes station return.

**Local:** Paris Navigo Easy card + carnet (~€2.50/ride, est.); everything else walkable.

## 5. DAY-BY-DAY

**D1 Fri 10 Sept — Paris (arrive).** RER B to Gare du Nord/Marais. Anchor: Marais walk + Place des Vosges; 15:00 Sainte-Chapelle timed slot (**€22**, https://www.sainte-chapelle.fr/en). Food: L'As du Fallafel (rue des Rosiers, ~€10, dairy-free). Fallback: nap.

**D2 Sat 11 — Louvre.** 09:00 timed slot (**€32 non-EEA**, closed Tue; Wed/Fri until 21:00; calendar opens ~4 months out, https://www.artvisitguide.com/louvre-museum-tickets-guide/). Afternoon free walking tour (Discover Walks / Sandemans, tip-based ~€10–15). Evening splurge: Bistrot Paul Bert (11e, ~€60–75; steak-frites, ask for sauce on the side). Fallback: Musée Carnavalet (free).

**D3 Sun 12 — Versailles half day + Orsay.** RER C 07:45; Passport **€35** (Palace slot only needed; gardens 07:00–20:30; closed Mon) (https://en.chateauversailles.fr/plan-your-visit/tickets-and-prices). Back by 14:00 → Musée d'Orsay (~€16 est.; closed Mon). Street food: Marché des Enfants Rouges (Alain Miam Miam sandwich ~€12). Fallback: swap to Mon-open Louvre? No — Orsay/Versailles both close Mon, so this order is fixed.

**D4 Mon 13 — Rouen.** Nomad 09:03 St-Lazare → 10:50. Cathedral (Monet's façade), Gros-Horloge, Place du Vieux-Marché (Joan of Arc stake site), Historial Jeanne d'Arc (~€11 est.; **closed Mon** — do it D5 09:30 before the train, or skip). Splurge dinner: **La Couronne** (oldest inn in France, 1345; duck rouennais, ~€60–90). Lean: Rue du Gros-Horloge crêperie.

**D5 Tue 14 — Bayeux.** TER via Caen (~11:00 → 13:15). Cathedral, Museum of the Battle of Normandy (~€8 est.), British cemetery, evening ramparts stroll. Dinner: La Rapière (ask for cream-free fish).

**D6 Wed 15 — D-Day, American sector, full day.** *Lean:* Bayeux Shuttle group "American D-Day Experience" **€108** (Omaha, US Cemetery, Pointe du Hoc, Sainte-Mère-Église, Utah) (https://bayeuxshuttle.com/tour/) or Ophorus small-group **€140** from Place de Québec (https://www.ophorus.com/product/247/d-day-tour-utah-omaha-beaches-ste-mere-eglise-pointe-du-hoc-us-cemetery). *Best:* Bayeux Shuttle **private guide from €625** (≤7 pax, museum fees incl., 24 h free cancel; Viator lists it at US$406 pp) (https://www.viator.com/tours/Bayeux/Private-Tour-Full-Day-Tour-to-American-D-Day-Beaches-from-Bayeux/d909-7237P6). Book 2–3 months ahead. Lunch packed (Bayeux Saturday market is missed; use boulangerie). Fallback: Arromanches 360 cinema.

**D7 Thu 16 — Juno → Mont-Saint-Michel.** 09:00 collect car; 25 min to **Juno Beach Centre** (Courseulles): museum **€9**, museum + 45-min bunker tour with a Canadian guide **€13.50**, Sept 09:30–18:00 (https://www.junobeach.org/practical-information/). Arromanches Mulberry harbour en route (15 min). Drive A84 to MSM (1 h 45), park (€22), shuttle, check in. Abbey 15:30 (**€16**, Sept–Apr 09:30–18:00). Dinner on the Mont: Auberge Saint-Pierre or a galette; skip Mère Poulard's €40 omelette. Dawn walk on the ramparts.

**D8 Fri 17 — Bay crossing → Saint-Malo.** **Hike 1:** Chemins de la Baie guided round-trip crossing from Bec d'Andaine (Genêts) or Saint-Léonard: **€22 adult, 13–15 km, ~6 h**, barefoot, quicksand demo, 45–60 min on the Mont (https://www.cheminsdelabaie.com/traversee/commentees-aller-retour-a-pied/). Dates are tide-locked — check the Sept 2027 calendar when published (~Jan 2027) and adjust D7/D8 order if needed; English departures exist. Drive 1 h to Saint-Malo; evening ramparts loop (2 km).

**D9 Sat 18 — Patrimoine weekend: Dinan + Cap Fréhel.** Morning: Dinan (30 min), medieval ramparts, rue du Jerzual, free Patrimoine openings. **Hike 2:** GR34 Cap Fréhel ↔ Fort La Latte, 8 km return, ~3 h, 100 m cumulative, easy-moderate, free (fort ~€8 est.). Dinner: Breizh Café Saint-Malo (galettes; note butter/cheese — order complète without cheese, ~€15).

**D10 Sun 19 — Cancale → Perros-Guirec.** Marché aux huîtres at Cancale port: a dozen No. 3 ~€8–12 with lemon, eaten on the sea wall (est.). Optional 7 km Pointe du Grouin GR34 stretch first. Drive 2 h 15 to Perros-Guirec.

**D11 Mon 20 — Côte de Granit Rose.** **Hike 3:** Sentier des douaniers (GR34) Trestraou → Ploumanac'h lighthouse → Trégastel Grève Blanche and back, ~14 km, 4–5 h, flat but rocky; extend to Île Renote. Boat to the Sept-Îles gannet colony (~€25 est., seasonal, gannets thinning by late Sept). Fallback (rain): Lannion old town; Crozon swap (Pen-Hir cliffs, 10 km loop) if you'd rather add 2 h driving for bigger cliffs.

**D12 Tue 21 — Carnac → Rennes → Reims.** 07:30 depart; 2 h 45 to Carnac. Alignments: Sept guided tours only (~€8 est.), Maison des Mégalithes free viewpoints. 13:00 drive 1 h 30 to Rennes station, return car 15:00; TGV 15:35 → Montparnasse 17:10; metro 4/RER to Gare de l'Est; TGV 18:28 → Reims 19:14. Dinner: Café du Palais (Art-deco brasserie, ~€35).

**D13 Wed 22 — Reims → Paris.** Cathedral (coronation church) 09:00; **Taittinger** cellar tour (chalk crayères, from ~€35 est., 3 tour formats, reopened July 2024, https://www.taittinger.com/en/visit) or Ruinart (~€80 est., books 2 months out). Lunch: Le Jardin at Les Crayères (~€45 menu, the one Champagne splurge). TGV 17:xx → Paris; final Paris night near Gare du Nord.

**D14 Thu 23 — fly.** RER B 07:15; AC881 ~10:35.

**Cooking class / food tour:** *Best:* **La Cuisine Paris** market-to-table class (~€150–165, est.; Marais location; dairy-adaptable on request) slotted D2 morning if Louvre moves to Fri 10 evening slot (open till 21:00). *Lean:* Paris by Mouth or Eating Europe Marais tour (~€120 est.) — or skip.

**Festivals/events:** Journées du Patrimoine 18–19 Sept (free); no ticketing needed. Nothing else clashes.

## 9. RISKS

1. **Bayeux Tapestry not viewable (certain).** Museum reopening "expected October 2027" and the loan runs to 11 July 2027. Mitigation: treat Bayeux as D-Day base; online scene-by-scene viewer; if essential, shift to late Oct 2027 (weather worse, Perros lodging closed, MSM crossings sparse) or defer to 2028.
2. **Automatic car unavailable at Bayeux.** Mitigation: book by 1 June; fallback Caen station (20 min TER) or Hertz Caen airport; keep the rail alternative (Bayeux→Pontorson TER + MSM bus; Saint-Malo TER from Rennes €9).
3. **Tide calendar vs itinerary.** Bay crossing dates depend on 2027 tides. Mitigation: lock Feb 2027; MSM night can become Fri 17 with Saint-Malo compressed, or use a shorter "departure from the Mont" walk.
4. **Entry requirements.** EES biometric registration is live at CDG (expect queues, allow 90 min for the RER-to-Paris plan); ETIAS launch date withdrawn, "likely 2027", €20, 3-year validity — apply only via the official EU site when announced (https://etias.com/articles/etias-drops-2026-date-as-launch-slips-out-of-view). Passport valid ≥3 months past 23 Sept 2027.
5. **Paris hostel-private quality / rail strikes.** The People Marais has documented plumbing/noise issues — use Generator high floor or Jeanne d'Arc. French rail strikes cluster around autumn budget season; Nomad/TER refundable to day before; keep D12's Rennes→Paris→Reims chain loose (fallback: overnight Paris, Reims as day trip on D13).
