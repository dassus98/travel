# Oaxaca — Día de Muertos 2027 (solo, from YYZ)

Researched 2026-09-12. All URLs accessed 2026-09-12. FX used: **1 CAD = 12.24 MXN** ([Wise](https://wise.com/us/currency-converter/cad-to-mxn-rate/history)); **1 USD ≈ 1.36 CAD** (est.). Prices are 2026 data; Day-of-the-Dead (DdM) lodging in 2027 marked (est.) = 2026 baseline × the documented festival multiplier.

## 1. WHEN + WHY

**Sat 23 Oct – Wed 3 Nov 2027, 11 nights** (2 CDMX + 9 Oaxaca). Nov 1, 2027 is a **Monday**, so the core falls Sun 31 Oct (Xoxocotlán/Atzompa vigils) → Mon 1 Nov (Etla Muerteada, city comparsas) → Tue 2 Nov (Panteón General, Etla/Zautla comparsas, San Felipe). Arriving Oaxaca 25 Oct gives 5 quieter days for Monte Albán, Sierra Norte, Mitla/Hierve el Agua and a cooking class before the crowds peak, while markets are already full of cempasúchil and pan de yema from ~25 Oct ([Monte Albán Heritage Center](https://montealbanoaxaca.com/day-of-the-dead-2026-in-oaxaca-the-ultimate-travelers-guide-to-dates-times-festivities-activities-and-more/)). Weather: end of rains — Oct 26.5/14 °C, 50 mm; Nov 26.6/11 °C, 10 mm ([climatestotravel](https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/mexico/oaxaca)). 11 nights is enough for all anchors plus one rest day; longer only adds cost at 2–3× rates.

**±1 week delta:** a week earlier misses every vigil/comparsa (the whole point). A week later (Nov 3–10) is cheaper (Volaris MEX–OAX from 71 MXN in Nov vs 351 in Oct, [Volaris](https://www.volaris.com/en-mx/flights-from-mexico-city-to-oaxaca-1)) and hotels drop to baseline, but the festival is over. **Mixquic (CDMX) Nov 1–2 is incompatible** with being in Oaxaca — CDMX goes at the front for Templo Mayor/Anthropology only.

## 9. RISKS

1. **Lodging sell-out / cancellation lock-in.** 2–3× rates, 3–5 night minimums, 7-day or non-refundable. → Book at inventory open, prefer free-cancellation rate even at +10%, insurance with trip-cancellation.
2. **Highway crime (Puebla–Oaxaca autopista) and roadblocks.** Aug 30/Sep 1 2026 ADO attacks; GC advisory on intercity buses/roadblocks. → Fly both domestic legs; no rural roads after dark; valley tours in licensed vans only.
3. **Night-crowd hazards at Xoxocotlán/Etla** — pickpockets, no apps/taxis late, gridlock, cell dead zones. → Small cash only, phone in front pocket, guided tour or pre-hired taxi with fixed meeting point, back by 01:00, never street-hail.
4. **Entry/admin:** Canadians need only a passport valid for the stay + the online **FMMD** (QR at arrival, downloadable once — keep the PDF); tourist fee is bundled in the air ticket ([travel.gc.ca](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/mexico), [FMMd explainer](https://www.getmethehookup.com/mexicos-new-digital-immigration-form-by-air-the-fmmd-explained/)). → Screenshot FMMD; carry a passport copy at night.
5. **Closures/disruptions:** Hierve el Agua community dispute; Oaxaca protest *bloqueos*; Museo de las Culturas & CDMX museums Mon; altitude (3,200 m) on the Sierra hike. → Verify Hierve the night before, keep valley tour as swap day, hydrate/acclimatise, avoid mezcal the night before the hike.
