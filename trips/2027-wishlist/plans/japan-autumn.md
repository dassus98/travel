# Japan — Autumn Foliage 2027 (Tokyo → Kanazawa → Kyoto → Kumano Kodo → Osaka)

Solo, 14 nights, Sat 27 Nov – Sat 11 Dec 2027. All sources accessed 2026-09-12. FX used: **C$1 = ¥110.7** ([Wise mid-market](https://wise.com/ca/currency-converter/cad-to-jpy-rate)); ¥10,000 ≈ C$90. Anything "(est.)" is my estimate, not a sourced figure. No car: this route is 100% rail/bus, so no IDP/automatic budget needed.

## 1. WHEN + WHY

**Dates:** depart YYZ Fri 26 Nov (arrive HND Sat 27 Nov), fly home from KIX Sat 11 Dec 2027. **14 nights** (Tokyo 4, Kanazawa 2, Kyoto 5, Kumano/Yunomine 1, Osaka 2).

**Why these dates.** Kyoto's historical peak window is Nov 22–Dec 7, densest Nov 29–Dec 3 ([WaTabi](https://watabi.org/en/articles/kyoto-autumn-foliage-guide)), but recent years run late: the JMC forecast issued 2 Sep 2026 puts Tokyo maples at **Nov 29** and Kyoto at **Dec 11** (six days behind the Dec 5 average) ([JapanTravel](https://en.japantravel.com/article/autumn-foliage-2026/72752), [BluePlanet](https://selfguidejapan.com/blog/kyoto-autumn-leaves-2026)). Landing in Tokyo Nov 27 and being in Kyoto Dec 3–8 straddles both a "normal" and a "late" year, and the Kyoto illuminations/special openings (Tofukuji Nov 15–Dec 7, Eikando to early Dec) are all still running ([japan-guide Tofukuji](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3930.html), [japan-guide Eikando](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3956.html)). Kanazawa's core window is Nov 10–25 extending into early Dec ([BluePlanet Kanazawa](https://selfguidejapan.com/blog/kanazawa-autumn-leaves-2026)) — Dec 1–3 is late there, accepted trade-off. No Japanese public holiday falls in the window (Labour Thanksgiving is Nov 23, before arrival). Weather: Kyoto/Tokyo early Dec ~5–14 °C, mostly dry (est.).

**Why 14 nights.** Five regions plus a jet-lag day; 4 nights Tokyo and 5 Kyoto are the minimum to do temples at opening time (the only real crowd defence) and still fit Kamakura, Kurama–Kibune and Nara. Takayama/Shirakawa-go cut (1-night detour adds ¥15k+ and a bus day; better as a winter trip).

**±1 week:** a week earlier (Nov 19–Dec 4) catches Kenrokuen's free illumination nights (2026: Nov 7/14/15/21/22/28/29, 18:00–20:45) and Tokyo ginkgo (~Nov 26) but risks pre-peak Kyoto in a late year; a week later (Dec 3–17) is safest for Kyoto but Tokyo/Kanazawa are past and Tofukuji/Eikando specials end ~Dec 7. Chosen dates are the hedge.

**JR Pass verdict: skip.** 7-day ordinary ¥50,000 (¥53,000 if bought abroad from 1 Oct 2026), 14-day ¥80,000/¥84,000 ([BluePlanet JR Pass](https://selfguidejapan.com/blog/japan-rail-pass-price-2026)). This route's long-distance rail totals ~¥37,000 spread over 9 days, so even a 7-day pass loses. Total intercity + day trips ≈ **¥42,000 (C$380)**; add local IC-card transit ~¥1,500/day × 14 = ¥21,000 (C$190).

**IC card:** Suica in Apple Wallet works on foreign iPhones (top-up can fail with some foreign cards — cash/7-Bank ATM); most non-Japanese Androids lack Osaifu-Keitai, so take a physical Welcome Suica (airports/major stations; iOS-only Welcome Suica Mobile app, 180-day validity, balance non-refundable). Works in Kyoto/Osaka (ICOCA area), not on one journey crossing IC areas ([JapanTravelSolved](https://www.japantravelsolved.com/suica-japan-tourists-2026/)).

**Kyoto buses:** bus-only day pass is gone; the **Subway & Bus 1-Day Pass ¥1,100** also covers the Sightseeing Limited Express EX100/EX101 (Kyoto Stn → Kiyomizu → Gion → Heian → Ginkakuji, ¥500 IC single, every 7–8 min from 09:04) ([Kyoto City EX bus](https://www2.city.kyoto.lg.jp/kotsu/webguide/en/bus/limited_express.html), [1-Day Pass](https://www2.city.kyoto.lg.jp/kotsu/webguide/en/ticket/regular_1day_card_comm.html)). Rail over bus in foliage season; no suitcases on city buses — forward luggage (ta-Q-bin ~¥2,500/leg est.).

## 5. DAY-BY-DAY

Sunrise ~06:30, sunset ~16:45 — front-load outdoor time. Museums mostly close Mon (Dec 6 is the Kyoto Monday: use it for Kurama/Kibune, which never closes).

- **D0 Fri 26 Nov:** AC1 13:05.
- **D1 Sat 27 Nov Tokyo:** land ~16:00, Suica, Keikyu to Shibuya. Food: Ichiran/Fuunji-style tonkotsu or tsukemen (~¥1,200; broths are pork/fish, no dairy). Fallback: konbini + sleep.
- **D2 Sun 28 Tokyo (Meiji/Harajuku, Shinjuku Gyoen):** Tokyo Localized free Harajuku–Meiji walk (tip-based) ([Tokyo Localized](https://www.tokyolocalized.com/)); Shinjuku Gyoen (¥500, closed Mon) for late maples/ginkgo; Shinjuku night free walk 2 h. Food: Omoide Yokocho yakitori (~¥3,000). Tsukiji closed Sundays.
- **D3 Mon 29 Tokyo (Kamakura hike + Daibutsu):** JR to Kita-Kamakura; **Daibutsu Hiking Course** Jochiji → Zeniarai Benten → Kotoku-in, 60–90 min, unpaved/steep bits, dry weather only ([japan-guide](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3113.html)); add the Ten-en ridge course (Kenchoji → Zuisenji, 60–90 min) for a 4–5 h day. Food: shirasu-don in Hase (~¥1,500). Fallback: Mt Takao (Keio from Shinjuku ¥430, 50 min; Trail 6 up/Inariyama down ~2.5 h; weekday only, "very crowded" in late Nov) ([japan-guide Takao](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3029.html)).
- **D4 Tue 30 Tokyo (Tsukiji, Yanaka, teamLab):** Tsukiji Outer Market 07:00 sushi breakfast (open 5–14, closed Sun/hol, some Wed) ([japan-guide](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3021.html)); Yanaka Ginza + cemetery ginkgo; **teamLab Planets** 18:00 slot (from ¥3,600, not sold on site, weekends sell out — book ~6 weeks ahead) ([Standard Japan](https://thestandardjapan.com/art/teamlab-tokyo-guide)). Optional cooking class: Cooking Sun Tokyo sushi class (Kyoto price ¥10,500 solo, [Cooking Sun](https://www.cooking-sun.com/kyoto/)).
- **D5 Wed 1 Dec → Kanazawa:** Kagayaki ~08:00, arrive ~10:30. Omicho Market kaisendon lunch (snow-crab season; ~¥3,000). Kenrokuen (¥320, 8:00–17:00; free early entry 06:30–08:00 via Mayumizaka gate) + Castle park. Higashi Chaya at dusk. Food: Kanazawa oden izakaya (~¥3,500).
- **D6 Thu 2 Kanazawa:** Kenrokuen at 06:30 free window; Nagamachi samurai district (Nomura house ~¥550); 21st Century Museum (closed Mon); D.T. Suzuki Museum. Food: Kanazawa curry lunch; sushi counter dinner (~¥6,000). Fallback: Myoryuji "Ninja temple" (reservation).
- **D7 Fri 3 → Kyoto:** train by 09:00, arrive ~11:30; luggage forward. **Tofukuji** Tsutenkyo (¥1,000 in special period, 8:30 opening; go at 08:30 or after 15:30 — "extremely crowded" midday) ([japan-guide](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3930.html)). Evening: Eikando light-up 17:30–21:00 (¥1,000) ([japan-guide](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3956.html)). Food: Nishiki Market snacks 15:00 (tamagoyaki, yuba — no dairy).
- **D8 Sat 4 Kyoto (Arashiyama early, Gion):** bamboo grove 07:00, Tenryu-ji (¥500), Jojakko-ji/Gio-ji maples, Okochi-Sanso; back by noon (weekend crowds). Afternoon: Kyoto Localized/Kyoto Free Walking Tour Gion evening walk (tip-based) ([KFWT](https://kyotofreewalkingtour.com/gion-walking-tour)). **Splurge:** Gion Nishikawa 1-star kaiseki counter ¥10–28k, book 2–3 weeks ahead; or Yoshikawa tempura ¥12–28k ([RFK](https://restaurantsforkings.com/blog/kyoto-best-restaurants-solo-dining-2026)). Kaiseki is dairy-free by tradition.
- **D9 Sun 5 Kyoto (Fushimi Inari dawn, Higashiyama):** Fushimi Inari 06:30 to the summit loop (4 km, 1.5 h, free); Kiyomizu at 06:00 opening ([WaTabi](https://watabi.org/en/articles/kyoto-autumn-foliage-guide)) is the alternative; EX100 bus back. Afternoon: **Cooking Sun izakaya class 14:00–17:00, ¥10,500** (daily; full refund ≥7 days) ([Cooking Sun](https://www.cooking-sun.com/kyoto/)).
- **D10 Mon 6 Kyoto (Kurama–Kibune hike):** Eizan from Demachiyanagi to Kurama; **Kurama-dera (¥500) → ridge → Kibune, 3.9 km, 2–3 h** ([Inside Kyoto](https://www.insidekyoto.com/kurama-to-kibune-hike)); extend with Kibune-guchi → Kifune shrine and, for a 5-h day, return on foot. Lunch: Kibune soba. Fallback (rain): Kyoto National Museum (closed Mon — use Nishijin textile centre instead).
- **D11 Tue 7 Kyoto (Nara + Uji):** Kintetsu Ltd Exp 35 min ¥1,280; Todaiji ¥800 before 10:00, Kasuga Taisha inner ¥700, deer crackers ¥200 — rutting season, bucks aggressive ([Nara guide](https://naradaytripfromkyoto.com/)). Return via JR Nara Line to Uji (Byodo-in ¥700 est., matcha at Nakamura Tokichi — matcha itself is dairy-free; skip parfaits). Food: Nara kakinoha-zushi.
- **D12 Wed 8 → Kumano Kodo (signature hike):** Kyoto ~07:00 → Shin-Osaka → Kuroshio → Kii-Tanabe ~10:00; bus #6 → Hosshinmon-oji (~12:30 est.). **Hosshinmon-oji → Kumano Hongu Taisha: 7.5 km, 2–3 h, +190 m/−460 m, Level 2**, forest and hamlet views, no guide needed ([TB-Kumano](https://www.tb-kumano.jp/en/kumano-kodo/nakahechi/tsugizakura-oji-to-kumano-hongu-taisha/hosshinmon-oji-to-kumano-hongu-taisha/)). Oyunohara torii at sunset; bus 10 min to Yunomine; minshuku dinner (tell them "nyū-seihin nashi" — no dairy; kaiseki-style is naturally clear). Fallback (heavy rain): stay on the bus to Yunomine, Tsuboyu bath (~¥800).
- **D13 Thu 9 → Osaka:** dawn Tsuboyu; optional Dainichi-goe Yunomine → Hongu 3.4 km 1 h; bus to Kii-Tanabe, Kuroshio to Shin-Osaka ~15:00. Dotonbori night; Mizuno okonomiyaki (~¥1,500; pork/seafood, no cheese unless ordered) or Kuromon.
- **D14 Fri 10 Osaka:** Osaka Castle park ginkgo; Kuromon Market lunch; Shinsekai kushikatsu (Daruma, ~¥2,500). Fallback: Minoo Falls maple walk (2.8 km, Hankyu 30 min).
- **D15 Sat 11 Dec:** Nankai Rapi:t to KIX; AC60 home.

**Guided history (BEST):** licensed English guides (Kyoto Visitor's Host / TripAdvisor) ~¥35–45k/day (est.), 4–6 weeks lead; Tokyo Localized paid Yanaka/Tsukiji tours; Kumano Travel guided Nakahechi walks (~¥15–20k est., 2–3 months ahead). **Festivals:** no major matsuri in window; Kenrokuen light-ups end ~Nov 29 (2026 pattern).

## 6. FOOD SHORTLIST

1. Tsukiji Outer Market sushi counter breakfast, Tokyo — ¥2,500–4,000; nigiri set; dairy-free; walk-in 07:00.
2. Omoide Yokocho yakitori, Shinjuku — ¥3,000; tare skewers, no dairy; walk-in.
3. Fuunji-style tsukemen, Shinjuku — ¥1,200; fish-pork dip; avoid "cream" or cheese ramen variants; queue.
4. Omicho Market kaisendon, Kanazawa — ¥2,500–4,000; crab/uni bowl; dairy-free; morning.
5. Kanazawa oden izakaya (Kuroyuri-style) — ¥3,500; kani-men when in season; dairy-free; walk-in.
6. Nishiki Market graze, Kyoto — ¥2,000; yuba, tamagoyaki, tsukemono; skip soft-serve; 10–17.
7. Gion Nishikawa (1★ kaiseki lunch counter) — ¥10–28k; omakase; kaiseki is dairy-free; book 2–3 wks ([RFK](https://restaurantsforkings.com/blog/kyoto-best-restaurants-solo-dining-2026)). *Modern.*
8. Yoshikawa tempura counter, Kyoto — ¥12–28k; lunch tempura; batter only, no dairy; 2–3 wks. *Modern.*
9. Yunomine minshuku dinner — included; onsen-steamed vegetables, ayu; specify no dairy at booking.
10. Mizuno okonomiyaki, Dotonbori / Daruma kushikatsu, Shinsekai — ¥1,500–2,500; ask "cheese nashi"; walk-in queues 30–60 min.

Dairy rule: Japanese savoury food is almost dairy-free; the traps are soft-serve, matcha lattes/parfaits, "cream" ramen, cheese tonkatsu, Hokkaido-style butter dishes and pastries. Carry lactase for izakaya cheese-plate moments.

## 9. RISKS

1. **Late/early foliage year** — JMC 2026 shows Kyoto peak Dec 11; a 2027 repeat leaves Dec 3–8 slightly early. Mitigation: dates already hedge; keep Kyoto lodging refundable to Oct; watch the early-Oct and early-Nov JMC updates and shift ±5 days if fares allow.
2. **Kyoto crowds/overtourism** — Tsutenkyo and Arashiyama unbearable 10–15h; buses packed, luggage banned in practice. Mitigation: temples at opening (Tofukuji 08:30, Kiyomizu 06:00, Fushimi 06:30), max two headline temples/day, subway/JR/EX100 not regular buses, forward suitcases, two "quiet" alternates (Komyo-ji Nagaokakyo ¥1,000, Jojakko-ji).
3. **AC59/60 Osaka not operating in Dec 2027** — mitigation: book HND return instead; last night in Tokyo (Nozomi ~¥14k) or fly ITM→HND on ANA/JAL (~¥15k est.).
4. **Kumano timing/weather** — single-day bus connections are sparse; heavy rain makes the Nakahechi slick. Mitigation: verify timetable #6 for Dec 2027 in Oct; keep the shorter Dainichi-goe or a Hongu-only day as fallback; trekking shoes, poles optional; Yunomine booked with dinner so no evening logistics.
5. **Entry/admin** — no visa/ETA needed for Canadians in 2027 (JESTA FY2028 at earliest); departure tax ¥3,000 in ticket; carry passport at all times (tax-free rules shift to refund-at-airport from 1 Nov 2026). Cash still needed for small shops, minshuku and some IC top-ups; ~¥30,000 cash from a 7-Bank ATM on arrival. Health/insurance: bring lactase, insurance with hiking cover.
