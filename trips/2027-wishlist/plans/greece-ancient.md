# Greece, Ancient-History Maximal 2027 — Athens → Attica → Peloponnese → Delphi/Thermopylae → Crete (15 nights, automatic car)

Researched 2026-09-12; all URLs accessed 2026-09-12. FX: **€1 = C$1.58 (est.)**, US$1 = C$1.37 (est.). "(est.)" = my estimate, not a quoted price. Web-search budget ran out mid-research: lodging rates and most site tickets are est. from the 2025 Ministry tariff — verify on Booking.com / hhticket.gr in Jan 2027. Chitra: solo, automatic + IDP, mildly lactose-intolerant (LI notes inline). Prefix ✔ = verified.

## 1. WHEN + WHY

**Dates: depart YYZ Thu 30 Sep 2027 → land ATH Fri 1 Oct → fly home from ATH (via Chania) Sat 16 Oct 2027. 15 nights in Greece.**

- **Early October is the sweet spot.** Athens/Crete ~24 °C days, sea still warm, summer opening hours (Acropolis to 20:00 through 31 Oct ✔ [thebettervacation.com](https://thebettervacation.com/athens/acropolis-official-website/)), Samaria Gorge open to 31 Oct ✔ ([samaria-gorge.gr](https://samaria-gorge.gr/)), crowds well off August, Parnassus/Taygetus cool enough for 5–6 h hikes. May is equal on weather but Samaria only opens ~1 May (flood-dependent, opened 30 Apr in 2026 ✔ [cretetip.com](https://www.cretetip.com/samaria-gorge-closed-until-30-april-2026/)) and spring fares are higher.
- **Avoid 25–26 Sep 2027**: last September weekend = free-entry days at all state sites (European Heritage Days) → worst Acropolis crowds of autumn ✔ ([archaeology-travel.com](https://archaeology-travel.com/travel-tips/buying-tickets-for-the-acropolis-and-other-ancient-sites-in-athens/)). Window starts 1 Oct, safely after.
- **No public holidays 1–16 Oct** (Ohi Day is 28 Oct; also a free-entry day). No Monday problem: state archaeological sites open daily; NAM opens Mon from 13:00 (est.); Acropolis Museum Mon 09:00–17:00 (est.).
- **Flights bottom in Oct**: AC lists YYZ–ATH from **C$950 RT for 14–21 Oct 2026** ✔ ([aircanada.com](https://www.aircanada.com/en-ca/flights-to-athens)); Kayak's Oct range is US$745–1,023 vs Dec US$984 ✔ ([kayak.com](https://www.kayak.com/flight-routes/Toronto-Pearson-Intl-YYZ/Athens-ATH)).
- **Why 15 nights:** Athens 4 + Nafplio 3 + Sparta 1 + Olympia 1 + Delphi 2 + Heraklion 2 + Chania 2. Chitra wants *every* battle/event site — Marathon, Salamis, Plataea, Thermopylae, Chaeronea, Leuctra-adjacent Thebes, Mantinea/Tegea en route, Bassae as a bonus — and Crete adds Minoan Knossos/Phaistos + Samaria. Cutting to 13 means dropping Crete or the northern battlefield loop; 15 is the minimum that keeps both with four 5 h+ hikes.

**±1 week delta:** −1 week (24 Sep–9 Oct) collides with the free-entry weekend, +2 °C. +1 week (7–23 Oct) = Samaria at higher rain-closure risk (Oct 50 mm ✔ [cretelocals.com](https://cretelocals.com/hiking-samaria-gorge/)), Attica sites move to 18:00 closing mid-Oct (est.), fares ~equal.

**Rental car (Mon 4 Oct ATH airport → Tue 12 Oct ATH airport, 8 days, no one-way fee):**
- Company: **Sixt ATH** (8.6/10 on Kayak ✔; on-airport) or Hertz/Avis; local CarQ/Caldera rate 10/10 ✔ ([kayak.com](https://www.kayak.com/Athens-Eleftherios-Venizelos-Airport-Car-Rentals.ATH.cap.ksp)). Kayak "medium" class averages US$30–52/day across the year ✔; ATH guide warns automatics "are less common in Greece — book in advance" ✔ ([santorinidave.com](https://santorinidave.com/rent-car-athens-airport)).
- **Book "Compact automatic" (Corolla/Golf class) by Feb 2027.** Budget **€50/day (est.)** + **supplier zero-excess (Sixt Loss Damage Waiver Plus / Hertz Super Cover) €18/day (est.)** = **≈€545**. Deposit pre-authorised on a credit card ✔ ([stressfreecarrental.com](https://www.stressfreecarrental.com/en/car-hire/athens-airport/zero-excess)). Lean lever: card CDW instead — but Greek gravel tracks (Bassae, Menelaion) make zero-excess the sane choice. IDP mandatory for Canadian licences in Greece (bring it).
- **Tolls (cars, 2026 ✔ [vignetim.com](https://vignetim.com/vignettes-and-tolls/greece), [tolls.eu](https://www.tolls.eu/greece)):** Attiki Odos €2.55 flat ×2; Elefsina €2.50; Isthmos €2.10; Moreas Corinth→Sparta ~€9 (est., full Corinth–Kalamata €11.80); Olympia Odos Pyrgos–Patras ~€6 (est.), Rio €2.70; **Rio–Antirrio bridge €15.90**; PATHE Tragana €4.55 + Thiva €4.55 + Afidnes €3.90. **Total ≈€57.** Cash or card at every plaza ✔.
- **Fuel:** 95 unleaded **€2.09/L** (7 Sep 2026 ✔ [tolls.eu](https://www.tolls.eu/greece)); ~1,450 km × 6.5 L/100 km ≈ **€195**.
- **Parking:** Athens night of 4 Oct — hotel/garage Psyrri ~€20 (est.); Nafplio harbour/Karathona lots free but full by 11:00 (est.); Sparta, Olympia, Delphi free street/hotel parking; every site has a free lot. No ZTLs in Greece; Athens has an odd/even "daktylios" ring rule — irrelevant, don't drive into the centre except to reach the garage.
- **Driving safety:** Greece is among the EU's higher road-fatality rates (est. ~60/million); risk is drivers overtaking on the shoulder and mountain bends, not the roads themselves. All legs are daylight, ≤3.5 h, mostly motorway or good two-lane. Skip the Langada pass (Sparta–Kalamata) — not on route.
- **Crete, no car:** KTEL Heraklion→Chania coach ~2 h 45, €15 (est.); Knossos city bus #2 €1.70 (est.); Phaistos/Gortyn by 1-day automatic hire from Heraklion (€55 incl. cover, est.) or a €50 group tour; Samaria by KTEL Chania→Omalos (05:00/06:15/07:45/08:45 ✔ [cretelocals.com](https://cretelocals.com/hiking-samaria-gorge/)) + Anendyk ferry Agia Roumeli→Sougia/Chora Sfakion **€17** ✔ ([anendyk.gr](https://anendyk.gr/prices/)) + return coach.

- *Best:* **Ergon House** (Mitropoleos, food-hall hotel) or **InnAthens** (courtyard boutique) est. €210–260 incl. breakfast, Booking ~9.0 (est.); both with garages nearby.

**Nafplio (3 n)**
- *Lean/Mid:* **Pension/Hotel Marianna** — under the Akronafplia rock, 300 m from centre, direct-booking rate guarantee ✔ ([hotelmarianna.gr](https://www.hotelmarianna.gr/)); est. €95–120 incl. its famous home breakfast; steep steps (no car access — park at the harbour). **Sparta (1 n)** — *Lean:* **Menelaion Hotel** (1930s, central, parking) est. €75–95; *Best:* **Mystras Grand Palace Resort** (Mystras village, pool) est. €160–220; **Pyrgos of Mystra** boutique est. €150.

**Olympia (1 n)** — *Lean:* **Hotel Pelops** (18 rooms, 15 min walk to site, free parking, buffet breakfast, cooking courses on request ✔ [hotelpelops.gr](https://www.hotelpelops.gr/)) est. €70–90; *Best:* **Hotel Europa** est. €120–150.

## 5. DAY-BY-DAY

Site tickets (est., 2025 tariff; summer rates to 31 Oct): Acropolis **€30 timed, year-round** ✔; Acropolis Museum **€20** ✔ ([thebettervacation.com](https://thebettervacation.com/athens/acropolis-official-website/)); **the state multi-site combo ticket was abolished 1 Apr 2025** ✔ ([archaeology-travel.com](https://archaeology-travel.com/travel-tips/buying-tickets-for-the-acropolis-and-other-ancient-sites-in-athens/)) — pay per site (Agora €10, Kerameikos €8, Roman Agora €8, Olympieion €8, NAM €12, Sounion €20, Mycenae €20, Epidaurus €20, Mystras €12, Olympia €20, Delphi €20, Knossos €20, Heraklion Museum €12, Phaistos €12; est.). Book Acropolis at **hhticket.gr** ✔; slots sell out 5–7 days ahead in high season ✔.

**D1 Fri 1 Oct — Athens.** Land 10:40, metro, check-in. Anchor: **Acropolis Museum** 15:00 (Fri late opening, est. to 22:00) — Parthenon gallery first, then Areopagus rock for sunset (free). Food: **Karamanlidika tou Fani** (Evripidou 52) cured-meat platters, €22; LI: skip the cheese board. Fallback: rain → museum only.

**D2 Sat 2 Oct — Acropolis + classical city.** **08:00 slot**, private licensed guide 2.5 h (Athens Walking Tours: Acropolis small-group €49, Acropolis + Museum 4 h €110 ✔ [athenswalkingtours.gr](https://www.athenswalkingtours.gr/); private licensed guide €150–300 for the group, est.). Then Ancient **Agora** (Stoa of Attalos museum), **Roman Agora/Hadrian's Library**, **Kerameikos** (Themistoclean wall, Dipylon), **Pnyx** (Demosthenes' bema) — all walkable. Food: **Diporto** (Theatrou 9, no sign, no menu) chickpea soup + grilled fish ~€15 cash; LI: feta on the table — decline. Evening: free walking tour of Plaka/Anafiotika (tip-based, 18:00, Athens Free Walking Tour, est.).

**D3 Sun 3 Oct — NAM + Piraeus.** **National Archaeological Museum** 2 h with a licensed private guide (est. €150 for 2 h; book via athensguide.gr / a Tourist-Guide-Association member) — Mask of Agamemnon, Artemision Zeus, Antikythera mechanism. Afternoon metro to **Piraeus Archaeological Museum** (Piraeus bronzes, €6 est.) and the Zea/Kantharos harbour walls, Themistocles' fleet base. Food: **Atlantikos** (Avliton 7) fried anchovies + grilled sardines €18, no dairy. Sunset Lycabettus. Fallback: Benaki Museum.

**D4 Mon 4 Oct — Attica battlefields (car day 1).** Metro to ATH airport (40 min), collect car 09:00. **Marathon**: Soros tumulus (the 192 Athenian dead) + Marathon Archaeological Museum + Plataean tumulus at Vranas (€6, est.; Tue closure possible — Mon is safe). **Rhamnous** fortress + Nemesis sanctuary (€6, est., 30 min, empty). **Brauron** if time. **Cape Sounion** for the Poseidon temple at sunset (€20, est.; opens to sunset). Drive back to Psyrri garage (1 h 15). Food: Sounion — **Akrogiali** taverna on the beach, grilled octopus + gigantes €22. Fallback: rain → Marathon museum + Sounion under cloud is still fine.

**D5 Tue 5 Oct — Salamis, Eleusis, Corinth → Nafplio (150 km).** 07:30 leave via Piraeus to **Perama / "Xerxes' throne" hill on Mt Aigaleo** (free viewpoint over the Salamis strait, 30 min). **Eleusis** sanctuary + museum (Telesterion, €6 est.). Motorway to the **Corinth Canal** (old bridge viewpoint), **Ancient Corinth** (Apollo temple, bema of St Paul, museum €8 est.), then **Acrocorinth** — hike 1: 3 km/300 m round trip inside the walls, 1.5 h, free, moderate, no guide. Nafplio 17:30. Food: **Kakanarakis 1986** (Vasilissis Olgas) rabbit stifado €14. LI: no cheese needed. Fallback: skip Rhamnous-style extras; Corinth museum is indoors.

**D6 Wed 6 Oct — Argolid Bronze Age loop (110 km).** **Mycenae** at 08:00 (Lion Gate, Grave Circle A, cistern with headtorch, Treasury of Atreus, museum €20 est.) with a local licensed guide met on site (2 h, €120–180 est.; ask the hotel or book via getyourguide "private Mycenae guide"). **Nemea** (Temple of Zeus, the stadium's vaulted tunnel, €6 est.). **Argos**: theatre + Larissa castle (free). **Tiryns** cyclopean galleries (€8 est.) on the way home. Food: lunch at **Taverna To Mycenaiko** (Mykines village) est. €15; dinner Nafplio **Aiolos** taverna (Vas. Olgas) lamb kleftiko €15 (contains no cheese; ask). Fallback: rain → Nafplio Archaeological Museum (Dendra armour, €6).

**D7 Thu 7 Oct — Epidaurus + Nafplio.** **Epidaurus** at 08:00 (theatre acoustics test, Asklepieion, museum €20 est.), 40 min drive. Back by 12:30: **Palamidi** via the 999 steps (€8 est., 45 min up), Bourtzi view, Akronafplia. Afternoon swim at Arvanitia. Food: **Alaloum** (modern Greek, Papanikolaou 10) €28, splurge; or **Antica Gelateria di Roma** for a sorbet (dairy-free options). Fallback: rain → Epidaurus museum + Nafplio's War Museum.

**D8 Fri 8 Oct — Nafplio → Tegea → Sparta (140 km, 2 h).** Via Tripoli: **Tegea** museum + temple of Athena Alea (€4 est., 20 min); **Mantinea** walls (free, 15 min) — the 362 BC battlefield where Epaminondas died. Sparta: **Menelaion** (Menelaus & Helen shrine, Bronze Age mansion, free, gravel track, 2 km walk, Eurotas views), **ancient Sparta** acropolis/theatre (free), **Archaeological Museum of Sparta** (€4 est.), Leonidas statue. Food: **Diethnes** (Palaiologou 105) garden taverna, lamb with orzo €13. Fallback: Museum of the Olive & Greek Olive Oil (€4).

**D9 Sat 9 Oct — Mystras + Taygetus → Olympia via Bassae (215 km, 3.5 h driving).** **Mystras** at 08:00 (€12 est.): park at the upper gate, walk down through the Despots' Palace, Pantanassa, Peribleptos, Metropolis (2.5 h). Hike 2: **Mystras → Taygeti village → Langadiotissa gorge → Parori** foothill loop, ~7 km, 350 m, 2.5–3 h, waymarked, moderate, no guide (est.); autumn is ideal. 13:00 depart via Megalopolis–Andritsaina to the **Temple of Apollo Epicurius at Bassae** (Iktinos' tent-covered temple, €6 est., 1,130 m, last 20 km twisty). Olympia 18:30. Food: **Taverna Bacchus** (Ancient Pissa, 5 min drive; "best in Olympia" est.) grilled pork, horta, €18. LI: order the Bacchus salad without feta.

**D10 Sun 10 Oct — Olympia → Rio–Antirrio → Delphi (215 km, 3 h).** **Olympia** at 08:00 (€20 est.): Philippeion, Temple of Zeus, run the stadium, Pheidias' workshop; museum for the Hermes of Praxiteles and Nike of Paionios. Local licensed guide 2 h €120–150 (est.). 12:00 drive Pyrgos–Patras motorway, cross the **Rio–Antirrio bridge** (€15.90 ✔), lunch in **Nafpaktos** (Lepanto 1571 harbour) — **Tsaras** grilled sardines €14 — then the corniche via **Galaxidi** to Delphi 17:30. Food: **Taverna Vakhos** (Delphi, Apollonos 31) rooster in wine, gigantes €16.

**D11 Mon 11 Oct — Delphi + Corycian Cave.** **Delphi** at 08:00 (€20 est.; open Mon): Sacred Way, Athenian Treasury, Temple of Apollo, theatre, stadium; museum (Charioteer, Sphinx of Naxos, Siphnian frieze ✔ [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delphi)); Athena Pronaia tholos + Castalian spring on the road below. Private licensed guide 2 h €150–200 (est.; the hotel or delphitours.gr can book). Hike 3: **Delphi → Kroki → Corycian Cave (Pan & the Nymphs) on Parnassus** via the E4: ~12 km round trip, +800 m, 5–6 h, strenuous, waymarked but bring a GPX; headtorch for the cave; no guide needed in dry weather (est.). Food: **To Patriko Mas** (Delphi) €20; or **Panagiota** in Arachova (kokoretsi, formaella cheese — skip). Fallback: rain → museum + Arachova; cave hike swaps with Wed morning if needed.

**D12 Tue 12 Oct — Battlefield loop → ATH → fly to Crete (330 km, ~5 h driving).** 07:30 **Osios Loukas** monastery (11th-c. mosaics, €4 est., 40 min). **Chaeronea**: the marble Lion over the Theban Sacred Band (free, roadside) + small museum. North to **Thermopylae** (1 h): Leonidas monument, the Kolonos hill of the last stand (epitaph stone), Thermopylae visitor centre (€3 est.), hot springs waterfall (free, swim). Back south on the PATHE: **Plataea** (Erchomenos village, walls of the 479 BC city, free) and **Thebes Archaeological Museum** (€6 est.). ATH airport by 17:30, return car, **Aegean/Sky to HER ~20:30** (est.). Taxi HER→old town €15. Food: pack a Delphi bakery lunch; Heraklion late supper at **Kritikos Fournos**/street souvlaki €8. Fallback: if Delphi hike ran late, drop Plataea.

**D13 Wed 13 Oct — Knossos + Heraklion Museum.** **Knossos** at 08:00 with a licensed private guide (est. €150–200 2.5 h, or small-group €40 est.), then bus #2 back for the **Heraklion Archaeological Museum** (Phaistos Disc, bull-leaper fresco, snake goddesses, €12 est.) — the museum makes sense *after* the site. Afternoon: Venetian walls walk to Kazantzakis' grave, Koules fortress. Food: **Peskesi** (Kapetan Haralampi 6–8; Cretan slow food from its own farm) €32 splurge; LI: avoid staka, order "apaki", "chochlioi", wild greens; book 2 days ahead.

**D14 Thu 14 Oct — Phaistos + Gortyn → Chania.** 1-day automatic hire from Heraklion (€55 est.) or a group tour (€50 est.): **Gortyn** (Law Code, Roman odeon, €6 est.), **Phaistos** (€12 est., grand staircase, Messara views), optional Agia Triada. Return car 15:00, KTEL to Chania (2 h 45, €15 est.), arrive 18:30. Food: **Tamam** (Zambeliou 49, in an old hammam) lamb with wild greens €18.

**D15 Fri 15 Oct — Samaria Gorge.** Hike 4: **Samaria** — 16 km, −1,250 m, 5–7 h, moderate-strenuous on knees ✔ ([hikeload.com](https://hikeload.com/blog/samaria-gorge-hike-guide-2026)); open **1 May–31 Oct, full crossing entry 07:00–13:00** ✔ ([samaria-gorge.gr](https://samaria-gorge.gr/)); fee **€10** ✔ ([crete-guide.com](https://www.crete-guide.com/the-selection/samaria-gorge)); KTEL 06:15 to Omalos, **ferry Agia Roumeli→Sougia 17:30, €17** ✔, coach back to Chania ~20:00. Lean: DIY ~€40 total (est.). Best/organised: GetYourGuide "Samaria trek from Chania" from **€32.40** ✔ ([cretelocals.com](https://cretelocals.com/hiking-samaria-gorge/)) — same walk, guaranteed seat. Fallback (rain closure): **Imbros Gorge** (8 km, 2.5 h, €2.50 est.) or Agia Irini. Food: Agia Roumeli beach taverna lunch €12; dinner **Oinopoieio** (Splanzia) grilled fish €22.

**D16 Sat 16 Oct — Chania → ATH → YYZ.** 06:00 taxi to CHQ (€25 est.); A3 ~08:00 → ATH; AC897 → YYZ afternoon. If the morning connection is tight, skip Samaria's coach and stay overnight near Chania airport.

**Guided-history summary:** Acropolis (D2), NAM (D3), Mycenae (D6), Olympia (D10), Delphi (D11), Knossos (D13). Lead time: private licensed guides for October fill 4–6 weeks out; book by 15 Aug 2027. **Cooking class:** Athens Walking Tours hands-on 4 h with 3-course dinner **€98** ✔ (fit on D3 evening). **Food tour:** Athens Food Tour 3 h **€69** ✔ (D1 alternative if not jet-lagged). **Free walking tour:** Plaka/Monastiraki, tip-based (D2 evening).

## 6. FOOD SHORTLIST (10)

1. **Diporto**, Athens — classic cellar; chickpeas, fried fish, retsina; €15 cash; no menu; dairy easy to skip.
2. **Karamanlidika tou Fani**, Athens — pastourma/soutzouki meze; €22; cheeses optional.
3. **Atlantikos**, Athens — modern-casual seafood; €18; dairy-free.
4. **Seychelles** (Kerameikos) — modern Greek bistro, €30, book on Instagram/phone; ask for dishes "choris tyri".
5. **Alaloum**, Nafplio — modern; €28; book.
6. **Diethnes**, Sparta — garden taverna; €13.
7. **Taverna Bacchus**, Ancient Pissa/Olympia — €18; salad without feta.
8. **Taverna Vakhos**, Delphi — gigantes, rooster kokkinisto; €16.
9. **Peskesi**, Heraklion — Cretan farm-to-table; €32; book 2 days ahead; LI flag staka/mizithra.
10. **Tamam**, Chania — Ottoman-Cretan; €18; walk in before 20:00. Bonus: **Bougatsa Iordanis** Chania is mizithra-filled — a lactase-tablet moment, not a skip.
LI rule: souvlaki without tzatziki, dakos without mizithra, moussaka/pastitsio have béchamel; gigantes, horta, grilled fish, fava, dolmades, briam, stifado are all safe.
