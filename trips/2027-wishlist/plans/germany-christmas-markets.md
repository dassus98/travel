# German Christmas Markets 2027 — Southwest + Northwest + Weimar, by train

Solo, Chitra (28, CAN). 14 nights, **Wed 24 Nov (fly) → Thu 9 Dec 2027**. All-in: **LEAN ≈ C$5,300 · BEST ≈ C$10,100**. FX used: €1 = C$1.58 (est., Sept 2026 mid-rate; re-check at booking). Access date for all links: 2026-09-12. 2027 market dates are not yet published; each is derived from the published 2026 date shifted −1 day to the same weekday, marked "(est.)".

## 1. WHEN + WHY

- **Totensonntag 2027 = Sun 21 Nov** (Advent 1 = 28 Nov). Most markets open the Mon–Thu after it and several (Köln Dom, Freiburg, Neumarkt) close *on* it — so do not arrive before ~Tue 23 Nov ([koeln-interaktiv](https://koeln-interaktiv.de/weihnachtsmaerkte-koeln/), [Freiburg official](https://weihnachtsmarkt.freiburg.de/en/)).
- **Chosen: arrive Thu 25 Nov, fly home Thu 9 Dec (14 nights).** Every market on the route is open by 25 Nov (est.), the two big weekends (1st + 2nd Advent) fall on the quieter stops (Freiburg/Heidelberg), and Köln's three markets are visited Mon–Wed when they are least crowded. Ravenna Gorge runs Thu–Sun only, so Freiburg must hold a Thu–Sun: opening weekend 25–28 Nov (est.). Weekday departure and return keep fares lower.
- **Why 14 not fewer:** 6 bases + 4 day trips (Aachen, Erfurt, Osnabrück optional, Siebengebirge) + 4 hikes; each 2-night base gives one full day; Köln gets 3 for Aachen + Drachenfels. 15 adds nothing; 12 would drop Münster or a hike.
- **±1 week delta:** one week earlier (18 Nov) = Totensonntag closures + Erfurt/Weimar/Esslingen/Ravenna not yet open. One week later (2–16 Dec) works (all markets run to 22–23 Dec; Schauinsland lift likely reopened, see §5) but Köln Heumarkt/weekend crowds grow and fares rise ~10–15% into mid-Dec (Skyscanner 2026 pattern, Nov C$907 → Dec C$934).
- **Weather:** 0–7 °C, wet in the NW, snow likely above 1,000 m in the Black Forest; sunset ~16:30. Markets are best 16:00–20:00.
