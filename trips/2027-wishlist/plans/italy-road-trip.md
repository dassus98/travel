# Italy Road Trip 2027 — Emilia-Romagna → Tuscany → Umbria (13 nights, automatic car)

Researched 2026-09-12. All URLs accessed 2026-09-12. FX used: **€1 = C$1.58 (est.)**, US$1 = C$1.37 (est.). "(est.)" = my estimate, not a quoted price. Chitra: solo, automatic-only + IDP, mildly lactose-intolerant (LI notes inline).

## 1. WHEN + WHY

**Dates: depart YYZ Wed 6 Oct 2027 → land FCO Thu 7 Oct → fly home from FCO Wed 20 Oct 2027. 13 nights in Italy (14 away).**

Why mid-October beats May and late September:
- **Harvest stack.** Sangiovese vendemmia runs late Sept–early Oct; white truffle season and Alba-type fairs open mid-Oct; olive harvest/olio nuovo peaks late Oct–Nov ([italyplanner.com](https://italyplanner.com/italy-autumn-fall-guide)). 7–20 Oct catches the tail of the grape harvest, the first truffles, and the first olive picking in Umbria — May has none of these.
- **Siena Duomo floor uncovered.** 2026 autumn window is 18 Aug–15 Nov (ticket €18 vs €16) ([operaduomo.siena.it](https://operaduomo.siena.it/news/il-sommo-bene-scopertura-del-pavimento-del-duomo-di-siena-2026/), [earthtrekkers.com](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/how-to-visit-the-siena-cathedral/)); some years it ends mid-Oct, so 14 Oct is safe in either pattern. Confirm 2027 dates when Opera del Duomo publishes (usually Jan–Feb).
- **Crowds ~40% of summer peak, 15–20 °C walking temps** ([italyplanner.com](https://italyplanner.com/italy-autumn-fall-guide)); Monte Subasio is "unbearably hot in the summer months" ([outdooractive.com](https://www.outdooractive.com/en/route/hiking-trail/assisano/monte-subasio-1-290-m-in-the-footsteps-of-st-francis-of-assisi/13061112/)).
- **Eurochocolate is NOT mid-October any more:** 2026 edition is 20–29 Nov ([dolcevia.com](https://www.dolcevia.com/en/events/italy/eurochocolate-in-perugia/)); expect similar in 2027. Not worth chasing — Perugia is better without it (more space, no chocolate-crowd pricing).
- **Flights bottom in October**: AC shows Oct 2026 as the cheapest month YYZ–FCO (C$809 RT) vs Sept C$908 ([aircanada.com](https://www.aircanada.com/en-ca/flights-from-toronto-to-rome)).

**Why 13 nights:** 3 (Bologna, no car) + 2 (Fiesole) + 2 (Chianti) + 3 (Val d'Orcia) + 2 (Assisi) + 1 (Orvieto). Five hikes, two museum blocks, one class, one food-tour day, and only five driving days over 1 h. Fewer nights forces dropping Umbria or the Val d'Orcia hikes; more adds nights without new anchors.

**±1 week delta:** −1 week (30 Sep–13 Oct): vendemmia still visible in Chianti, warmer, no truffles. +1 week (13–27 Oct): first olio nuovo in Umbria, Sagra del Tordo Montalcino is the *last weekend of October* (2027: 29–31 Oct — just outside even the +1 week) ([sagradeltordo.it](https://sagradeltordo.it/en/)); floor may be re-covered; 30% more rain risk on Amiata/Subasio (est.).

**Rental car (Sun 10 Oct BLQ → Wed 20 Oct FCO, 10 days):**
- Company: **Sixt BLQ** — explicitly "one of the few rental agencies that offers automatic transmission cars" at BLQ; open 08:00–23:59; compact from US$30/day, average US$39/day (manual) ([kayak.com](https://www.kayak.com/Bologna-Guglielmo-Marconi-Airport-Car-Rentals.BLQ.cap.ksp), [kayak.com/Sixt](https://www.kayak.com/Sixt-Car-Rentals-Bologna.BLQ.ASIXT.cla.ksp)). Deposit from €300 ([carsrentalitaly.com](https://www.carsrentalitaly.com/bologna-airport/sixt)). Hertz/Avis/Maggiore also at BLQ; compare on AutoEurope.
- **Automatic category:** book "Compact automatic" (VW Golf / Toyota Corolla class). **Lead time: automatics are ~10–15% of Italian fleets; book by March 2027 or you get upsold to a premium automatic.** Budget **€65–80/day (est.)** for an automatic in October + **zero-excess (Sixt "Loss Damage Waiver + Super Cover") €20–30/day (est.)** → **≈€950–1,100 total (est.)**. Lean lever: decline supplier cover and rely on a credit card CDW (check the card excludes nothing in Italy) — but supplier zero-excess is the no-argument option for a solo driver.
- **One-way BLQ→FCO:** same-country drops are often €0 but "may be zero, or $100+" ([ricksteves.com forum](https://community.ricksteves.com/travel-forum/italy/one-way-car-rental-dropoff-charges)); budget **€60 (est.)** and check the quote line.
- **Tolls:** ≈€9 per 100 km ([tolls.eu](https://www.tolls.eu/italy)). This route avoids most autostrada (Futa pass, Chianti, Val d'Orcia, E45 are free). Only Orvieto→FCO A1 ≈ €10. Total **≈€15–25**. Pay by card at booths; take the white "carte" lane.
- **Fuel:** petrol €2.08/L (11 Sep 2026) ([travelinformation.eu](https://travelinformation.eu/italy/fuel-prices/)); ~950 km × 6 L/100 km ≈ **€120**. Fill at supermarket stations (8–12 c cheaper).
- **Winter tyres:** legal obligation on many roads 15 Nov–15 Apr; not needed 10–20 Oct.
- **ZTL rules (rental fines arrive 60–90 days later + €20–60 admin fee):**
  - Florence: Mon–Fri 07:30–20:00, Sat 07:30–16:00; €80–163, repeat up to €335 ([florence-trip.com](https://www.florence-trip.com/guides/florence-ztl-explained/)); "highest-volume fine generator in Italy for foreigners" ([travelinformation.eu](https://travelinformation.eu/journal/italy-ztl-zones-guide/)). **Never drive into Florence** — park in Fiesole and take bus 7.
  - Bologna: permanent ZTL 07:00–20:00 daily + "T-days" pedestrian core (est. hours; verify at comune.bologna.it). Irrelevant: no car in Bologna, airport is outside.
  - Siena: "one of the strictest 24/7 ZTLs in Italy" ([travelinformation.eu](https://travelinformation.eu/journal/italy-ztl-zones-guide/)). Park **Stadio-Fortezza €2/h, €26/day** or San Francesco (escalators) €2/h, €35/day ([sigericospa.it](https://sigericospa.it/en/parking-fees/)).
  - Perugia: most of the centre ZTL is **24 h**; park **Pian di Massiano (free) + Minimetrò €1.50** or SABA Piazza Partigiani €1.50/h, €7.75/24 h ([auto-jardim.com](https://auto-jardim.com/parking/italy-perugia/)).
  - Assisi: camera ZTL, walled centre; €83–160; park Saba Giovanni Paolo II (Basilica), Porta Nuova, or Mojano (covered escalators). Hotels inside can register your plate for bag drop ([tyremap.com](https://tyremap.com/city/assisi/)).
  - Orvieto: partial ZTL; Campo della Fiera €1.50/h with free lift 07:00–02:00; Piazza della Pace free + funicular ([orvietoviva.com](https://www.orvietoviva.com/en/orvieto-parking/)); Hotel Posta sits outside the ZTL ([hotelpostaorvieto.it](https://www.hotelpostaorvieto.it/en/how-to-get-here/)).
- **Bologna local:** Marconi Express BLQ↔Centrale ~€11 (est.); city walkable. Modena by regional train 20–30 min, €4–5.

## 5. DAY-BY-DAY

Sunrise ~07:25, sunset ~18:35 mid-Oct. Mondays: Uffizi/Accademia closed. No Italian public holidays in window.

- **D0 Wed 6 Oct** — AC890 20:00.
- **D1 Thu 7 Oct — Bologna.** FCO→Termini→Bologna (arrive ~15:30). Anchor: Piazza Maggiore, Quadrilatero, Asinelli tower (book slot). Food: **Osteria dell'Orsa** (tagliatelle al ragù ~€12, LI: ragù is dairy-free; skip tortellini in cream). Fallback: Archiginnasio anatomical theatre (€3).
- **D2 Fri 8 Oct — Food Valley day.** **Italian Days Food Experience**, €159, 07:00–17:00, Mon–Sat, max 27: Parmigiano dairy at cooking/moulding, acetaia (12/25-yr balsamic), prosciutto, family lunch; lactose-free option with 3 days' notice ([italiandays.it](https://www.italiandays.it/tour-item/italian-days-food-experience-bologna-day-tour/)). LI note: 24–36-month Parmigiano is virtually lactose-free; the fresh curd tasting is not. LEAN alt: train to Modena (€4.50), Mercato Albinelli, Acetaia Giusti visit (€15–25 est.), Duomo. BEST alt: private Parma tour from $423 ([bolognaitalyfoodtours.com](https://bolognaitalyfoodtours.com/tours/food-tour-from-bologna-parmigiano-balsamic-red-wine-lunch)).
- **D3 Sat 9 Oct — Bologna + class.** Morning: Portico di San Luca walk (3.8 km, 666 arches, UNESCO). Free walking tour 11:00 (Piazza Maggiore, tip-based; Sat runs). Afternoon **cooking class**: Taste Bologna €115, 3 h, max 12, tagliatelle/tortelloni/tortellini in brodo, non-refundable inside 48 h ([tastebologna.net](https://www.tastebologna.net/cooking-class)); or Cesarine tortellino class €140, 3 h, max 10, in a home ([cesarine.com](https://cesarine.com/en/experiences/tortellino-the-king-of-bolognese-cuisine-85xdnXLjvG)). LI: tortelloni filling is ricotta — eat the tortellini in brodo instead.
- **D4 Sun 10 Oct — Car → Passo della Futa → Fiesole (~120 km, 2.5 h).** Marconi Express to BLQ, Sixt 09:00. SR65 over the Futa (903 m) — German war cemetery, chestnut woods; lunch at Passo della Futa or Barberino. Sunday = Florence ZTL off, but still park in Fiesole. Evening: Fiesole Roman theatre & sunset from San Francesco. Fallback: Mugello via A1 if fog.
- **D5 Mon 11 Oct — Monte Ceceri hike + Florence.** **Hike 1: Fiesole–Monte Ceceri–Cave Braschi loop**, ~5 km, ~200 m, 1.5–2 h, easy, Leonardo's flight site + quarries; extend on Via degli Dei toward Settignano to ~10 km ([alltrails.com](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/italy/tuscany/monte-ceceri-cava-fratelli-braschi-via-fiesole)). Bus 7 down: Duomo exterior/Baptistery, Santa Croce (open Mon), San Miniato at vespers. Food: **Trattoria Mario** (lunch only, closed Sun; ribollita €8, bistecca by weight), **All'Antico Vinaio** schiacciata €7–10 (ask no cheese).
- **D6 Tue 12 Oct — Uffizi + Accademia → Chianti (45 min).** Uffizi 08:15 slot, €29 pre-booked (€25 same-day), closed Mon ([uffizi.it](https://www.uffizi.it/en/tickets)); Accademia €16 + €4 booking ([galleriaaccademiafirenze.it](https://www.galleriaaccademiafirenze.it/en/tickets/)). BEST: licensed guide 2.5–3 h, €180–260 (est.) via Context Travel/Florence guide association — book 4–6 weeks out. Drive SR222 Chiantigiana via Greve (Antica Macelleria Falorni, Sat market). Dinner: agriturismo table.
- **D7 Wed 13 Oct — Hike 2: Radda–Castelvecchi–Volpaia loop**, 14.2 km, 654 m, 4.5–6 h, moderate, from Radda car park; winery stops at Castelvecchi and Volpaia ([10adventures.com](https://www.10adventures.com/hikes/tuscany/radda-loop/)). Lunch **Bar-Ucci**, Volpaia (crostini, €10–15). Vendemmia tail: ask the host which cantina is still crushing.
- **D8 Thu 14 Oct — Siena → Val d'Orcia (60 km).** Park Stadio (€2/h). **Opa Si Pass €18 (floor uncovered)**, Duomo + Piccolomini Library + Crypt + Museo + Facciatone; Porta del Cielo €23 for rooftop ([earthtrekkers.com](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/how-to-visit-the-siena-cathedral/)); hours 10–19. Guided: Opera guide slot for the floor, ~€8 (est.). Lunch: **Osteria Le Logge** or pici cacio e pepe substitute → **pici all'aglione** (dairy-free). Drive SR2 Cassia to San Quirico; dinner Il Rigo €40.
- **D9 Fri 15 Oct — Hike 3: Pienza–Monticchiello loop**, 12.4 km, 310 m, ~3.5 h, moderate, from Monticchiello ([komoot.com](https://www.komoot.com/smarttour/1017465)). Pienza: pecorino (aged = low lactose), Pieve di Corsignano. Sunset at Bagno Vignoni thermal pool / Parco dei Mulini. Fallback: Montalcino Brunello tasting.
- **D10 Sat 16 Oct — Hike 4: Monte Amiata summit** — Prato delle Macinaie→Vetta 1,738 m, 7 km RT ([lavia.cc](https://lavia.cc/gpx/tracce/vetta-del-monte-amiata-prato-delle-macinaie/)); or a 12–15 km section of the 27 km Anello (1,000–1,300 m, "undemanding" elevation, autumn = foliage, mushrooms, chestnuts) ([visittuscany.com](https://www.visittuscany.com/en/itineraries/loop-monte-amiata/)). Beech forest, first chestnut sagre. Fallback (rain): Abbazia di Sant'Antimo + Montalcino. Optional BEST: **Il Rigo cooking class** (10:30–14:30, 4–8 pax, price on request ~€120–150 est.) instead of Amiata.
- **D11 Sun 17 Oct — Montepulciano → Perugia → Assisi (110 km).** Montepulciano Sunday morning (Palazzo Comunale tower, Cantina de' Ricci). Perugia: park Pian di Massiano, Minimetrò €1.50; Galleria Nazionale dell'Umbria (Perugino, Piero), Rocca Paolina escalators, Corso Vannucci passeggiata. Perugina chocolate shop instead of Eurochocolate. Assisi by 18:00.
- **D12 Mon 18 Oct — Hike 5: Monte Subasio**, 16.7 km, 900 m, ~6 h, moderate: Matteotti car park → path 50 → Eremo delle Carceri → path 60 → summit 1,290 m ([outdooractive.com](https://www.outdooractive.com/en/route/hiking-trail/assisano/monte-subasio-1-290-m-in-the-footsteps-of-st-francis-of-assisi/13061112/)). No guide needed; carry 2 L water. Evening: Basilica lower church at vespers (free; quieter than day).
- **D13 Tue 19 Oct — Assisi → Spello → Orvieto (120 km).** Basilica di San Francesco 08:30 (free; Giotto cycle; guide via the Sanctuary's own service, ~€10 pp est.). Spello: Villa dei Mosaici (€6–8 est.), Pinturicchio chapel, first olive harvest in the groves below — buy olio nuovo if a frantoio is pressing. Orvieto by 15:00: Duomo (Signorelli chapel), **Orvieto Underground €8, ~1 h, tickets Piazza Duomo 23** ([tripadvisor.com](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g194843-d3664573-Reviews-Orvieto_Underground-Orvieto_Province_of_Terni_Umbria.html)); Pozzo di San Patrizio. Splurge: **Trattoria La Palomba** (umbrichelli al tartufo — first white truffles).
- **D14 Wed 20 Oct — Orvieto → FCO (1h20, A1 ≈€10). Drop car 09:30, AC891 home.**

Free walking tours: Bologna (daily 11:00), Florence (multiple daily), Perugia/Assisi (weekend only, est.) — tip €10–15.

## 6. FOOD SHORTLIST (price pp, LI = lactose notes)

1. **Osteria dell'Orsa**, Bologna — tagliatelle al ragù, €15–20. LI: ragù fine; skip tortelloni burro e salvia. No reservations.
2. **Mercato delle Erbe / Quadrilatero**, Bologna — mortadella, crescentine, €8–12. LI: ask "senza formaggio".

4. **Trattoria Mario**, Florence — lunch only, ribollita/bistecca, €20–35. LI: ribollita is dairy-free. Queue at 11:45.
5. **Bar-Ucci**, Volpaia — crostini, Chianti by glass, €12–18.
6. **Osteria Le Logge**, Siena — pici, guinea fowl, €50–60. Book 1 week out.
7. **Il Rigo table**, San Quirico — €40 4-course, rotating; tell them LI at booking (they cook to order).
8. **La Terrazza del Chiostro**, Pienza — modern Tuscan, €60–80 (BEST splurge). Book.
9. **Osteria Piazzetta dell'Erba**, Assisi — modern Umbrian, torta al testo, €35–45. Book.
10. **Trattoria La Palomba**, Orvieto — umbrichelli al tartufo, €35–45 (truffle supplement). Closed Wed; book.
All prices est. from 2025–26 menus; fine-dining Tuscan menus rarely list lactose, so say "sono intollerante al lattosio" — kitchens handle it well.
