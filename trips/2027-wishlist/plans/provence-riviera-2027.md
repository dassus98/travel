## 1. WHEN + WHY

**Depart YYZ Fri 18 June 2027 (overnight) → arrive Sat 19 June → fly home NCE Sat 3 July 11:10 → YYZ 14:30. 14 nights.**

Why this window:
- **Air Canada's new YYZ–NCE nonstop starts 16 June 2027, Wed + Sat only** (A330-300; NCE→YYZ 11:10→14:30) — so ending in Nice on a Saturday gives a nonstop home. Source: https://upgradedpoints.com/news/air-canada-adds-summer-2027-flights-toronto/
- **Cannes (11–22 May 2027) is over**; Riviera prices normalise by June. https://www.screendaily.com/news/cannes-film-festival-confirms-2027-dates-for-80th-edition/5217448.article
- **Lavender**: Sénanque/Luberon blooms late June; Valensole is at/near peak the last week of June — hit both en route to the Verdon on 25 June.
- **Calanques fire-closure season starts 1 June** but the coastal massif "went red only 3 times through mid-2026"; late June is well before peak-heat closures. https://calanquesnationalpark.org/closures-and-reservations
- **Blanc-Martel shuttle** is Fri–Sun only until 28 June, daily from 29 June — the Verdon hike is placed on **Fri 25 June**. https://liveverdon.com/en/pratique/navettes-parkings/
- French school holidays and the Avignon/Aix festivals (both "July 2027, dates TBC") start after we leave the Rhône valley — crowds and prices are lower than July. https://84.agendaculturel.fr/festival/festival-d-avignon.html
- **Fête de la Musique, Mon 21 June** — free concerts everywhere; we're in Avignon that night.
- Pentecost Monday (17 May) is irrelevant to this window.

**Length**: 14 nights = 5 bases, 3 big hikes, 2 rest-pace cities, no single-night stops. 12 nights would drop Aix or the Verdon; 15+ would only add beach days.

**±1 week delta**: 
- One week earlier (12–26 June): AC nonstop not yet running (fly via CDG both ways, +C$0 but +3 h), Verdon shuttle only Fri–Sun, lavender barely open in Luberon, Valensole not yet purple. Lodging ~10 % cheaper (est.).
- One week later (26 June–10 July): Verdon shuttle daily, lavender peak, but Avignon Festival opens (~5 July, TBC) — Avignon lodging doubles and sells out, Aix Festival begins, French holidays start ~3 July, Calanques red-day risk rises. Nice/Riviera hotels +15–25 % (est.).

## 6. FOOD SHORTLIST

1. **Les Halles d'Avignon** — covered market, Tue–Sun to 13:30; €10–20; oysters, tapenade, truffle stand; dairy easy to avoid. No booking.
2. **Pollen, Avignon** — modern, ~€60 tasting; note dairy in booking form. Book 3 wks.
3. **Chardon, Arles** — modern bistro, ~€40; ask about butter sauces. Book 1 wk.
4. **Le Fournil, Bonnieux** — Provençal classic in troglodyte room, ~€45; lamb, ratatouille. Book 2 wks.
5. **La Bastide de Capelongue, Bonnieux** (splurge) — Michelin 1★, €120–160; tell them lactose. 6 wks.
6. **Roy René calissons, Aix** — almond/melon sweets, dairy-free; €8/box.
7. **Chez Fonfon, Marseille** — bouillabaisse €80 pp; order 48 h ahead; rouille = garlic/oil, safe.
8. **Noailles market + Chez Yassine, Marseille** — Tunisian street food, €10; dairy-free.
9. **Chez Pipo, Nice** — socca €4, pissaladière; cash-ish, queue. No booking.
10. **La Merenda, Nice** — Niçois classics ~€35; book in person same day; stockfish, daube (no dairy); avoid the gnocchi's butter.
Dairy notes: Provençal cooking is olive-oil based; main dairy traps are butter sauces in bistros, aïoli is egg/oil (safe), goat cheese (Banon) is common at breakfast — most B&Bs will do fruit/bread instead if asked in advance.

## 9. RISKS

1. **New AC NCE route slips or is cancelled before launch** — book on AC so re-accommodation is free; fallback NCE→CDG same day (AF hourly) or plan B TGV. Buy travel insurance with "schedule change" cover.
2. **Fire-risk red days close the Calanques / Sainte-Victoire (1 Jun–30 Sep)** — binary open/closed, published 17:00–18:00 the day before (https://calanquesnationalpark.org/closures-and-reservations). Both hike days have swap-in urban alternatives; keep Cassis as a day trip so no lodging is stranded.
3. **Entry systems**: **EES live since Apr 2026** — biometric kiosks at CDG causing long queues; take the later TGV. **ETIAS postponed to 2027** (https://openjaw.com/newsroom/travel-alerts/2026/07/07/just-in-eu-delays-etias-launch-until-next-year/); if it goes live before June, apply online (~€20, 3-yr validity) ≥1 week before; a grace period is expected at launch. Canadians: no visa, 90/180 rule.
4. **Heat / mistral**: late-June heatwaves are now routine (35 °C+); start hikes by 07:00, Blanc-Martel needs 3 L water and there is no water on the trail; mistral days make the Rhône valley cold and the Verdon ladders exposed — the Luberon block is flexible so re-order D5–D7 by forecast.
5. **Lodging quality drift** (Le Limas ownership change, Villa Saint Exupéry cleanliness, The People bed-bug report) — book free-cancellation rates, re-read the last 30 days of reviews 4 weeks out, and keep a backup per city (Avignon: Hôtel de l'Horloge; Nice: Hôtel Nice Excelsior; Marseille: Hôtel Hermès).
Also: solo driving — Luberon lanes are narrow but low-risk; no night driving planned; Aix drop-off before dark.
