## 1. WHEN + WHY

**Tue 5 Oct 2027 (dep YYZ) → Wed 20 Oct 2027 (dep BER) = 14 nights in Germany.**

- Oktoberfest 2027 runs Sat 18 Sep–Sun 3 Oct (ends first Sunday of Oct = Unity Day, so no extension). Arriving Wed 6 Oct means Munich hotel rates have dropped ~40–60 % from Wiesn levels (est.) and beer halls are back to locals.
- Sun 3 Oct is German Unity Day — we skip it entirely (museums open but shops closed, trains packed on the long weekend).
- Alpine lifts: Zugspitze/Garmisch-Classic/Wank/Eckbauer autumn maintenance began **10 Nov** in 2025 (Kranzberg 21 Oct) — pattern implies everything is running on 9–12 Oct 2027 ([zugspitzland.de PDF](https://www.zugspitzland.de/fileadmin/Mediendatenbank/ZugspitzLand/Urlaubsplanung/Aktuelles/Revision_Bergbahnen_Herbst_2025.pdf), 2026-09-12). Eagle's Nest road "closes between mid and end of October" ([kehlsteinhaus.axess.shop FAQ](https://kehlsteinhaus.axess.shop/en/document/custom/2402), 2026-09-12). Königssee **summer boat timetable ends 11 Oct 2026** ([seenschifffahrt.de](https://www.seenschifffahrt.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Fahrplaene/KS/KS_Timetable_summer.pdf)) — 2027 cutover ~10 Oct, which is why the alpine block sits 9–12 Oct.
- Larch/beech colour peaks 5–20 Oct in the Alps and Saxon Switzerland; daylight ~11 h; typical highs 12–16 °C valley, 0–5 °C on Zugspitze; first snow possible above 2,000 m.
- **Pergamonmuseum reopens 4 Jun 2027** (North Wing + Pergamon Altar) — the trip lands four months after reopening ([smb.museum](https://www.smb.museum/en/whats-new/detail/the-pergamonmuseum-returns-opening-on-june-4th-2027/), 2026-09-12). Expect timed tickets and crowds; book the moment 2027 individual sales open.
- 14 nights, not 15: 3 Munich + 3 alps + 2 Nuremberg + 2 Dresden + 4 Berlin covers every anchor with one rest morning. Bamberg and Potsdam are day trips, not stops. A 15th night adds nothing except cost.
- **±1 week:** a week earlier (28 Sep) collides with Oktoberfest's last weekend + Unity Day — Munich lodging 2–3×, trains full. A week later (12–27 Oct) risks Eagle's Nest closed, Königssee on winter timetable, Neuschwanstein winter hours (10–16 from ~16 Oct), Sachsenhausen winter hours from 27 Oct, and the Air Transat BER–YYZ season end (23 Oct 2026 pattern). Chosen week is the sweet spot.

## 9. RISKS

1. **ETIAS/EES.** EES (biometric entry) rolled out from Oct 2025; ETIAS (€20, 3-yr, Canadians) expected in late 2026 — official page could not be fetched (JS-only). Apply ≥1 month before departure; expect longer MUC arrival queues (est. +30 min).
2. **Air Transat BER–YYZ season may end before 20 Oct** or route may be cut. Mitigation: don't book AC outbound until Transat's 2027 schedule is confirmed; fallback LH/AC via FRA same day (+C$150–250).
3. **Alpine weather / lift closures.** Snow above 2,000 m is normal in mid-Oct; Zugspitze summit closes in high wind. Mitigation: flexible D5/D6 order, Zugspitze tickets bought 1–2 days out after checking the webcam; Wank/Partnach as low fallbacks. Autumn Revision dates for 2027 not published — check zugspitze.de Revision page in Sep 2027.
4. **Sell-outs:** Neuschwanstein (same-day tickets rarely available in Oct), Historisches Grünes Gewölbe (capacity-capped), Pergamon reopening crowds. Mitigation: dated timeline above; Neuschwanstein has free cancellation to 24 h so book early and adjust.
5. **Crowds/noise at social hostels on Fri–Sat (Dresden Neustadt, Berlin Mitte).** Mitigation: courtyard/upper-floor rooms requested at booking; BEST column removes the issue. DB delays on ICE legs (2025 punctuality ~62 %): take a train ≥1 h earlier than any timed ticket needs, and use Sparpreis (not Super) on the Dresden day so a missed Grünes Gewölbe slot doesn't cascade.
