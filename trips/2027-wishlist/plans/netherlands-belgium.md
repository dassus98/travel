# Netherlands + Belgium by Train — 12 nights, 11–24 April 2027

## 1. WHEN + WHY

**Chosen: depart YYZ Sun 11 Apr 2027, arrive AMS Mon 12 Apr, fly home BRU Sat 24 Apr. 12 nights.**

Why mid-April beats late May and September for THIS trip:
- **Tulips.** Keukenhof runs 18 Mar–9 May 2027, 08:00–19:00 ([keukenhof.nl](https://keukenhof.nl/en/)); optimal bloom is roughly 8–22 April, weather-dependent ([wanderingclogs](https://thewanderingclogs.com/keukenhof-2027-guide/)). Late May and September have no bulb fields at all.
- **Airfare.** Air Canada's own fare grid shows YYZ–AMS from **C$827 in April 2027 vs C$1,332 in May and C$738 in Sept/Oct** ([aircanada.com](https://www.aircanada.com/en-ca/flights-from-toronto-to-amsterdam)). April is the last cheap month before summer pricing.
- **Crowds.** King's Day (Tue 27 Apr) is a city-wide street party with limited public transport, no lockers at Centraal, canal cruises stopping at 12:15 ([holland-explorer](https://holland-explorer.com/kings-day-faq/)). Chitra dislikes crowds, so the plan **ends 3 days before King's Night (26 Apr)** and Keukenhof is done on a Tuesday before 09:00, ahead of the 10:00–10:30 coach wave.
- **Weather.** April: 8–14 °C, showers, long light (sunrise ~06:45, sunset ~20:45). Hike days need a shell; fine for dunes and Ardennes.
- **Holidays/closures.** Easter is 28 Mar 2027, before the trip. Mondays: Mauritshuis opens 13:00 ([holland-explorer](https://holland-explorer.com/mauritshuis/)), Antwerp museums and Magritte/Horta closed Mon — the route puts Antwerp on Sat/Sun and Brussels on Wed–Fri to dodge them.

**±1 week delta.** One week earlier (4–17 Apr): bloom slightly less full, same fares, tail of Easter school holidays. One week later (18 Apr–1 May): crowds build toward King's Day, Bloemencorso parade (~Sat 24 Apr, the bulb region's busiest day), and you collide with King's Day. Late May: fares +60%, no tulips. September (C$738): cheapest and calm, no tulips; Mauritshuis closed 24 Aug–20 Sep 2026 for a Vermeer loan — the Girl can travel.

**Why 12 nights.** Six stops with museum programmes need ≥2 nights each except Delft/Rotterdam (1 each, 12 min apart). A 13th night would only add a second Ardennes day; 10 nights forces cutting Ghent/Bruges or the hike.

## 9. RISKS

1. **Anne Frank House sells out in minutes.** Mitigation: exact release time above; second attempt for week 19–25 Apr is Tue 9 Mar but you'd need to be in Amsterdam — so if you miss it, take the Anne Frank walking tour (Jordaan) and Rembrandthuis instead.
2. **Tulip timing.** Cold spring → bloom slips to late April; warm → early. Keukenhof's greenhouse pavilions guarantee flowers regardless; the fields on the Express bus route are the variable. Don't shift the trip for it.
3. **ETIAS/EES rollout confusion** — launch dates have slipped repeatedly; long EES kiosk queues reported at first-entry airports. Mitigation: check status monthly from Dec 2026; arrive with ETIAS if live; AMS arrival at 11:00 avoids the 06:00 bank of transatlantic arrivals.
4. **Rubenshuis may still be closed** in April 2027 (only "2027" is promised). Mitigation: Plantin-Moretus + cathedral Rubens altarpieces cover Antwerp baroque.
5. **Ardennes public transport** — TEC bus 8 Libramont–Bouillon is infrequent. Mitigation: verify timetable 1 week out, carry a taxi number (Bouillon–Libramont ~€45, est.), keep Sonian Forest as the no-risk substitute. After heavy rain the Semois riverside path floods; the Rocher-des-Gattes ridge stays dry.

Dairy: cheese, butter and cream are in most traditional dishes; stoofvlees, frites, mussels, Indonesian/Surinamese and Vietnamese are the reliable lanes. Bring lactase; "lactosevrij" (NL/Flanders), "sans lactose" (Brussels).
