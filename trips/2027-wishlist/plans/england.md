# England — London · Salisbury/Stonehenge · Bath · Cotswolds · Oxford, Wed 8 Sep – Tue 21 Sep 2027 (12 nights, 11 full days)

Researched 2026-09-12 (web only; booking sites blocked, so lodging rates are "(est.)" from 2026 shoulder-season norms). FX: £1 = C$1.87 (x-rates, 2026-01-01 — re-check at booking). All prices pp. Everything below is *prepare*; you book.

## 1. When + why

**Mid-September beats late May.** Late May 2027 collides with the Spring bank holiday (Mon 31 May) and English school half-term (~29 May–6 Jun) — Cotswolds/Bath at peak family crowds, plus Chelsea Flower Show pushing London hotel rates (2027 England bank holidays: 3 May, 31 May, 30 Aug — [ukpublicholidays.co.uk](https://ukpublicholidays.co.uk/uk-bank-holidays-2027/), acc. 2026-09-12). Mid-September has no holidays, schools are back (11 Sep), heritage sites are on full summer hours, the Stone Circle Experience still runs (none Oct–Nov — [English Heritage](https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/visit/places/stonehenge/things-to-do/stone-circle-access-visits/)), and **Open House London** (free access to ~800 normally closed buildings) ran 12–20 Sep 2026 — expect ~11–19 Sep 2027 (est.) ([openhouse.org.uk](https://programme.openhouse.org.uk/)). Trade-off: daylight ~13 h (sunset ~19:30 vs 21:00 in May) and cooler (14–20 °C, ~25% rain days); nothing on this route needs long evenings.

**12 nights**: 5 London, 1 Salisbury (the evening Stone Circle session makes a same-night hop to Bath impractical), 2 Bath, 2 Cotswolds (hike day + village day), 2 Oxford. 10 nights = drop Salisbury + one London night, ~C$350 cheaper but loses inner-circle Stonehenge.

**±1 week**: 1–14 Sep — warmer, pricier flights, misses Open House. 15–28 Sep — cheaper flights, same crowds; fine as Plan B.

## 9. Risks

1. **Stone Circle Experience sold out / cancelled by weather** — evening sessions go first; book at release, have the standard £30 (est.) daytime ticket as a fallback and keep the Salisbury night either way. EH refunds if they cancel.
2. **Rail strikes / engineering works** (GWR Sunday works on the Cotswold Line are common; Sun 19 Moreton→Oxford is the exposed leg) — check National Rail 4 weeks out; fallback is bus 801 to Cheltenham + train, or the S3/Stagecoach. Advance tickets are refunded on cancelled trains.
3. **Lodging quality gaps in the Cotswolds** (no hostel; inns are old buildings — creaky floors, uneven plumbing) — book rooms with recent-refurb mentions, request a quiet room off the High Street (A429 lorry traffic through Moreton/Stow).
4. **Windowless/tiny rooms in London budget hotels** (Z, Point A) — explicitly choose "outside/window" room categories; YHA private en-suites all have windows.
5. **Entry/ID**: UK ETA must be approved before boarding (apply ≥ 1 week out, official app only); passport valid for the stay (no 6-month rule for UK). Driving: left-hand traffic, automatic scarcity — if no automatic is confirmed by May, drop the car and go bus.
