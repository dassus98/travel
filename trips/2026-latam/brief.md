# Trip brief — Latin America, solo, two weeks, Nov–Dec 2026

Status: FROZEN 2026-09-10 (destination open — decided among `plans/*.md`)

## Who
- Chitra, solo. Basic Spanish (order, ask directions).

## When
- Two continuous weeks, Saturday out → Sunday back (15 nights, 10 PTO days — all of 2026's allowance; confirm they're available). Dates float; nothing anchored to an event.
- Windows researched: Oaxaca + CDMX **7–22 Nov** (post-Día de Muertos, deliberately); Bajío corridor + CDMX **14–29 Nov**; Colombia **28 Nov – 13 Dec** (Alumbrado lights, Día de las Velitas).

## What THIS trip is for
- Vibe: cultural experiences first — history, food, everyday life; day hikes (4–6 h, moderate, no multi-day treks); meet people.
- Must-haves: own room every night; high-quality lodging (no insects, no bad plumbing, hot water, wifi) in social places — well-run hostels with privates, boutique hostels, guesthouses with a host. **No Airbnb.**
- Spend less on housing, more on culture, history, food and guides.
- Transport: safest method every time — daytime executive buses on main corridors, domestic flights where the road is risky, app cars never street taxis.
- Pace: balanced.

## Constraints
- Budget: C$2,000 soft target; **C$2,500 ceiling** for the maximized version.
- Dietary: mildly lactose-intolerant.
- Dislikes: crowds, dirt, rudeness, disorganization; areas with gang or robbery risk.

## Findings that changed the picture (see plans for sources)
1. **None of the three reaches C$2,000, and C$2,500 needs cuts.** With your own room for 15 nights and C$700–1,150 of air, the lean versions land at C$3,000–3,300 with 10% contingency; the "best" versions at C$4,300–5,700. Each plan lists the levers to ~C$2,550–2,650 (fewer nights, cheapest-tier privates, ultra-low-cost fares, no guided hike).
2. **Safe transport costs money**: fly MEX↔OAX (ADO buses on the Oaxaca autopista were attacked at night on 30–31 Aug 2026); fly MDE→PEI and PEI→CTG; fly into BJX rather than bus through the Celaya–Irapuato corridor under Canada's "avoid non-essential travel" line.
3. **Lodging screen** rejected several popular names: Casa Pepe (bed bugs, sewage smell), Hotel Casa de Sierra Azul (plumbing), Hostal de las Américas (dirty), Selina SMA (no water), Punto 79 (ants, rodents), Blue Bicycle (cleanliness 6.0), Rango and Purple Monkey (noise, roaches), República (no hot water), La Serrana (mould, no hot water). Picks with clean records are in each plan.
4. Two windows have real calendar effects: Oaxaca's Día de Muertos (31 Oct–2 Nov) doubles lodging and is already booked out — avoided on purpose; Colombia's 8 Dec holiday tightens flights and Cartagena rooms — book early.
