## 8. Top risks

1. **Scopolamine / dating-app lures.** Medellín scopolamine robberies +89% y/y to Aug 2026, concentrated in Poblado; US Embassy logged 13 dating-app drugging reports; a foreigner was found dead in a Manila hotel in Aug 2026 ([medellin.co](https://medellin.co/blog/american-found-dead-manila-hotel-el-poblado-august-2026-traveler-safety/)); Canada cites "an increase in deaths related to online dating applications" in Medellín, Cartagena, Bogotá ([travel.gc.ca](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/colombia)). **Mitigate:** no dating apps in Colombia; never accept drinks, watch pours, never leave a glass; meet people through hostel events; if disoriented say "me drogaron", call 123, hospital before police.
2. **Express kidnapping / taxi robbery.** Canada: victims pulled from streets or taxis to ATMs, sometimes held overnight. **Mitigate:** never street-hail; Uber/Cabify/inDrive (verify plate, back seat); official airport taxis; ATMs inside banks by day only; second card at the hostel; Centro Medellín 10:00–17:00 only.
3. **Phone snatching (Lleras, Plaza Botero).** **Mitigate:** phone away on the street, cheap second phone for nights, cross-body bag, no jewellery; sleep in Manila/Laureles, not the Lleras blocks.
4. **Hike weather.** No altitude issue (Medellín 1,500 m; Cocora tops at 2,860 m — pace it). December is drier, but Cocora turns to deep mud after rain and afternoon fog kills views. **Mitigate:** 06:30 starts, poles, spare shoes, short-loop fallback; Arví on marked trails with a guide. Water: Medellín tap is potable; bottled/filtered in Salento and Cartagena.
5. **Crowds and the 8 Dec pinch.** Velitas (7–8 Dec) are the most crowded Alumbrado nights; 8 Dec is a holiday, so flights and Cartagena rooms tighten; Salento fills on weekends; Comuna 13 is "an amusement park" on weekends. **Mitigate:** lights on weeknights 1–3 Dec, Comuna 13 Monday 09:00, Cocora Monday, Velitas in Salento, book 8 Dec flights and the Cartagena block early.
