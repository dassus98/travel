# Mexico City + Oaxaca — Sat 7 Nov → Sun 22 Nov 2026 (solo)

Researched 2026-09-10 by web search only; every source accessed that day, listed as [S#] at the end. Rates used: **USD 1.36 CAD, MXN 0.075 CAD**. "(est.)" = my estimate, not sourced. Prepare only; you book.

**Calendar check.** Sat 7 → Sun 22 Nov is **15 nights**, not 14 — your 5+7+2 shape sums to 14. Plan below is 15 nights (CDMX 5 → Oaxaca 7 → CDMX 3); to keep 14, drop the last CDMX night and fly home Sat 21 (−C$45 lodging, −C$30 food, but you lose the Desierto hike day). Holidays: **Mon 16 Nov = Revolution Day (observed)** — banks/offices shut, INAH sites open, museums closed as every Monday. Museums closed Mondays 9 and 16 (Anthropology [S27], Santo Domingo cultural museum); archaeological sites open daily.

**Día de Muertos delta (31 Oct–15 Nov instead).** Oaxaca lodging runs **2–3× normal (US$150–250/night) 31 Oct–2 Nov and hotels fill by May** [S22]; by 10 Sep the hostels' private rooms are almost certainly gone and Casa Pepe/Casa Angel apply 7-day cancellation on those dates [S9]. Kayak's November average fare is ~US$662 vs ~US$400 in Oct [S3], so flights are similar either way. Realistic delta: **+C$400–700 and a high risk of no acceptable private room** — the 7 Nov window is the right call for someone who hates crowds.

## 1. Flights — Air Canada nonstop, book by mid-October

- **Out Sat 7 Nov: AC991 YYZ ~08:30 → MEX ~12:15** (737 MAX 8, daily, 4 h 45; June-data timings 08:16–08:39 dep, arrival shifts +1 h in November because Toronto is on EST) [S1][S2]. **Back Sun 22 Nov: AC990 MEX 11:55 → YYZ ~17:40** (est. from 4 h 45 block) [S2].
- Fare: Expedia shows AC round trips "from C$689" and one-ways from C$368 [S4]; Kayak's November average is ~US$662 ≈ C$900, cheapest ~10 weeks out, Friday out/Tuesday back cheapest [S3]. **Budget C$700 lean (AC Basic/Standard, book by ~10 Oct) / C$820 best.** Aeroméxico also flies nonstop (AM693) [S2]; Flair C$368 rt but a 00:25 return [S5]. Checked bag: AC Basic charges per bag; buy Standard if you'll bring a bag back full of Oaxacan textiles (est. C$70 each way otherwise).
- **Domestic legs (safest option): fly MEX↔OAX, 50 min.** Volaris MXN 900–1,800, VivaAerobus 800–1,500, Aeroméxico 1,200–2,500 with bag included [S6][S7]. Lean: Volaris + paid bag ≈ C$90/leg; best: Aeroméxico ≈ C$150/leg. Book a morning departure (≤10:00) on both legs.
- **Airport → Roma/Condesa at 12:15:** prepaid **authorized sitio taxi from the booth inside T1** (fixed zone fare, no meter; est. MXN 350–400 ≈ C$28) or Uber/DiDi from the signed pickup zone (cheaper, fare shown up front); 20–60 min; never accept a driver who approaches you [S8]. Oaxaca airport → centro: booth taxi MXN 200–280 [S7].

## 2. Route + safe transport

**CDMX 5 nights (Sat 7–Thu 12) → fly → Oaxaca 7 nights (Thu 12–Thu 19) → fly → CDMX 3 nights (Thu 19–Sun 22).**

- Canada (updated 9 Sep 2026): "exercise a high degree of caution"; use VIP/executive-class buses only, **avoid highway travel after dark**, prefer toll ("D") roads; Mexico City metro is crowded and a pickpocket hotspot; use only authorized taxis/apps [S10].
- **Bus vs flight, MEX→OAX:** ADO GL/Platino daytime coaches from TAPO, 600–900 MXN, 6–7 h, departures 06:00–14:00 [S6]. But Puebla press reports **two attacks on ADO buses in 24 h (30–31 Aug 2026)** on the Cuacnopalan–Oaxaca autopista — rocks and gunfire to force a stop, at night [S11]. Daytime GL bus is still within Canada's guidance; the **flight is safer, C$30–90 more, and saves a day**. Recommendation: fly both ways. Day bus = lean fallback only, TAPO departure ≤08:00.
- Oaxaca's blockade risk (CNTE teachers) is on Hwy 190/175 and peaks May–Sep; not violent toward tourists; flying bypasses it [S12].
- In town: walk, Uber/DiDi after dark; Oaxaca centro rated very safe for solo travellers into the evening [S12].

## 4. Day by day

Fees: INAH Category I sites (Teotihuacán, Anthropology, Chapultepec Castle, Templo Mayor, Monte Albán, Mitla) are **MXN 210 each in 2026**, ~double 2025 [S28][S29]. Hydrate; CDMX is 2,240 m [S10].

**Sat 7** — AC991, land ~12:15; sitio taxi to base. Roma/Condesa walk, Parque México. Dinner **Taquería Orinoco** (Roma, tacos est. MXN 30–40; "sin queso").

**Sun 8** — **Estación México free walking tour, Centro Histórico** (tip-based; check times) [S30], then **Templo Mayor** (MXN 210). Lunch **El Cardenal** (MXN 180–280, walk-in early) [S31]. Skip Anthropology today — Sundays are free for residents and "very crowded" [S27]. Evening: hostel lucha libre outing (Casa Pepe/Viajero organise them) [S9]. Fallback: Palacio de Bellas Artes.

**Mon 9 (museums closed)** — **Teotihuacán early with a guide**: small-group tours US$25–65 [S32]; lean DIY = Autobuses México–San Juan Teotihuacán from Terminal Norte, MXN 55–65, 50–60 min, from 07:00 [S32], hire a licensed guide at Gate 1 (est. MXN 600–800/group). Open 09–17, no climbing the Sun pyramid in 2026 [S32]. Lunch Mercado de Medellín comedor (est. MXN 120).

**Tue 10** — **Anthropology Museum 09:00** (Tue–Sun 09–18, MXN 210; 3–4 h; guided 2-h tours sold on GYG) [S27][S28], then **Chapultepec Castle** (MXN 210). Lunch **Contramar** (Roma, MXN 400–650, tuna tostadas; Tock opens 30 days ahead, walk-in waitlist works) [S31]. Fallback: Museo Tamayo.

**Wed 11 — HIKE 1: Iztaccíhuatl lower trails (guided).** Level 1: 8–9 km, +380 m, 3.5–4 h, start 3,600–3,970 m; 05:00–06:00 pickup; US$139 with private transport, park fee, certified guide, poles, insurance; min 2 people [S33]. Day 5 at altitude is the minimum acclimatisation (a Nevado reviewer got sick after 1 day) [S34]. Fallback if wiped out: Xochimilco.

**Thu 12** — Morning flight MEX→OAX. Check in Casa Angel; afternoon **Oaxaca free walking tour** (GuruWalk/Estación México/freewalkoaxaca) [S30]; Santo Domingo church. Dinner **Levadura de Olla** (mole negro MXN 240; Wed–Sat 13–21) [S35]. Fallback: Museo Textil.

**Fri 13** — **Monte Albán**: shuttle from Hotel Rivera del Ángel MXN 65 each way, every 30 min from 08:30; be there 08:30–10:00; MXN 210 incl. museum; **licensed guide at the gate MXN 500–700 per group** — join 2–3 others from the hostel [S36][S37]. Afternoon **Museo de las Culturas** in Santo Domingo (closed Mon). Lunch **Mercado 20 de Noviembre / Pasillo de Humo** (set lunch MXN 80–120; grilled tasajo — skip the quesillo) [S38]. Fallback: Textile museum + Jardín Etnobotánico.

**Sat 14 — Cooking class.** **La Cocina Oaxaqueña**: 09:00–14:00, 5 h, Central de Abastos market tour, hotel pickup, meal, MXN 1,600 group / MXN 2,500 private; 50% PayPal deposit, refund only ≥10 days out [S39]. Alt **Casa Crespo** (13:30, 4.5 h, no set menu, 15 max, non-refundable on Viator) [S40]. Tell them "intolerante a la lactosa" when booking — moles are dairy-free, quesillo and crema aren't. Evening: mezcal at In Situ or Mezcaloteca (est. MXN 250 flight).

**Sun 15** — **Tlacolula Sunday market + Mitla + Hierve el Agua + Teotitlán del Valle weaving + Santiago Matatlán palenque**: GYG full-day, US$43, 11 h, entrances extra (Tule MXN 20, Mitla 220, Hierve 100), free cancel 24 h [S41]. Ask the operator to swap Tule for the Tlacolula market on Sunday. Hierve el Agua confirmed open 2026 [S41]. Lunch in Teotitlán at a comedor (est. MXN 120).

**Mon 16 (Revolution Day; museums closed) — HIKE 2: Sierra Norte, Benito Juárez → Cuajimoloyas.** 8–10 km, 3,050–3,230 m, +350 m, 4–5 h walking. Options: **GYG/Valterra US$106**, 9 h, bilingual guide, breakfast + lunch, free cancel 24 h [S42]; **Coyote Aventuras MXN 2,950**, 07:00–17:00, certified guide, meals, min 3 [S43]; **lean: Expediciones Sierra Norte office, M. Bravo 210** — community guide MXN 300–500/group, entry MXN 50–100, comedor meal MXN 60–100, colectivo from Central de Abastos MXN 40–100 (leave 07:00–10:00) [S44]. Book the GYG version; the DIY route means a chaotic market pickup [S44]. Dinner **Tlayudas Libres** (est. MXN 120, order "sin quesillo").

**Tue 17** — **Jardín Etnobotánico** English tour (est. MXN 100, Tue/Thu/Sat 11:00), **Museo Textil** (free), coffee at **Boulenc** [S35]. Lunch **Itanoní** (native-corn tlayudas MXN 100) [S35]. Dinner **Casa Oaxaca** (MXN 400–700; terrace books weeks ahead) [S38].

**Wed 18 — Buffer.** Colectivo to **San Bartolo Coyotepec** (black pottery) and **Arrazola** (alebrijes), or a second palenque day in Matatlán with the hostel crowd, or Casa Crespo if Sat was full. Dinner **Zandunga** (Isthmus food, MXN 150–200) [S35].

**Thu 19** — Morning flight OAX→MEX; check in (Kuxan or Casa Comtesse for the upgrade). Afternoon **Coyoacán**: Frida Kahlo (book online 2–3 weeks ahead, est. MXN 320), Mercado de Coyoacán tostadas (est. MXN 80). Fallback: Museo Anahuacalli.

**Fri 20** — **Xochimilco** with a hostel group (official rate **MXN 750/boat/hour**, same for 1–18 people — never pay per person; go by Tren Ligero from Tasqueña) [S45]; solo alternative: Roma–Condesa free walking tour + **Expendio de Maíz** (MXN 150–250, walk-in) [S31]. Dinner **Máximo Bistrot** (MXN 300–500, book direct) [S31].

**Sat 21 — HIKE 3: Desierto de los Leones.** Uber from Condesa ~MXN 250, 40 min, to "Ex Convento"; convent MXN 21, 10–17 (closed Mon); loops north and south of the monastery, ~10–12 km / 4 h at 2,800–3,200 m (est.); **no signposts, no signal — go on a weekend when trails are busy, or with hostel mates; finish by 16:30** [S46]. Farewell dinner **Rosetta** (MXN 500–800, Resy 2–3 wks) [S31] or tacos at Los Cocuyos (est. MXN 100).

**Sun 22** — Taxi by 09:15 for AC990 11:55.

## 8. Top risks

1. **Bus attacks on the Cuacnopalan–Oaxaca autopista** (two ADO incidents 30–31 Aug 2026, nighttime) [S11] → fly both legs; if bus, GL/Platino daytime only, TAPO ≤08:00.
2. **Altitude** — Izta start ≥3,600 m, Sierra Norte 3,200 m, CDMX 2,240 m [S10][S33][S42] → hikes on days 5 and 10, hydrate, no alcohol the night before, tell the guide at the first headache.
3. **Water/food** — bottled or filtered water only, no ice from unknown sources, market food from busy stalls; dairy hides in quesillo, crema, hot chocolate de leche → order "sin queso/sin crema"; carry lactase.
4. **Taxi and pier scams** — unlicensed airport touts, per-person trajinera pricing [S8][S45] → booth taxi or app; official rate board at the pier; pink/white "A/B"-plate taxis only [S10].
5. **Metro pickpocketing and Casa Pepe-style noise/party fatigue** [S10][S9] → Uber after dark, bag in front on Line 1/2; base in Condesa/Juárez, not a Centro party hostel.
