# Bajío colonial corridor + Mexico City — Sat 14 Nov → Sun 29 Nov 2026 (solo, 15 nights)

Researched 2026-09-10 by web search only; every URL accessed that day. Rates used: USD 1.36 CAD, MXN 0.075 CAD. "(est.)" = estimate, not sourced. Prepare only; you book. Delta for 7–22 Nov: return fares roughly C$50–100 cheaper (est.); Revolution Day (observed Mon 16 Nov) falls in either window.

## 0. Advisory check (shapes the whole route)

Canada's advisory (updated 9 Sep 2026): Mexico "exercise a high degree of caution"; **Guanajuato state: "avoid non-essential travel in all areas south of and including highways 43D and 45D"** — Celaya, Salamanca, Irapuato (CJNG vs Santa Rosa de Lima). Guanajuato city, San Miguel de Allende, Dolores Hidalgo and Querétaro are north of 45D and outside the zone; BJX airport sits on 45D at Silao (transit only). Intercity travel only by "VIP or executive class" bus on toll roads, daylight only; "illegal roadblocks are common"; in Mexico City use apps or official pink/white taxis and verify the plate ([travel.gc.ca/destinations/mexico](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/mexico)). Context: February 2026 nationwide cartel roadblocks after El Mencho's death; tourist zones have stabilised ([jetpacglobal](https://www.jetpacglobal.com/blog/canada-mexico-travel-warning/)). Local April 2026 guidance: historic centre safe; avoid Celaya/Salamanca/Irapuato; 45D toll road in daylight only ([mexicotravelandleisure](https://www.mexicotravelandleisure.com/blog/is-guanajuato-safe/)).

**Consequence:** fly into BJX. Do not bus MEX→Guanajuato — that route runs 45D through Celaya–Salamanca–Irapuato. Ask Primera Plus at the Guanajuato counter which road the San Miguel bus takes; the Dolores Hidalgo road (Hwy 110/51) stays north of 45D.

The 57D corridor's crime is mostly night-time cargo theft; daytime ETN/Primera Plus is the standard safe choice. Querétaro is "one of Mexico's safest mid-size cities" (same source).

**Alternative without CDMX:** GTO 5 → SMA 4 → QRO 3 → GTO 2 → fly BJX home on an Aeroméxico return (~C$1,000). Saves ~C$150 in CDMX lodging and one bus; loses Teotihuacán, Anthropology, Frida and the two best day hikes. Net ≈ C$250 cheaper, far less culture per dollar — not recommended unless the advisory expands.

## 8. Top risks

1. **Guanajuato-state cartel violence / roadblocks on the 45D corridor.** Fly BJX, daylight executive buses only, Dolores road if offered, no car rental, follow El Sol de León the week before.
2. **Express kidnapping / rogue taxis in CDMX and at bus terminals.** App cars only, verify the plate, no street cabs, no ATMs after dark, cards in two places.
3. **Altitude and exposure on hikes** (2,000–3,900 m; Bernal's rock and the Bufa scramble are unshaded). Two easy days before the Bufa, 3 L of water, early starts, never alone in Desierto de los Leones, skip Bernal if wet.
4. **Stomach / dairy.** Bottled or filtered water only; "sin crema, sin queso"; lactase tablets; ORS sachets; market stalls only at peak lunch turnover.
5. **Scams and overbooking.** Xochimilco per-person pricing (check the board), Valenciana mine "tips", Frida resellers (official site only), non-refundable hostel rates; the flagged properties above (Selina, Punto 79, Casa Pepe, Blue Bicycle) — avoid.
