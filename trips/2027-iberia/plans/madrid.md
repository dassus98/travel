## 1. Flights — no nonstop in late March; Air Canada via Montréal, book by 15 Dec 2026

- Nonstop status: Air Canada's YYZ–MAD (AC824/825) is seasonal — no operations Jan–Mar 2026, first in May. Iberia's YYZ–MAD (IB368) launched June 2026 as summer-seasonal. Assume **no nonstop on 25 Mar 2027**; IATA summer season starts 28 Mar, so the return might be nonstop — set an alert.
- **Out, Thu 25 Mar**: AC YYZ→YUL mid-afternoon feeder (~15:00), then **AC834 YUL 17:55 → MAD 06:50 Fri** (A330-300, year-round). ~2 h connection, one ticket.
- **Back, Sun 4 Apr**: **AC835 MAD 14:55 → YUL** (~17:20), then YUL→YYZ evening feeder (~19:00→20:20).
- Alternative: TAP TP262 to Lisbon + LIS–MAD hop, arriving ~15:30 (loses half of Friday); TAP one-stops from ~C$574 rt in shoulder months.
- Fare: Air Canada shows "from C$916" rt for Mar/Apr 2027; Kayak average C$1,186, cheapest ~8 weeks out. **Budget C$1,100 pp, Economy Standard** (1 checked bag + seat; Basic charges C$75/bag each way).
- Aeroplan ~35k pts each way + taxes ≈ 1.5¢/pt — skip unless you have surplus.
- Fri 26 departure: −1 night, −1 food day, fares typically +C$50–100; net ≈ −C$130 pp but you lose Saturday. Not recommended.

## 2. Lodging — Chamberí or Retiro, 9 nights, no tourist tax

- **Chamberí**: residential, clean, Ponzano tapas street, 10 min metro to the Prado, quietest value. **Retiro/Ibiza**: park, museums walkable, calm.
- Salamanca/Goya: fine, priciest. Avoid **Malasaña** for sleeping (loud, gritty spots), **Sol/Centro** (procession routes, crowds), **Lavapiés** (pickpockets).
- Madrid has **no tourist tax**. Listings must show a Comunidad de Madrid **VT-/HM-** registration number; unlicensed listings get cancelled.
- Candidates: **Líbere Madrid Chamberí** (José Abascal 8, licence HM-121, 2-bed, 24/7 reception, flexible rate; est. €230–290/night, no cleaning fee) ≈ €2,300 for 9 nights; **Airbnb 2-bed Retiro/Ibiza** est. €190–240 + €70–90 cleaning ≈ €1,950; **Vrbo/Booking Salamanca** est. €240–320 + cleaning ≈ €2,600.
- Plan **€200/night + €80 cleaning = €1,880 ≈ C$3,021 group / C$1,007 pp**. Book by **1 Nov 2026**, free cancellation to 7–14 days out. Keep the apartment through the loop night.

## 3. Car — Tue 30 to Wed 31 Mar (2 days), Sixt Atocha

- Pick up **Sixt Madrid Atocha** (arrivals hall, 07:30–23:00, returns to Saba P3). Outside the Distrito Centro restricted zone; on the A-6 in 15 min. Compact automatic ≈ €55–70/day at Atocha; book ~40 days out; skip Record go/Goldcar.
- Zero-excess cover (€20–25/day) unless a card gives primary CDW; hold €300–1,000. Rental cars carry the DGT eco label (Madrid ZBE fine) — never drive into Distrito Centro or **Toledo's old town (ZPR camera fine €200)**.
- Tolls: AP-6/AP-61 to Segovia ~€10 (cash/card, no transponder). Fuel ~€1.55/L, ~380 km ≈ €45. **IDP required in Spain** (CAA, C$35).
- Parking: Segovia Padre Claret ~€2/h; Ávila Puerta del Carmen ~€1.50/h; **Parador de Toledo free**; Toledo Safont lot €0.40/h.
- Total ≈ **€240 ≈ C$386 group / C$129 pp**. (3-day with-beach car version ≈ €480 ≈ C$770.)

## 4. Day by day

Lunch **Bar Ludeña** carcamusas (€15) or **Alfileritos 24** (€30). 17:00 → Madrid (1 h), return car at Atocha. Dinner **Sala de Despiece** (Chamberí, modern counter, €40, queue 20:30).

**Thu 1 Apr — Beach day by train (recommended over driving)** — AVE/Iryo 07:30 Atocha → Valencia Joaquín Sorolla, 1h50 (€25–60 each way; book at 60 days).
