# Plan B — Barcelona, Thu 25 Mar – Sun 4 Apr 2027 (9 nights, 8 full days)

Researched 2026-09-10 by web search only. € × 1.607 = CAD. "(est.)" = estimate. Sources in `research/2026-09-10-plans-sources.md`. Prepare only; you book.

## 1. Flights — one-stop out via Montréal, likely nonstop back; book mid-Dec 2026

- Nonstop status: Air Canada YYZ–BCN is seasonal, historically starting at the IATA summer season (29 Mar 2025). Summer 2027 starts **Sun 28 Mar 2027**, so: **Thu 25 Mar out = no nonstop; Sun 4 Apr back = nonstop likely** (verify when AC loads S27, ~Oct–Nov 2026).
- **Out, Thu 25 Mar**: AC YYZ→YUL shuttle landing by ~16:30, then **AC822 YUL 18:35 → BCN ~07:15 Fri** (A330-300, year-round daily). ~2 h layover, bags through, one ticket. Backups: TAP via LIS, Lufthansa via FRA/MUC, Air France via CDG (9.5–11 h total).
- **Back, Sun 4 Apr**: **AC821 BCN 13:15 → YYZ 16:00** (787/777) if loaded; else BCN→YUL + shuttle.
- Fare: Kayak 2026 range C$808–1,236, avg C$992; ~14 weeks ahead is optimal. **Budget C$1,100–1,300 pp, Economy Standard.** Buy if ≤ C$1,050. Set alerts YYZ↔BCN and YUL→BCN in October.
- Aeroplan: only with ≥60k pts each; skip otherwise.
- Fri 26 departure: saves one apartment night (≈ −C$110 pp; tax cap already hit) but loses Saturday — the only day before Easter Sunday with markets open. Not recommended.

## 2. Lodging — Eixample Dreta or Gràcia, 9 nights (or a 7-night split)

- **Eixample Dreta**: the grid between Passeig de Gràcia and Sagrada Família; safe, quiet at night, licensed aparthotels, L2/L4/L5. **Gràcia**: village feel, best casual food, quiet plaças, 15 min walk to Batlló; fewer licensed 2-beds.
- Sant Antoni: close third (market, bars, near Sants for the car), noisier weekends. Sarrià-Sant Gervasi: calm but 20–30 min from everything.
- Avoid **Raval** (theft, dirt) and **Barceloneta / lower Gòtic near the Ramblas** (crowds, noise, pickpockets).
- Rules: only listings with **HUTB-0xxxxx** (and from 2026 a national ESFCTU code). Tourist tax on apartments: €9.50/person/night to 31 Mar, **€10.50 from 1 Apr 2027**, + 10% VAT, first 7 nights only, paid on arrival → ≈ **€230–245 for three**.
- Candidates: **Aspasios Rambla Catalunya "Stylish"** 2-bed/2-bath, 65 m², Eixample Dreta, no cleaning fee, free cancel to 48 h, HUTB-licensed (est. €290–330/night Easter week); **Eric Vökel Gran Vía Suites** 2-bed + sofa, 15% direct discount, free cancel 48 h (est. €230–270); **Airbnb 2-bed Vila de Gràcia**, HUTB, ≥4.8, Superhost (est. €180–220 + €80–120 cleaning).
- Plan **€200/night × 9 + €90 cleaning + €243 tax = €2,133 ≈ C$3,428 group / C$1,143 pp**. Money-saver: two stays at the same aparthotel (26–29 Mar and 31 Mar–4 Apr), bags stored, saves ~€400–600 — the tax cap resets per stay but 3 + 4 = 7 nights anyway.
- Book by **early November 2026**; free-cancellation rates; screen for the licence number in text and a photo of the plaque.

## 3. Car — Mon 29 to Wed 31 Mar (3 days), Sixt Sants

- Pick up **Sixt, Barcelona Sants station** Mon 09:00; return Wed by 19:00. West side → Ronda/AP-7 north, no centre driving; metro L3/L5 to it.
- Compact/Intermediate; Easter ~€45–55/day (est.); automatic +€10–15/day. Take **zero-excess/Top cover (~€25–30/day)** — Spanish basic-cover deposits run €900–1,800.
- **AP-7 is toll-free**; via AP-7/C-55 to Montserrat avoids the C-16 tunnel toll. Fuel ~450 km ≈ €55. **IDP required** (CAA, C$32, two photos) — for both drivers.
- Parking: Girona Parking Catedral (~€2.5/h); Calella de Palafrugell — Hotel Sant Roc has no parking, street/municipal lot free in March (est.); Peratallada signed lot outside walls (€3–5); Pals free lot below old town; Montserrat car park ~€6.50.
- Total ≈ **€310 ≈ C$500 group / C$167 pp**.

## 4. Day by day

Transit: **T-casual** (10 rides, €13, zone 1) each, two per person over the trip; airport L9 is a separate €11.80 return; single €2.90. Cheaper than Hola BCN at this pace.

**Thu 25** — YYZ→YUL→BCN.

**Fri 26 (Good Friday; arrive ~07:15)** — Aerobus/L9 to base; store bags. Slow walk of Passeig de Gràcia facades + Gràcia squares; nap. Lunch **Bar del Pla** (Born, tapas €25–35, book 1 wk) or a Gràcia menú. Light tapas dinner in Gràcia (walk-in). Shops shut; Gaudí sites open. Fallback: Picasso Museum.

**Sat 27** — **Sagrada Família 09:00 with towers (€36)**; **book the day 2027 tickets release (~Nov 2026) — sells out 2–4 wks ahead at Easter**. Afternoon **Mercat de Sant Antoni** (open Sat) then Montjuïc/MNAC terrace. Dinner **Gresca** (€50–75, book 1–2 wks). Fallback: MNAC.

**Sun 28 (Easter; clocks +1 h)** — **Park Güell 09:30 slot** (~€10; book 1–2 wks; bus 116 up). Skip the Gòtic today (Easter Mass crowds). Lunch **Can Solé** (Barceloneta, Sun 13–16, paella/lobster, book 1 wk). Sunset at Bunkers del Carmel. Many restaurants closed tonight — apartment supper (shop Saturday).

**Mon 29 (Easter Monday, Catalan holiday) — Loop day 1** — Car at Sants 09:00. AP-7 → **Girona** (1h15): Cathedral + Sant Feliu (€7.50 with audio guide), Arab Baths (€3; holidays 10–14 only), walls, Onyar houses. Lunch **Nu** (open Mon; tasting €40–60, book 2 wks) — many Girona places close Monday. 50 min → **Calella de Palafrugell**; check in **Hotel Sant Roc** (beachfront, sea-view rooms sleep 3, open from mid-Feb, ~US$188/night for two). Dinner in the hotel dining room (~€35) — village restaurants mostly closed Mon.

**Tue 30 — Beach day** — Camí de Ronda coastal path Calella → **Llafranc** and back (1.5 h). **Beach lunch: Tragamar**, Platja del Canadell, tables on the sand; rice dishes and whole fish ~€45; Tue 13–15:30 from March; reserve by phone 1 wk ahead (terrace not reservable — arrive 13:00). Afternoon: **Pals** (free) → **Peratallada** (walls, lanes); dinner **Can Bonay** (Catalan classics, €30–40, book). Back to Sant Roc (25 min). Rain fallback: Dalí Theatre-Museum, Figueres (45 min, ~€17, timed).

Pre-season note: Costa Brava hotels and restaurants reopen between mid-Feb and Easter; Easter week is the season opener, but hours are short and Mondays close. Phone-confirm Sant Roc, Tragamar and Can Bonay in early March. Backups: Sol i Mar (Calella), Hotel Aigua Blava restaurant (Begur).

**Wed 31 — Loop day 3** — 09:00 AP-7 → C-55 → **Montserrat** (2 h). Basilica €9–10; basilica + museum €12–14 online; Sant Joan funicular for the ridge walk (~€18 return). Escolania choir likely on Easter break. Picnic from Calella (cafeteria is poor). 1 h to Sants, return car by 17:00. Dinner **Bodega 1900** (Sant Antoni, vermouth bar, €35–45, book 1–2 wks). Fog fallback: museum only.

**Thu 1 Apr** — **Casa Batlló 09:00 Silver (€40; book 2 wks)** + Casa Milà exterior; Palau de la Música tour (~€22, book 1 wk). Lunch Boqueria back-row bars before 13:00 (**Direkte Boqueria** tasting ~€55, book 3–4 wks) or Santa Caterina's Cuines. Dinner **Bar Cañete** (€40–70; book 2–3 wks, or bar walk-in before 20:00).

**Fri 2** — Gòtic + Born early: cathedral, Santa Maria del Mar, Picasso Museum (~€15, book 1 wk). Lunch **Cal Pep** (Born counter, €50–60; no reservation for the bar — arrive 12:45 for 13:00). Afternoon Ciutadella → beach walk to Poblenou. Dinner **Els Pescadors** (Poblenou seafood, €50–60, book 1 wk). Fallback: MUHBA Roman ruins.

**Sat 3** — **Sant Pau Recinte Modernista** (~€16, uncrowded) or Montjuïc by cable car. Lunch **La Cova Fumada** (Barceloneta bombas, cash, €15–20, 12:30, no reservations). Farewell dinner **Besta** (Eixample, Galician-Catalan modern, ~€70, book 3 wks) or **Suculent** (~€60). Pack.

**Sun 4** — Check-out 11:00 → L9/Aerobus to T1 by 10:30 for AC821 13:15.

## 8. Risks

1. No nonstop out / S27 shifts — one AC ticket via YUL (protected connection); never separate tickets.
2. Sagrada Família sells out — buy on release day; fallback guided-tour tickets or Thu 1 Apr 08:00 slot.
3. Costa Brava pre-season closures — phone-confirm early March; backups listed.
4. Unlicensed or scam listing — HUTB visible, plaque in photos, ≥20 reviews, pay on-platform; aparthotels as default.
5. Pickpocketing/crowds (Ramblas, L3, processions) — cross-body bags, Gòtic at dawn, Ramblas never after dark; zero-excess car cover to keep deposit exposure off the table.
