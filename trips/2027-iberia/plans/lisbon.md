## 2. Lodging — 2-bed apartment, Campo de Ourique or Estrela/Lapa, 9 nights

- **Campo de Ourique** (first choice): residential, flat-ish, excellent market, tram 28 terminus, 10 min to Sete Rios car pickup, quietest and best value; 20 min to Chiado.
- **Estrela/Lapa**: leafy, embassy-quiet, Jardim da Estrela, MNAA; slightly pricier, very clean.
- Avoid as a base: Alfama/Bairro Alto (bar noise to 2 am, stairs, tuk-tuks, weekend grime), Santos/Cais do Sodré (club noise), Príncipe Real (lovely, pricey, party-adjacent) — visit, don't sleep there.
- Rates: Lisbon 2-bed median ≈ €171/night; Campo de Ourique/São Bento 2-beds aggregate ~US$197. Plan **€170/night × 9 = €1,530 + cleaning ~€70 + tourist tax €4/person/night capped at 7 nights = €84 → €1,684 ≈ C$2,706 group / C$902 pp**.
- Rules: every legal listing shows an **AL/xxxxx** licence number — skip any without. Lisbon cancelled ~6,800 licences in Feb 2026; legal stock is thinner.
- Book by **mid-October 2026**, free cancellation to ≥7 days out. Keep the apartment through the loop nights (bags stay; cheaper than checking out).

## 3. Car — Wed 31 Mar to Fri 2 Apr (3 days)

- Pick up **Sete Rios** (Sixt/Europcar city branch): 10 min from base, no airport surcharge, straight onto the A2 over the 25 de Abril bridge. Oriente station is the alternative.
- Compact automatic (308/Golf class) ~€45–70/day off-peak. Take the renter's **zero-excess (SCDW, €10–20/day)**; credit-card CDW leaves you fronting a €1,000–2,500 hold. Deposit on the driver's physical credit card. Avoid Goldcar (1.3/5).
- Tolls: **Via Verde transponder add-on (€1.50–2/day)** mandatory for electronic-only roads; bridge ~€2.10 northbound only; Lisbon–Évora A2/A6 ≈ €10 each way (est.). Fuel ~€1.90/L, ~450 km ≈ €60.
- IDP: Canadian licences recognised in Portugal; IDP recommended, rarely checked. Get one anyway (CAA, C$32).
- Parking: Évora hotel has a free lot; Sesimbra harbour ~€1/h; Portinho da Arrábida lot €3–5 (the summer road closure is 4 Jun–15 Sep only).
- Total ≈ **€321 ≈ C$516 group / C$172 pp**.

## 4. Day by day

Lunch: Mercado de Campo de Ourique stalls (€8–15). Dinner: **Tasca da Esquina** (Campo de Ourique, €30–40, book 1–2 wks). Museums open; some family tascas shut. Fallback: MNAA.

**Sun 28 (Easter; clocks +1 h)** — Pena, Jerónimos, Belém Tower **closed**. Anchor: **Cascais** by CP train from Cais do Sodré (~40 min, ~€2.40): marina → Boca do Inferno. Lunch **Mar do Inferno** (cliff-top shellfish, €40–55; **book Easter lunch 3 wks ahead**). Sunday timetable. Light dinner at Time Out Market. Fallback: Museu Paula Rego (Cascais); malls open.

**Mon 29 (not a holiday in Portugal)** — **Sintra early**: Rossio 08:11 train (€2.30) → bus 434 (€4.10, first ~08:50, queues) → **Pena 09:30 first slot** (Palace + Park ~€20; timed, **no lateness tolerance**, book ~2 wks ahead). Then **Quinta da Regaleira** (€20). Lunch **Tascantiga** (petiscos €15–20, walk-in). Optional bus 1624 via Cabo da Roca to Cascais (€4.50, 60 min) for sunset, train home. Rain fallback: Palácio Nacional de Sintra + Monserrate.

**Tue 30** — **Belém** (tram 15E/bus 728). **Jerónimos** €18 (closed Mon; timed slot, book 1 wk; combo with Torre €21); MAAT; Pastéis de Belém at 09:00 (no queue). Lunch **O Frade** (Alentejo counter, €25–30, book 1 wk). Dinner **Taberna da Rua das Flores** (no reservations, queue 19:00, mains €14–22). Fallback: MAAT / Berardo.

**Wed 31 — Loop day 1** — Sete Rios 09:00 → A2 → **Cabo Espichel** cliffs → **Sesimbra** harbour. **Beach lunch: Portinho da Arrábida, Restaurante O Farol** (12:15–15:30, closed Mon; grilled dourada/robalo, arroz de marisco, €30–40; reserve by WhatsApp 1–2 wks, slow to reply). Afternoon: Azeitão (José Maria da Fonseca cellar ~€12) → A2/A6 → **Évora** (1h30). Stay 2 nights **Évora Olive Hotel** (4★, historic centre, free parking, 9.0+/10, ~US$80–100/room × 2 rooms). Dinner **Botequim da Mouraria** (counter, no bookings, 19:00) or **Taberna Típica Quarta-Feira** (set feast, book 2 wks). Rain fallback: Setúbal Mercado do Livramento.

**Thu 1 Apr — Loop day 2** — Évora on foot: Templo Romano, Sé (~€5), **Capela dos Ossos** €7. Afternoon: **Almendres Cromlech** (15 min, free) → **Monsaraz** hilltop + Alqueva lake (55 min). Dinner **Dom Joaquim** (Bib Gourmand, €30–35, book 1 wk). Fallback: Cartuxa winery (10 min).

**Fri 2 — Return** — Évora 09:30 → Arraiolos → A6/A2 → Sete Rios by 13:00 (1h45). Afternoon MNAA (Lapa) or LX Factory. Dinner **Prado** (modern Portuguese, €45–55, book 2–3 wks).

**Sat 3** — Tram 28 at 07:30 from the Campo de Ourique end to Graça; **Feira da Ladra** flea market (Sat); Museu Nacional do Azulejo (~€10). Lunch **O Velho Eurico** (Mouraria petiscos €6–14, book 7+ days). Farewell dinner **Solar dos Presuntos** (mains €18–32, book 2 wks). Fallback: Gulbenkian.

**Sun 4** — Coffee at Jardim da Estrela; check-out 11:00; metro to LIS by 15:00 for TP261 18:05.
