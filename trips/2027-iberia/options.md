## What would change the ranking
- If Air Canada is running YYZ–BCN or YYZ–MAD nonstop in late March 2027 (check on the live pass, ~Oct–Nov), B and C gain ~C$100–200 and a lot of friction relief.
- If lodging comes in at the low end in Barcelona (Eixample 2-bed ≤ €200/night), B fits the ideal budget.
- If the beach day is quietly dropped by the group, C becomes the lowest-risk option and A loses its main edge.
- If you find a cheap one-stop via the Azores (SATA/Azores Airlines) to Lisbon, A drops another ~C$150.

## Recommendation for the vote (not a decision)
A fits every must-have, every nice-to-have, the ideal budget, and the fewest dislikes. B is the better road trip and the worse everything else. C is the option if "safe, clean, organized" outranks "coast" for the group.
