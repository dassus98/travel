## Safety / crowding / taxes
- Canada advisories (9 Sep 2026): Spain "high degree of caution" (terrorism); Portugal "normal precautions". Pickpocket hotspots: BCN — El Prat, Ramblas, Pl. Catalunya, Sagrada, Park Güell; MAD — Barajas, Atocha, Sol, Gran Vía, Retiro; LIS — trams 15/25/28.
- Barcelona has by far the highest absolute theft count (Mossos 87k in 2025, down 7.6%). Madrid thefts −9.9% Jan–Sep 2025. Lisbon pickpocketing +7.7% in 2025 nationally, ~35% of it in Lisbon city.
- Cleanliness: OCU 2023 scored Barcelona and Madrid 40/100 (dated). Lisbon graffiti/pavements in Alfama/Bairro Alto read as grimy to sensitive travellers.
- Short-term rentals: all legal in 2027. Barcelona licences expire Nov 2028 (book only listings with an HUTB number); Lisbon cancelled 6,765 AL licences Feb 2026 (fewer, pricier legal listings); Madrid Centro restricted, existing units continue.
- Tourist tax per person per night: Lisbon €4 (cap 7 nights) ≈ C$45/person; Madrid none; Barcelona apartments ≈ €10.50 + VAT from 1 Apr 2027 (cap 7 nights) ≈ C$120/person.

## Car loops
- Lisbon: Sintra (35 min, go at 9:30 opening) + Cascais; Arrábida/Sesimbra (45 min) → Comporta (~1h15, unverified) → Évora (1.5–2 h). Beach lunch: Portinho da Arrábida or Comporta — sheltered, quiet in April.
- Madrid: Segovia (1 h) → Ávila (~1 h) → Toledo (1 h from Madrid) → optional Cuenca (1h45). No beach in reach (Valencia ~3.5 h by car, unverified).
- Barcelona: Girona (1 h) → Begur/Calella de Palafrugell (2h15 total) → Pals/Peratallada → back via Montserrat (1 h) or Sitges. Beach lunch: Calella de Palafrugell — best of the three, but pre-season: some restaurants shut midweek.

## Food fit (dairy)
- Lisbon: lightest dairy (olive-oil cooking; watch butter sauces, cheese starters). Tascas: Zé dos Cornos, Imperial de Campo de Ourique, Tasquinha do Lagarto, Adega Solar Minhoto, O Tachadas.
- Madrid: moderate (béchamel croquetas, cheese boards). Casa María, Rayuela, Los Galayos, Casa Mories, Terramundi. Dinner rarely before 21:00; menús weekday-only.
- Barcelona: moderate (crema catalana, some cheese). Calçots still in season. Arrosseria Xàtiva, Cal Boter, Fonda Pepa, La Cova Fumada (cash, closes 15:15), El Quim de la Boqueria.
