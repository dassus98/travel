# Plan — SW Germany by regional train, Sat 21 Nov – Sun 6 Dec 2026 (15 nights, 14 on the ground)

Researched 2026-09-10 by web search only (all URLs accessed that day). € × 1.607 = CAD. "(est.)" = estimate. Prepare only; you book. Route: FRA → **Köln 3 n → (Aachen) → Heidelberg 3 n → Tübingen 2 n → Stuttgart 2 n → Freiburg 4 n** → FRA.

**Headline findings:** (1) Every market on the route is already open when you land (Köln 16 Nov, Freiburg 19, Aachen 20, Heidelberg 23, Esslingen 24, Ravenna Gorge 26 Nov) — the "markets in week 2" constraint is met in week 1 too. (2) **Schauinslandbahn is closed for revision 16 Nov–4 Dec 2026** — reopens Sat 5 Dec, your last full day. (3) The **right-Rhine line (Troisdorf–Koblenz–Wiesbaden) is closed for Generalsanierung 10 Jul–11 Dec 2026**, so regional Köln→Heidelberg is a 5-h left-Rhine slog; one ICE Sparpreis (~€25–40) fixes it. (4) **Kölner Dom charges €12 for tourist entry since 1 Jul 2026.** (5) Deutschland-Ticket is €63/calendar month; your trip straddles Nov/Dec so it is two tickets or one plus day tickets. (6) The C$2,500/3,000 target is not reachable with 14 private-room nights; realistic lean ≈ C$4,400.

## 8. Risks

1. **Daylight + weather:** 16:30 dark, 3–8 °C, rain or wet snow; Feldberg snow/ice likely. Start hikes by 09:00, carry microspikes + headlamp, keep Wurmlinger Kapelle and Kaiserstuhl as low-altitude swaps.
2. **Lift closures:** Schauinslandbahn shut until 4 Dec (reopening "subject to change"); Feldbergbahn 2026 winter start unposted. Plan Sat 5 Dec as the flexible day; hike-up variants need no lift.
3. **Rail disruption:** right-Rhine closure through 11 Dec loads the left-Rhine regionals; ICE Sparpreis for the two long legs; DB Navigator alerts; keep 90-min buffers before the flight home (or airport hotel).
4. **Crowds/pickpockets:** Köln Dom market and Hbf, Heidelberg Hauptstraße Saturdays, Esslingen weekends. Weekday evenings for markets, money belt, phone in front pocket; Köln Hbf/Dom forecourt is the one "watch your bag" zone on this route.
5. **Entry:** **EES** biometric registration live since Oct 2025 (5-h queues reported at peaks); **ETIAS (€7, 3 years) planned Q4 2026** — apply the day it opens if before 21 Nov ([Travelweek, 6 Jul 2026](https://www.travelweek.ca/news/destinations/canadian-govt-posts-etias-heads-up-for-travellers-to-europe-as-back-end-ees-continues-to-cause-headaches/)). Passport valid ≥3 months past 6 Dec. Sunday-morning FRA arrival is the light EES slot.
