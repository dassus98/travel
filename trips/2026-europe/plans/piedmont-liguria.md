# Plan — Piedmont + Liguria by train, Sat 31 Oct – Sun 15 Nov 2026 (15 nights, 14 on the ground)

Researched 2026-09-10 by web search only; all URLs accessed that day. € × 1.607 = CAD. "(est.)" = estimate. Prepare only; you book. Dated Booking/Hostelworld pages blocked fetches, so nightly rates are aggregator/review-derived ranges — confirm before booking.

**Headline tension:** own-room-every-night on this route prices at ≈C$3,500 lean / C$4,600 best (§6). C$2,500 is not reachable with the lodging rule; C$3,000 needs 3–4 dorm nights or dropping Milan + the class. Decide early.

## 1. Flights — no nonstop in the window; Air Canada via Montréal on one ticket

- **No YYZ–MXP nonstop after the summer season.** FlightsFrom lists the route inactive (AC last 23 Feb 26 one-off; Neos last 15 Sep 26) ([flightsfrom.com/YYZ-MXP](https://www.flightsfrom.com/YYZ-MXP); [flightconnections](https://www.flightconnections.com/flights-from-yyz-to-mxp)). Momondo's "daily nonstop" claim is stale.
- **Out, Sat 31 Oct — AC894** YYZ 17:00 → YUL 18:23 / 20:00 → **MXP 08:55 Sun 1 Nov**, A330-300, same flight number through Montréal, operates Mon/Wed/Thu/Sat from 24–26 Oct ([flightmapper AC894](https://info.flightmapper.net/flight/Air_Canada_AC_894)).
- **Back, Sun 15 Nov — AC895** MXP 12:05 → YUL 16:00 → **YYZ 18:50**, Tue/Thu/Fri/Sun from 25 Oct ([flightmapper AC895](https://info.flightmapper.net/flight/Air_Canada_AC_895)).
- **Fare:** Air Canada shows "from C$928" rt for Oct 2026 ([aircanada.com](https://www.aircanada.com/en-ca/flights-to-milan)); momondo Nov typical ≈ C$854 ([momondo](https://www.momondo.ca/flights/toronto/milan)). **Budget C$950 Economy Standard** (bag + seat); Basic ≈ C$850 but €75/way bag fees make it a wash. Book by **mid-Oct**.
- **Open-jaw (GOA or PSA out):** neither has North America service; you'd connect on LH/ITA at +C$150–300 (est.) to save a €25, 3 h Levanto–Milan train. Not worth it. Keep MXP both ways; end with one Milan night.
- **Airport→Turin (Sun 1 Nov):** FlixBus MXP T1 → Torino Porta Susa, ~17/day, from ~€16, ~2 h (est.) ([flixbus](https://www.flixbus.com/bus-routes/bus-milan-malpensa-airport-turin)). Alternative: Malpensa Express → Centrale €15/~50 min ([malpensaexpress.it](https://www.malpensaexpress.it/en/tickets/travel-documents/prices/)) + Regionale Veloce to Turin ~€13, 1h50 (est.). **Return (Sun 15):** Malpensa Express from Centrale ≈ 08:25.
- **±1 week:** earlier = Cinque Terre peak-priced to 1 Nov, fares ≈ same; later (7–22 Nov) = the historically best truffle weekends, fares ≈ −C$50 (est.), 15 min less daylight, more rain, Levanto hostel may have closed (unverified).

## 3. Lodging (own room every night; no Airbnb)

## 4. Day by day (sunset Turin ≈ 17:10 → 16:55; Genoa 17:13 on 1 Nov, 16:56 on 16 Nov — [WeatherSpark](https://weatherspark.com/m/58985/11/Average-Weather-in-November-in-Genoa-Italy))

**Sat 31 Oct** — AC894 17:00. Eat before YUL.

**Sun 1 Nov (All Saints; arrive 08:55)** — FlixBus to Turin (~12:30). Check-in, walk Via Roma → Piazza San Carlo → Piazza Castello. Food: **Caffè Al Bicerin** (bicerin = espresso + chocolate + cream, ~€7 — dairy; ask for cioccolata senza panna). Evening aperitivo **Spaccio Vini e Olii** (Quadrilatero, wine + boards €12–15) ([Italy Segreta](https://italysegreta.com/torino-a-tavola/)). Shops/most markets shut; museums open holiday hours. Fallback: rest.

**Mon 2** — **Free walking tour** 10:30 Piazza Carlo Felice, 2.5 h, tip-based, English Thu–Mon ([freewalkingtouritalia](https://freewalkingtouritalia.com/free-walking-tour/free-walking-tour-turin/)). Afternoon **Museo Nazionale del Cinema + Mole lift** (open Mon 9–19, closed Tue; buy online; museum+lift ≈ €19 est.) ([museocinema.it](https://www.museocinema.it/en/opening-hours-museo-nazionale-del-cinema)). Musei Reali closed Mondays. Food: **Piola da Cianci** (Quadrilatero, plin/tajarin, carafe wine, ~€20; tajarin is butter-sauced — order plin al sugo d'arrosto instead).

**Tue 3** — **Museo Egizio with guide**, 09:00: €18 ticket (online required) + private tour €70/60 min or €100/90 min for 1–6 people in English — solo pays the whole €70; free web-app audioguide is the lean option ([castellosangiorgio guide](https://castellosangiorgio.it/museo-egizio-di-torino-biglietti-orari-e-cosa-vedere/), [museoegizio.it](https://museoegizio.it/en/info/prices/)). Book 3–4 wks. Afternoon **Musei Reali/Palazzo Reale** €15, Tue–Sun 9–19 ([glocalgems](https://glocalgems.com/ita/posts/orari-e-costi-dei-musei-reali-di-torino/)). Food: **Porta Palazzo market** lunch (farinata, fruit, €8) — Monday-closed markets are open Tue; dinner **Consorzio** (Slow Food, battuta di Fassona, braised beef; tasting from €39, book 1 wk).

**Wed 4 — Hike 1: Sacra di San Michele** — Train Porta Susa → Sant'Ambrogio di Torino (~35 min, ~€4 est.), mule track/Via Francigena no. 502 from the church of San Giovanni Vincenzo: **~600 m gain, 1h30 up**, 3–4 h round trip; abbey **Nov hours Mon–Sat 9:30–16:30** (no Monday closure), entry ≈ €8 (est.) ([sacradisanmichele.com](https://sacradisanmichele.com/en/informazioni/)). Lunch: panino from Alba-bound bakery or the abbey café. Back by 16:00. Rain fallback: GAM or Palazzo Madama; or bus option from Avigliana (€6.30 combined, 09:00/10:00/14:00).

**Thu 5 — Hike 2: Superga, then Alba** — Tram 15 to Sassi; **Sentiero 29 from Corso Casale/Croce** through the Collina di Superga park, ≈ 5 km / 450 m / 1h30 up (est.; AllTrails "Sassi–Superga via Sentiero 27/29"). Basilica dome €6, Royal Tombs €6 (dome closed in rain/fog); ride the **Sassi–Superga rack tram** down (reopened 3 Apr 2026, closed Wednesdays, weekday €4 single — confirm Nov timetable) ([turismotorino](https://turismotorino.org/en/visit/things-to-do-and-things-to-see/royal-residences/basilica-di-superga-appartamento-e-reali-tombe-di-casa-savoia), [guidatorino](https://www.guidatorino.com/tranvia-sassi-superga-torino-orari-tariffe/)). 15:41 Regionale to Alba (1h12). Dinner **Osteria dell'Arco** (Piazza Ferrero, 40 yrs, Langhe wines by the glass, €35; book 1 wk) ([Gambero Rosso](https://www.gamberorossointernational.com/news/the-best-trattorias-to-enjoy-white-truffle-in-alba-and-surrounding-areas/)).

**Fri 6 — Hike 3: Barolo villages** — GrandaBus 2172 morning run to **La Morra** (confirm times at [grandabus.it](https://grandabus.it/orari-per-localita/); taxi €20 fallback). Walk La Morra belvedere → Cappella del Barolo (Sol LeWitt) → **Barolo** → Novello loop: Komoot "Barolo–Novello loop" 13 km / 370 m / ~4 h moderate, vineyard tracks ([Komoot](https://www.komoot.com/guide/541300/hiking-around-barolo)). WiMu wine museum in Barolo castle (~€9 est.). Lunch in Barolo (tajarin al ragù, €14–18). Bus 15:27 or 19:27 back. Vineyards mostly done with harvest; many cellars need appointment — book one tasting (~€25) via the enoteca. Rain: Alba's MUDET + Enoteca.

**Sat 7 — Truffle fair** — **Mercato Mondiale del Tartufo**, Cortile della Maddalena, 09:30–19:30; **€6 online (includes MUDET), wine coupon €10** (2 glasses + producer tastings); go at opening — dislikes crowds, and by noon it's packed ([fieradeltartufo.org](https://www.fieradeltartufo.org/eventi-fiera-del-tartufo/mercato-mondiale-del-tartufo/)). Market truffle €250–800/100 g ([meetpiemonte](https://www.meetpiemonte.com/albas-white-truffle-price-preservation-and-consumption/)). **Tartufo lunch at La Piola** (Piazza Risorgimento, Tre Gamberi): tajarin + white truffle shaved at table, ≈ €45–60 (est.; tajarin is egg + butter — accept or order plin in brodo + truffle); book 2–3 wks. Afternoon: bus 45 to Neive (every 2 h) + 3 km walk to **Barbaresco** tower/enoteca, or taxi €20. Fair cooking-show/sensory tickets at [ticket.fieradeltartufo.org](https://ticket.fieradeltartufo.org/) (book by mid-Oct; prices not published yet).

**Sun 8 → Genoa** — Early quiet market hour or Alba cathedral; 12:30-ish Regionale via Asti (€12, ~2 h). Evening **Sa Pesta** (1889 sciamadda; trofie al pesto — pesto contains Parmigiano; farinata dairy-free; €15) ([BudgetTraveller](https://budgettraveller.org/best-cheap-eats-genoa/)). Truffle hunts (GetYourGuide from US$93, 4.8/5) **need your own car to follow the guide** — only book a version with Alba pickup (~€110 est.) ([GetYourGuide](https://www.getyourguide.com/alba-l1166/langhe-truffle-hunting-experience-t453401/)).

**Mon 9 — Rolli + port** — **"7 Secrets of Genoa" free tour** 11:00, 2 h, 4.87/5 ([GuruWalk](https://www.guruwalk.com/genoa)). **Musei di Strada Nuova**: Mondays only Palazzo Rosso opens; ticket €14 incl. Sant'Agostino; lift out of service ([museidigenova.it](https://www.museidigenova.it/en/strada-nuova-museums)). Rolli Days 2026 ran 27–29 Mar; no autumn edition announced ([rolliestradenuove.it](https://www.rolliestradenuove.it/news/)). Porto Antico at dusk; **Antica Friggitoria Carega** cone €8. Dinner **Trattoria da Maria** (workers' trattoria, €12–15, cash).

**Tue 10 — Hike 4: Portofino park** — Regionale to Camogli. **San Rocco → Pietre Strette → San Fruttuoso → Base 0 → Portofino**: ≈ 12 km, ~600 m, 4.5–5 h. **Passo del Bacio and Via dei Tubi are closed by ordinance through 2026** — the Pietre Strette inland route avoids them ([portofinotrek](https://portofinotrek.com/en/6-main-paths-of-the-portofino-park-and-its-surroundings), [parcoportofino.it](https://www.parcoportofino.it/enparprtfn/po/mostra_news.php?id=35&area=H)). Abbey of San Fruttuoso €9 (est.); winter ferries limited (weekends, weather) — plan to walk out. Bus 782 Portofino → Santa Margherita, train back. Lunch: focaccia from Camogli (Revello). Rain fallback: Boccadasse by bus 31 + Nervi promenade, or swap with Wed.

**Wed 11 — Pesto class, then Levanto** — **Cesarine "Genovese pesto" home class** (mortar pesto, walnut sauce, sage focaccia; 3 h, max 6, €140, free cancellation) ([Cesarine](https://cesarine.com/en/experiences/genovese-pesto-cooking-class-na64DDuTqS)); cheaper: Viator group classes ≈ €75–95 (est.). Pesto = cheese; you make it, eat a little. 15:00 train to Levanto (from €8, 1h15). Dinner: Levanto focacceria (Il Laboratorio del Pesto / Focacceria il Falcone, €8).

**Thu 12 — Hike 5: Sentiero Azzurro** — Train Levanto → Monterosso (€5). **Monterosso → Vernazza 3.5 km/2 h, Vernazza → Corniglia 4 km/1.5 h**, both OPEN; **free from 2 Nov** (only Via dell'Amore stays paid) ([cinqueterre.eu.com status](https://www.cinqueterre.eu.com/en/cinque-terre-current-situation), [Card page](https://www.cinqueterre.eu.com/en/cinque-terre-card)). Corniglia–Manarola is landslide-closed; continue Corniglia → Volastra → Manarola (+2 h) if legs allow. Trains back €5. Lunch: anchovies at Vernazza (Belforte closes for winter — use Il Pirata delle Cinque Terre). Trails close after heavy rain — check the park board each morning.

**Fri 13 — Levanto → Monterosso coastal path** (7 km, 3 h, free, Punta Mesco views) then train; afternoon Riomaggiore + **Via dell'Amore** (1 km, one-way, booked slot ≈ €10 est.). Rain: Levanto's covered market/La Spezia CAMeC.

**Sat 14 → Milan** — IC Levanto → Milano Centrale (~3 h). Duomo terraces (online, ~€16 est.), Galleria, Brera walk; Navigli aperitivo (€12). Last Supper needs weeks' notice — skip unless a slot appears.

**Sun 15** — Malpensa Express 08:25 (€15) → AC895 12:05.
