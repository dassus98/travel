# Provence + Côte d'Azur by TER — Sat 31 Oct → Sun 15 Nov 2026

Rate used: **EUR 1 = CAD 1.607**. All prices accessed 2026-09-10 unless noted. "(est.)" = my estimate.
Window notes: Toussaint school holidays end Mon 2 Nov (all zones) — Nice days 1–2 slightly busier; **Wed 11 Nov Armistice** (banks/shops shut, monuments mostly open); sunset ~17:15 Nice / ~17:20 Avignon.

## Headline verdict
Solid route, near-zero car need, but **the C$3,000 ceiling is not reachable with an own room every night** (see Budget): lean ≈ **C$4,250**, best ≈ **C$5,900**. Levers: cut to 11 nights (−C$700), or accept 2–3 dorm nights, or shift to a cheaper country. Two evidence-driven changes to the suggested shape: (1) **Atelier Cézanne & Jas de Bouffan are closed 1 Nov–3 Jul** ([aixenprovencetourism.com](https://www.aixenprovencetourism.com/en/aix-en-provence-en/paul-cezanne/)); Bibémus quarries stay open. (2) **Sainte-Victoire summit stretch (GR9 Prieuré→Croix) is closed weekdays 1 Oct–27 Nov, weekends only** ([amisdesaintevictoire.asso.fr](https://amisdesaintevictoire.asso.fr/news-posts/nouvelles-des-sentiers/)) → Aix is scheduled on Sat/Sun. Cassis hotels are ~€165/night in Nov ([kayak.com](https://www.kayak.com/Cassis-Hotels-Le-Clos-Des-Aromes.2226828.ksp)), so Cassis becomes a day trip from Marseille.

## 4. Day by day
Dairy key: **DF** = naturally dairy-free. Provençal cooking is olive-oil based; aïoli (garlic-oil emulsion) is DF; socca, pissaladière, pan bagnat, bouillabaisse, daube, tapenade, ratatouille all DF; gratins, tarte tropézienne, pistou (often has cheese), tielle no. Ask "sans produits laitiers / sans crème".

- **Sat 31 Oct — YYZ→FRA.** LH471 18:20.
- **Sun 1 Nov — Nice (Toussaint holiday; first-Sunday free municipal museums).** Land ~11:40, Tram 2 to Vieux Nice, check-in. 15:00 Walkative free tour (2.5 h, 4.7/5) ([guruwalk](https://www.guruwalk.com/nice/tag/old)). Food: **Chez Pipo** socca + pissaladière (~€10, DF); dinner **Lou Pilha Leva** or **La Merenda** (walk-up, cash; daube ~€20, DF). Fallback: jet-lag nap, Promenade.
- **Mon 2 Nov — Matisse + Chagall (both closed Tue).** Bus 15/17 to Cimiez: Musée Matisse €10, 10–17 ([nissactu](https://nissactu.fr/musee-matisse-nice/)); Chagall €8 (€10 in exhibition), 10–12/14–17 ([agendaculturel](https://06.agendaculturel.fr/musee-national-marc-chagall)). Cours Saleya market (Tue–Sun mornings; Mon = antiques). Dinner **Bistrot d'Antoine** (book, ~€30). Rain-proof day.
- **Tue 3 Nov — Hike 1: Coastal path Nice→Villefranche + Nietzsche path to Èze.** 5.3 km from port around Cap de Nice/Mont Boron to Villefranche (1.5–2 h; closed if seas rough) ([niceandbeyond](https://niceandbeyond.com/walk-from-nice-to-villefranche-sur-mer/)); TER Villefranche→Èze-bord-de-mer (€3.50 est.), **Chemin de Nietzsche** 2.1 km / 350 m up / 1.5 h, unmarked, grit+steps, all-season ([explorenicecotedazur](https://www.explorenicecotedazur.com/en/itinerary/chemin-de-nietzsche/)); Jardin Exotique €7.50 (est.); bus 82/112 back to Nice (Lignes d'Azur €1.70). ## 8. Risks
1. **Trail closures/weather:** Sainte-Victoire summit closed weekdays to 27 Nov (plan locked to Sat 7 Nov; rain fallback = Bimont dam loop); En-Vau dangerous when wet; coastal path closes in swell. Mitigation: flexible order within Aix/Marseille days, Nov mistral days → museums.
2. **Seasonal closures:** Atelier Cézanne/Jas de Bouffan shut; Pont du Gard museum shut Nov–Feb; Chagall/Matisse/MuCEM closed Tue; Fondation Van Gogh/Granet closed Mon; Armistice 11 Nov. Itinerary already sequenced around these.
3. **Strikes:** SNCF/RATP autumn strikes are common; TER most affected. Book flexible TGV fares, keep OUIGO-to-CDG alternatives (TGV INOUI Sat evening + CDG hotel as backup).
4. **Pickpockets/safety:** Marseille Saint-Charles, Noailles, night buses; Nice tram 2 and Cours Saleya. Cross-body bag, no phone-in-hand at stations, avoid Marseille northern districts at night; solo-female reviews rate Vieux-Port/Panier fine by day.
5. **Entry (EES/ETIAS):** EES biometric registration is live since 10 Apr 2026 — allow 60+ min at FRA/CDG arrival; ETIAS slated for Q4 2026 with a 6-month no-requirement transition ([fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/european-union-european-travel-information-and-authorisation-system-etias-launch-delayed.html)) — check travel.gc.ca 2 weeks before; passport valid ≥3 months past return.
