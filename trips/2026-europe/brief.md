# Trip brief — Western Europe, solo, two weeks, Nov–Dec 2026

Status: FROZEN 2026-09-10 (destination open — decided among `plans/*.md`). Supersedes `trips/2026-latam` (rejected: safety and Oaxaca timing/cost).

## Who
- Chitra, solo. English; basic Spanish; no German/French/Italian beyond phrases.

## When
- Two weeks + weekends: Saturday out → Sunday back (14–15 nights, 10 PTO days).
- Windows researched: **Germany SW 21 Nov – 6 Dec** (Christmas markets in week 2, chosen deliberately); **Provence + Riviera 31 Oct – 15 Nov**; **Piedmont + Liguria 31 Oct – 15 Nov** (Alba truffle fair).

## What THIS trip is for
- Culture, history, food first; day hikes (4–6 h, moderate); meet people.
- Own room every night, high quality (no insects, no bad plumbing, hot water, wifi), in social hostels with privates, boutique hostels, B&Bs/pensions with a host. **No Airbnb.**
- Trains, not cars. Safe, organized, uncrowded.
- Spend less on lodging, more on guides, classes, hikes, food.

## Constraints
- Budget: C$2,500 soft target; **C$3,000 ceiling** for the maximized version.
- Dietary: mildly lactose-intolerant (matters most in Germany and Piedmont).

## Findings that changed the picture (see plans for sources)
1. **The ceiling doesn't hold.** With a private room every night, all three lean plans land at ≈ C$4,300–4,500 with contingency; best versions ≈ C$6,200–6,700. Lodging is the whole gap: clean private rooms in November run €55–75 in hostels and €100–150 in B&Bs, and there are 14 of them. Realistic floors: Germany ≈ C$3,400 at 10 nights; Italy ≈ C$3,900; France ≈ C$4,000. The outline estimates given before research (C$2,600–3,300) were wrong on lodging and flights — this brief corrects them.
2. **Flights**: only Frankfurt has a reliable nonstop (AC840). Nice has none in November (via Frankfurt out, Paris CDG home). Milan's Air Canada nonstop appears inactive in the window (via Montréal on one ticket) — verify on the live pass.
3. **Seasonal closures that shape the itineraries**: Schauinslandbahn closed 16 Nov–4 Dec (Freiburg hikes rerouted); the right-Rhine rail line closed to 11 Dec (one ICE leg needed Köln→Heidelberg); Atelier Cézanne closed all November (Bibémus quarries instead); Sainte-Victoire main trail weekends-only to 27 Nov; Cinque Terre trails free from 2 Nov but Corniglia–Manarola closed by landslide; Portofino's Passo del Bacio closed through 2026.
4. **Lodging screen rejected**: Alex 30/Hostel Alex Stuttgart (bed bugs), Pop/HO36 Avignon (bed bugs), Vertigo Marseille and Auberge Bagatelle (cleanliness), Hotel Langhe Alba (drains), Station Hostel and Hostel Köln (noise/cleanliness), Ostello Bello Genova (mould, ants, station alley).
5. Entry: EES biometrics live since April 2026 (allow time at first entry); ETIAS still slated for late 2026 with a grace period — recheck in October.
